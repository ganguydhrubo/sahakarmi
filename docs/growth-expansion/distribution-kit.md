**Description (about 100 words):**
Sahkarmi is a free, Apache-2.0 desktop app for Windows 10 and 11 that runs a team of AI coworkers on
your own computer. Each coworker has its own workspace folder, its own browser profile with
persistent sign-ins, memory and a task queue. Coworkers browse and act on websites, work with your
files and local knowledge bases, and, with the "Control this PC" permission, use Windows desktop
apps through UI Automation. They run on free built-in models or on Claude, ChatGPT (Codex), Gemini
and other providers you already use. Posting, sending, submitting and paying wait for your
approval, in the app or on Telegram. Interface in English, Hindi and Bengali.
