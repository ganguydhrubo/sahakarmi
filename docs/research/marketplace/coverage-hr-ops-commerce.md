# Marketplace coverage: HR, Operations, E-commerce, Legal, Education, Productivity

Date: 2026-10-25. Source of truth: `src/shared/agent-catalog/{hr,operations,ecommerce,legal,education,productivity}.ts`.

This note maps the roles we were asked to cover onto Marketplace coworkers, says what each one does on the computer, and gives one acceptance test per agent. Status "Operational" means the agent runs on Sahkarmi's built-in tools only: files in Shared, documents, spreadsheets, code and commands, web research, Sahkarmi's browser with the owner signing in through takeover, delegation and scheduled runs. No agent in these six categories needs an API key or outside connector, so none is marked `needs-setup`. Every service is used through the owner's own browser login or through exports saved in Shared.

Occupational references are used only to define scope: O*NET-SOC 2019 occupations, ISCO-08 unit groups, and India's National Classification of Occupations 2015 (NCO-2015). NCO-2015 follows the ISCO-08 structure at the 4-digit unit-group level, so the ISCO unit groups cited below also identify the NCO-2015 unit groups.

Shared approval rule: any action that leaves the computer (sending, posting, publishing, booking, paying, filing, submitting, uploading to a live store, contacting a person, accepting or creating invites) or that cannot be undone (deleting, moving originals, writing off stock) goes through `sahkarmi.request_approval`. The Approvals column lists the triggers for each agent.

## HR

**What this work is.** In a small Indian business, HR is usually handled by the owner, an office manager or one HR executive. The work covers hiring logistics, joining formalities (PAN, Aadhaar, bank, UAN, PF/ESI enrolment), letters and policies, staff training, and a monthly headcount, attendance and leave MIS. The computer work is mostly documents, sheets, job portals (Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed, apna) and calendars. References: O*NET 13-1071.00 Human Resources Specialists, 13-1151.00 Training and Development Specialists, 43-4161.00 Human Resources Assistants; ISCO-08 / NCO-2015 2423 Personnel and careers professionals, 2424 Training and staff development professionals, 4416 Personnel clerks. India context: the four Labour Codes took effect on 21 Nov 2025 and require appointment letters for all workers. State rules are still being notified, so HR Documentation cites a source for every statutory figure and sends policies to a professional for review.

## Legal (support only)

**What this work is.** Small businesses rarely have in-house counsel. The legal support work they need is reading and comparing contracts, tracking renewal and notice dates, keeping registrations and licences findable, drafting customer-facing and internal policies, keeping a statutory compliance calendar, and researching what rules apply. All of it is document, sheet and official-portal work. References: O*NET 23-2011.00 Paralegals and Legal Assistants, 13-1041.00 Compliance Officers, 43-6012.00 Legal Secretaries and Administrative Assistants; ISCO-08 / NCO-2015 3411 Legal and related associate professionals, 3342 Legal secretaries. Every legal agent carries the same limits line: not a lawyer, no legal advice, flag for a qualified advocate. India context: the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 is operational through the DPDP Rules, 2025, notified on 13-14 Nov 2025. The Data Protection Board is constituted immediately, consent-manager provisions apply after 12 months, and most data-fiduciary obligations apply after 18 months (reported as around May 2027 by law-firm summaries; the agent confirms dates against official notifications).
