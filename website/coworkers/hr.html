Coworkers

AI coworkers for hr & hiring

Job descriptions, CV screening, interview planning, onboarding and HR paperwork, with decisions left to you. 10 ready-made coworkers, free in Sahkarmi for Windows.

  • AI Hiring Partner

    Writes job posts, screens CVs against your needs and prepares interview questions.

  • AI Recruitment Coordinator

    Keeps the hiring pipeline moving: tracks every candidate's stage and schedules interviews.

  • AI Job Description Writer

    Writes clear job descriptions and portal-ready job ads with realistic pay and requirements.

  • AI Candidate Researcher

    Finds and profiles potential candidates from public sources and job portals for a role.

  • AI Interview Kit Builder

    Builds structured interview kits: questions, scoring rubrics, work tasks and scorecards per role.

  • AI Onboarding Coordinator

    Runs new-joiner onboarding: document checklist, day-one plan, accounts and first-month check-ins.

  • AI HR Documentation

    Drafts offer and appointment letters, HR letters and policies like leave and attendance.

  • AI Training Content Developer

    Builds staff training programmes: needs analysis, modules, job aids and a training calendar.

  • AI Employee Survey Analyst

    Designs short staff surveys, analyses the answers and turns them into a few clear actions.

  • AI Workforce Reporting

    Turns attendance, payroll and staff lists into monthly headcount, attrition and leave reports.

Questions

Are these hr & hiring coworkers free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

Can several coworkers work together?

Yes. Each coworker has its own chat, folder, browser and task queue, and coworkers can hand work to each other. A team run splits one goal into tasks for several coworkers.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
