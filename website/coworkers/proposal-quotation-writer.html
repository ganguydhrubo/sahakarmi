AI Proposal & Quotation Writer

Turns a customer requirement into a priced quotation or proposal on your letterhead, with GST and terms.

Role: Quotations and sales proposals · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Proposal & Quotation Writer is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

quotation · proforma · sales proposal · estimate · price quote · commercial offer · techno-commercial proposal · BOQ pricing · RFQ response

What you give it

Needs

  • The customer's requirement (message, RFQ or BOQ).
  • Price list or costing.

Helpful

  • Letterhead or past quotations.
  • Standard terms (validity, payment, delivery, warranty).
  • Margin floor.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Restate the requirement line by line and note anything ambiguous to confirm.
  2. Price each line from the price list or costing, apply GST by HSN/SAC and show freight and installation separately.
  3. Write the quotation or proposal: scope, line items, totals in figures and words, validity, payment and delivery terms.
  4. Check margin against the floor and flag lines priced below it.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A quotation or proposal PDF and editable copy.
  • A costing sheet with margin per line.
  • A list of points to confirm with the customer.

Everything is saved in Shared/Sales/Quotations on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every requirement line is priced or listed as excluded.
  • Totals and GST recompute correctly.
  • Amount in words matches the figure.

Done when: the quotation and costing are saved and every open point is listed.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending the quotation to the customer.
  • Quoting below the margin floor.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Proposal & Quotation Writer” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Proposal & Quotation Writer free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Proposal & Quotation Writer need from me?

The customer's requirement (message, RFQ or BOQ) and price list or costing. It also helps to have letterhead or past quotations; standard terms (validity, payment, delivery, warranty) and margin floor.

Does the AI Proposal & Quotation Writer act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending the quotation to the customer and quoting below the margin floor. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Proposal & Quotation Writer keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Sales/Quotations. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
