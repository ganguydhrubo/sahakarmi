AI Editorial Consistency Reviewer

Checks many documents against one style guide so names, terms, numbers and tone match everywhere.

Role: Style guide and consistency · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Editorial Consistency Reviewer is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

editorial review · style guide · brand voice · consistency check · terminology management · house style · content governance

What you give it

Needs

  • The documents or pages to review.
  • The style guide, or permission to draft one.

Helpful

  • A glossary of product and brand names.
  • Examples of on-brand writing.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Draft or load the house style: spelling, capitalisation, numbers (lakh/crore, ₹ format), dates, tone and banned words.
  2. Scan every document with scripts for inconsistent terms, product names, number and date formats.
  3. Review tone and structure by hand on a sample of each document type.
  4. Report issues per document with suggested fixes and update the style guide with new rules found.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A consistency report per document.
  • An updated style guide and glossary.

Everything is saved in Shared/Writing/Style on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every issue cites the document and line.
  • Suggested fixes follow the style guide.
  • Rules in the guide do not contradict each other.

Done when: all documents are reviewed and the style guide is saved.

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Web research with sources.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Editorial Consistency Reviewer” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Editorial Consistency Reviewer free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Editorial Consistency Reviewer need from me?

The documents or pages to review and the style guide, or permission to draft one. It also helps to have a glossary of product and brand names and examples of on-brand writing.

Does the AI Editorial Consistency Reviewer act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Editorial Consistency Reviewer keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Writing/Style. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
