AI Document & Data Comparer

Compares versions of reports, price lists, spec sheets and spreadsheets and lists every change that matters.

Role: Comparing reports, price lists and specs · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put the two (or more) files to compare (PDF, Word, Excel or CSV), and what kind of changes matter most in Shared/Research/Comparisons, then ask in plain words for a change workbook or redline document and a change summary with section or row references. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Document & Data Comparer covers

document comparison · version comparison · diff · price list comparison · spec sheet comparison · report version comparison · spreadsheet comparison · dataset diff

What the AI Document & Data Comparer needs from you

Needs

  • The two (or more) files to compare (PDF, Word, Excel or CSV).
  • What kind of changes matter most.

Helpful

  • The key column for row matching (item code, SKU, HSN).
  • Sections to focus on.

How the AI Document & Data Comparer works

  1. Extract text and tables from both versions (OCR scans if needed) and align them by section or key column.
  2. For tables, compare row by row: added, removed and changed rows with old and new values and % change.
  3. For text, produce a redline of additions, deletions and moves, ignoring formatting noise.
  4. Summarise the changes that matter (prices, quantities, specs, dates, figures).

What you get from the AI Document & Data Comparer

  • A change workbook or redline document.
  • A change summary with section or row references.

Everything is saved in Shared/Research/Comparisons on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every summary item points to its place in both versions.
  • Row counts reconcile: unchanged + changed + added + removed.
  • Numbers in the summary match both files.

Done when: the comparison and summary are saved.

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).

Limits

For contracts and policies, use the legal document-comparison coworker.

Add the AI Document & Data Comparer

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Document & Data Comparer” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Document & Data Comparer free?

Yes. The AI Document & Data Comparer comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Document & Data Comparer need from me?

The two (or more) files to compare (PDF, Word, Excel or CSV) and what kind of changes matter most. It also helps to have the key column for row matching (item code, SKU, HSN) and sections to focus on.

Does the AI Document & Data Comparer act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Document & Data Comparer keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Research/Comparisons. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
