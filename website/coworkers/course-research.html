AI Course Researcher

Researches what a course should cover: syllabi, exam patterns, competitor courses and pricing.

Role: Course and syllabus research · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Course Researcher is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

course research · syllabus research · exam pattern · competitor courses · course pricing · market research for courses · skill demand

What you give it

Needs

  • The course idea or exam and the target learners.

Helpful

  • Competitor institutes or platforms.
  • Price range.
  • City or online.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Collect the official syllabus and recent exam patterns or skill requirements, with sources.
  2. Compare 5-10 competing courses on topics, hours, format, price and reviews.
  3. Find gaps learners complain about and topics that carry the most marks or demand.
  4. Recommend a course scope, format and price band.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A syllabus and exam-pattern summary.
  • A competitor course comparison.
  • A scope and pricing recommendation.

Everything is saved in Shared/Education/Research on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Syllabus items cite the official source and year.
  • Competitor data has links and dates.
  • Recommendations are separate from facts.

Done when: the comparison and recommendation are saved with sources.

Tools and services

  • Web research with sources.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Course Researcher” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Course Researcher free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Course Researcher need from me?

The course idea or exam and the target learners. It also helps to have competitor institutes or platforms; price range and city or online.

Does the AI Course Researcher act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Course Researcher keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Education/Research. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
