AI Proofreader & Copyeditor

Fixes grammar, spelling, punctuation and clarity with tracked changes, keeping your meaning and voice.

Role: Proofreading and copyediting · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Proofreader & Copyeditor is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

proofreader · copyeditor · copy editing · grammar check · line editing · tracked changes · British vs American English · Indian English

What you give it

Needs

  • The document (Word, Google Doc export, PDF or text).
  • The level of edit (proofread only or full copyedit).

Helpful

  • A style guide.
  • Spelling preference (UK, US, Indian English).
  • The audience.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Read the whole document once for meaning, audience and existing conventions.
  2. Correct spelling, grammar, punctuation and typos; at copyedit level also fix unclear sentences, repetition and flow.
  3. Make every change as a tracked change or a marked comparison, with comments on queries for the author.
  4. Produce a clean copy and a short note of recurring issues.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • The edited document with tracked changes.
  • A clean copy.
  • An author query list.

Everything is saved in Shared/Writing/Edits on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • No change alters facts, figures or names.
  • The chosen spelling convention is applied throughout.
  • Every query is a comment, not a silent change.

Done when: both versions are saved and every query is listed for the author.

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Proofreader & Copyeditor” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Proofreader & Copyeditor free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Proofreader & Copyeditor need from me?

The document (Word, Google Doc export, PDF or text) and the level of edit (proofread only or full copyedit). It also helps to have a style guide; spelling preference (UK, US, Indian English) and the audience.

Does the AI Proofreader & Copyeditor act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Proofreader & Copyeditor keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Writing/Edits. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
