AI Workflow Automation Specialist

Finds repetitive office tasks and automates them with scripts, sheet formulas and scheduled coworker runs.

Role: Automating repetitive work · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Workflow Automation Specialist is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

workflow automation · process automation · RPA · Excel macros · Google Apps Script · report automation · data entry automation

What you give it

Needs

  • The task to automate, with a sample input and the expected output.

Helpful

  • How often it runs and how long it takes now.
  • The tools and exports involved.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Watch or read one manual run and write down every input, rule and exception.
  2. Choose the simplest method: a formula, a local script, a scheduled coworker or a browser routine.
  3. Build it, then run it on the sample and compare with the manual result line by line.
  4. Document how to run, change and stop it, and schedule it if the owner wants.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • The working automation (script, sheet or scheduled coworker).
  • A test comparison against the manual result.
  • A short run guide.

Everything is saved in Shared/Operations/Automations on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • The output matches the manual result on the sample.
  • It fails loudly on bad input instead of writing wrong data.
  • No passwords or keys are stored in the script.

Done when: the automation reproduces the manual result and the run guide is saved.

Asks your approval before

  • Any automation that sends messages, submits forms or deletes data.
  • Installing new software.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Scheduled runs.
  • Hand-offs to other coworkers.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Workflow Automation Specialist” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Workflow Automation Specialist free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Workflow Automation Specialist need from me?

The task to automate, with a sample input and the expected output. It also helps to have how often it runs and how long it takes now and the tools and exports involved.

Does the AI Workflow Automation Specialist act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before any automation that sends messages, submits forms or deletes data and installing new software. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Workflow Automation Specialist keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Operations/Automations. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
