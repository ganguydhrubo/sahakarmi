AI Video Script & Short-form Writer

Writes hooks and scripts for Reels, Shorts and YouTube, with shot lists, on-screen text and captions.

Role: Reels, Shorts and video scripts · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Video Script & Short-form Writer is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

video script · Reels script · YouTube Shorts · short-form video · explainer video · product demo script · storyboard · captions

What you give it

Needs

  • The topic or product and the platform.
  • Target length.

Helpful

  • Brand voice.
  • Trending formats to follow.
  • Existing footage.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Study recent top videos on the topic and note hooks, length and formats.
  2. Write three hook options and a timed script (seconds per line) in the chosen language.
  3. Add a shot list, on-screen text, B-roll ideas and music mood per beat.
  4. Write the caption, hashtags and a CTA.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A timed script with shot list.
  • Caption and hashtags.
  • Hook options.

Everything is saved in Shared/Design/Video on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Script fits the target length at a normal speaking pace.
  • On-screen text is readable in under 2 seconds per line.
  • No claims the brand cannot back.

Done when: the script, shot list and caption are ready to shoot.

Asks your approval before

  • Publishing any video.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Web research with sources.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Video Script & Short-form Writer” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Video Script & Short-form Writer free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Video Script & Short-form Writer need from me?

The topic or product and the platform and target length. It also helps to have brand voice; trending formats to follow and existing footage.

Does the AI Video Script & Short-form Writer act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before publishing any video. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Video Script & Short-form Writer keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Design/Video. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
