Coworkers

AI coworkers for finance & accounts

Bookkeeping, payables, invoicing, reconciliation, GST paperwork and reports, prepared in files you check before anything is posted or paid. 12 ready-made coworkers, free in Sahkarmi for Windows.

  • AI GST Accountant

    Prepares GSTR-1/3B figures, checks invoices and reconciles purchases with 2B.

  • AI Payment Reminders

    Tracks unpaid invoices by age and drafts friendly reminders.

  • AI Bookkeeper

    Turns bills, receipts and bank statements into clean, categorised entries ready for Tally or Zoho Books.

  • AI Accounts Payable

    Processes supplier invoices, matches them to POs and receipts, and proposes what to pay and when.

  • AI Sales Invoicing

    Prepares GST-compliant sales invoices, credit notes and e-invoice / e-way bill data from orders.

  • AI Bank Reconciliation

    Matches bank, UPI and payment-gateway statements with the books and explains every difference.

  • AI MIS & P&L Reporter

    Builds the monthly MIS: P&L, balance sheet view, cash position and key ratios with plain-language notes.

  • AI Expense Manager

    Checks staff expense claims and petty cash against policy and prepares the reimbursement list.

  • AI Budget & Cash Forecaster

    Builds a yearly budget and a rolling 13-week cash forecast with clear assumptions.

  • AI Payroll Operations

    Prepares the monthly salary sheet with PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS working and payslips, without paying anyone.

  • AI Tax Document Organizer

    Collects and checks Form 16, 26AS, AIS and proofs into one tidy ITR folder for the owner's CA.

  • AI Procurement Documentation

    Compares supplier quotations, drafts purchase orders and keeps a clean purchase file for each buy.

Questions

Are these finance & accounts coworkers free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

Can several coworkers work together?

Yes. Each coworker has its own chat, folder, browser and task queue, and coworkers can hand work to each other. A team run splits one goal into tasks for several coworkers.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
