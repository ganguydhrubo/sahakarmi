AI Sales Invoicing

Prepares GST-compliant sales invoices, credit notes and e-invoice / e-way bill data from orders.

Role: Raising GST invoices · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Sales Invoicing is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

invoice preparation · billing executive · GST tax invoice · e-invoice · IRN · e-way bill · credit note · debit note · proforma invoice · accounts receivable

What you give it

Needs

  • Orders, delivery challans or a list of what to bill.
  • The seller's GSTIN, address and invoice series.

Helpful

  • Customer master with GSTINs and addresses.
  • HSN/SAC and rate list.
  • The invoice template or letterhead.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Check each order for buyer GSTIN, state code, place of supply, HSN/SAC and rate.
  2. Apply CGST+SGST or IGST correctly and number invoices in sequence within your series.
  3. Produce invoice PDFs in your format and a JSON or Excel file for e-invoice / e-way bill upload where turnover requires it.
  4. Prepare credit or debit notes for returns and price changes, linked to the original invoice.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • Invoice and credit-note PDFs.
  • An e-invoice / e-way bill upload file.
  • A sales register for the batch.

Everything is saved in Shared/Accounts/Invoices on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Invoice numbers are continuous with no gaps or repeats.
  • Tax type matches place of supply.
  • Register totals equal the sum of invoices.

Done when: every order is invoiced or flagged, the register is saved and upload files are ready.

Asks your approval before

  • Generating an IRN or e-way bill on the portal.
  • Sending invoices to customers.
  • Cancelling any invoice.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.

Services

  • GST e-invoice (IRP) and e-way bill portals: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; the owner signs in; generation needs approval.
  • Tally / Zoho Books: from exports you put in the Shared folder.

Limits

Follows the owner's GST settings; the owner or their CA confirms e-invoicing applicability.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Sales Invoicing” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Sales Invoicing free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Sales Invoicing need from me?

Orders, delivery challans or a list of what to bill and the seller's GSTIN, address and invoice series. It also helps to have customer master with GSTINs and addresses; HSN/SAC and rate list and the invoice template or letterhead.

Does the AI Sales Invoicing act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before generating an IRN or e-way bill on the portal; sending invoices to customers and cancelling any invoice. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Sales Invoicing keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Accounts/Invoices. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
