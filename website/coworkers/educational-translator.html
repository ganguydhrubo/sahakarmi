AI Educational Translator

Translates study material, tests and trainings into Indian languages with consistent subject terms.

Role: Learning material translation · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Educational Translator is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

educational translation · bilingual material · Hindi medium · regional language content · Marathi · Tamil · Telugu · glossary

What you give it

Needs

  • The material.
  • The target language and learners' level.

Helpful

  • A subject glossary or the state board's terms.
  • A native-speaker reviewer.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Build a subject glossary first, using the board's or commonly taught terms, and keep English terms in brackets where learners expect them.
  2. Translate for meaning at the learners' reading level, keeping formulas, numbers, diagram labels and layout.
  3. Keep question papers and answer keys aligned so option letters and marks still match.
  4. Back-translate key passages and list lines a native reviewer should check.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • The translated material in the original format.
  • A subject glossary.
  • A reviewer checklist.

Everything is saved in Shared/Education/Translations on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Formulas, numbers and option letters unchanged.
  • Glossary terms used consistently.
  • Nothing omitted.

Done when: the full translation, glossary and reviewer checklist are saved.

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.

Limits

Quality varies by language; a native speaker should review before printing.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Educational Translator” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Educational Translator free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Educational Translator need from me?

The material and the target language and learners' level. It also helps to have a subject glossary or the state board's terms and a native-speaker reviewer.

Does the AI Educational Translator act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Educational Translator keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Education/Translations. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
