AI Lead Researcher

Finds businesses that fit your ideal customer and builds a sourced prospect list with a first-message draft for each.

Role: Prospect lists (SDR) · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

You can walk the industrial area for a week, or start with a list of the forty units that really use your part. This coworker builds that list from public pages, with a link for each line, so you can check it before you call.

Example: a first task

“We sell mechanical seals to pump makers with 10 to 100 workers near Rajkot. Find 30, leave out the names in customers.xlsx, and write a short first message for the best ten.”

What comes back

  • A prospect sheet: name, city, size signal, public phone or email, fit score and source link.
  • A personal first message for the top leads, with one reason it fits them.
  • A note on which sources gave the best leads.

More you can ask

  • Find 30 pump makers near Rajkot, leave out my customers.
  • Write a three-line first message for the top ten.
  • Which source gave the best leads?

Good to know

  • It uses only business contact details that are publicly listed.
  • It sends no message and no connection request without your approval.
  • If it finds only 22 good ones, it says 22 and why.

What the AI Lead Researcher covers

SDR · sales development representative · lead generation · lead research · prospecting · B2B lead list · outbound research · IndiaMART buyers · Google Maps leads · LinkedIn prospecting

What the AI Lead Researcher needs from you

Needs

  • The ideal customer (industry, city or region, size, who decides).
  • What you sell and why it helps them.

Helpful

  • Existing customers to exclude.
  • How many leads are wanted.
  • LinkedIn or IndiaMART access.

How the AI Lead Researcher works

  1. Turn the ideal customer into search queries and sources (directories, maps, associations, trade-fair exhibitor lists).
  2. Collect each business: name, website, city, size signal, contact role and a public business phone or email, with the source URL.
  3. Score fit against the criteria and note one specific reason the business might need the offer.
  4. Draft a short, personal first message per top lead that mentions that reason.

What you get from the AI Lead Researcher

  • A prospect sheet with sources and fit scores.
  • First-message drafts for the top leads.
  • A note on where the best leads came from.

Everything is saved in Shared/Sales/Leads on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every lead has a working source URL.
  • No duplicates or existing customers.
  • Only business contact details that are publicly listed.

Done when: the requested number of fitting leads is in the sheet with sources, or the shortfall is explained.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending any message, email or connection request.
  • Buying or exporting data from a paid list.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Web research with sources.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Files in its folder and Shared.

Services

  • LinkedIn, IndiaMART, Google Maps, JustDial: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; reading public listings; no automated connection requests.

Questions

Is the AI Lead Researcher free?

Yes. The AI Lead Researcher comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Lead Researcher need from me?

The ideal customer (industry, city or region, size, who decides) and what you sell and why it helps them. It also helps to have existing customers to exclude; how many leads are wanted and LinkedIn or IndiaMART access.

Does the AI Lead Researcher act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending any message, email or connection request and buying or exporting data from a paid list. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Lead Researcher keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Sales/Leads. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
