AI Payment Reminders

Tracks unpaid invoices by age and drafts friendly reminders.

Role: Collections · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Asking for your own money is uncomfortable, so it waits. Ninety days later it is a bigger problem. This coworker sorts dues by age, writes a polite line for the new ones and a firmer one for the old ones, and keeps a note of who promised what.

Example: a first task

“The outstanding list from Tally is in Shared/Accounts/Receivables. Make the ageing by customer, and draft a reminder for everyone above 30 days. Use the UPI ID in bank-details.txt.”

What comes back

  • An ageing report: each customer in 0-30, 31-60, 61-90 and 90+ days, with totals that match the ledger.
  • One reminder per overdue customer with invoice numbers, amounts and your payment details.
  • A collections tracker with promised dates and the next reminder.

More you can ask

  • Who promised to pay this week and did not?
  • Write the reminder in Hindi, very polite, for Gupta Stores.
  • Every Monday, update the ageing and show me the five biggest dues.

Good to know

  • It copies payment details exactly as you gave them, so check the UPI ID once before the first run.
  • It asks before sending any reminder, and before offering any discount or settlement.
  • It can open WhatsApp Business or your email in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.

What the AI Payment Reminders covers

accounts receivable · collections · overdue invoices · payment follow-up · ageing report

What the AI Payment Reminders needs from you

Needs

  • A ledger or outstanding-invoices export.

Helpful

  • UPI or bank details to include.
  • Customer contact list.
  • Credit terms.

How the AI Payment Reminders works

  1. Build an ageing table per customer (0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ days) from the due dates.
  2. Prioritise by amount and age; note promised payment dates.
  3. Draft a reminder per customer with invoice numbers, amounts and your payment details; firmer tone for older dues.
  4. Track replies and promises, and schedule the next reminder.

What you get from the AI Payment Reminders

  • An ageing report.
  • Reminder drafts.
  • A collections tracker.

Everything is saved in Shared/Accounts/Receivables on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Totals match the ledger.
  • Each reminder lists the right invoices and amounts.
  • Payment details exactly as the owner gave them.

Done when: every overdue customer has a reminder draft and the tracker is saved.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending any reminder.
  • Offering settlement or waivers.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • Tally / accounting software: from exports you put in the Shared folder.
  • WhatsApp Business / email: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.

Questions

Is the AI Payment Reminders free?

Yes. The AI Payment Reminders comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Payment Reminders need from me?

A ledger or outstanding-invoices export. It also helps to have UPI or bank details to include; customer contact list and credit terms.

Does the AI Payment Reminders act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending any reminder and offering settlement or waivers. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Payment Reminders keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Accounts/Receivables. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
