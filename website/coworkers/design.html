Coworkers

AI coworkers for design & media

Design briefs, references, asset organising, presentations, video scripts and design checks around the creative work. 13 ready-made coworkers, free in Sahkarmi for Windows.

  • AI UI/UX Researcher

    Plans user interviews and usability tests, reviews competitor apps and turns findings into clear design priorities.

  • AI UI/UX Designer

    Designs screens as wireframes, specs and clickable HTML prototypes that developers can build from.

  • AI Website Content Designer

    Plans page structure and writes clear website copy (headlines, sections, CTAs, microcopy) in English, Hindi or Hinglish.

  • AI Graphic Design Brief Writer

    Writes precise briefs for posters, packaging, banners and social creatives, with sizes, copy and references.

  • AI Presentation Designer

    Turns notes and numbers into clear slide decks (PowerPoint, Google Slides or PDF) with one idea per slide.

  • AI Brand Identity Assistant

    Builds a simple brand kit: positioning, name ideas, voice, colours, typography and logo briefs.

  • AI Image Generation Workflow

    Writes prompts and generates, edits and sizes images for products, ads and posts, keeping a log of what was used.

  • AI Video Script & Short-form Writer

    Writes hooks and scripts for Reels, Shorts and YouTube, with shot lists, on-screen text and captions.

  • AI Video Production Planner

    Plans shoots and edits: schedule, locations, props, call sheets, edit decision lists and rough cuts with ffmpeg.

  • AI Podcast & Audio Producer

    Plans podcast episodes, transcribes recordings, cleans audio and writes show notes and clips.

  • AI Creative Research

    Finds creative references, trends and competitor campaigns and organises them into mood boards with sources.

  • AI Digital Asset Organizer

    Sorts, renames, de-duplicates and catalogues photos, videos and design files so the team can find them.

  • AI Design QA

    Compares built pages and creatives against the design spec and lists every mismatch with screenshots.

Questions

Are these design & media coworkers free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

Can several coworkers work together?

Yes. Each coworker has its own chat, folder, browser and task queue, and coworkers can hand work to each other. A team run splits one goal into tasks for several coworkers.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
