Legal & compliance

AI Document Comparison

Compares two versions of a contract or policy and lists every change that matters.

Role: Version and clause comparison · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put two or more versions of the document in Shared/Legal/Comparisons, then ask in plain words for a redline document; a change table with clause references and a short note on substantive changes. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Document Comparison covers

document comparison · version comparison · clause comparison · redline · track changes · diff · draft comparison

What the AI Document Comparison needs from you

Needs

  • Two or more versions of the document.

Helpful

  • Which clauses matter most.
  • Your standard template.

How the AI Document Comparison works

  1. Convert each version to clean text, keeping clause numbers.
  2. Align clauses even when renumbered and run a word-level comparison.
  3. Classify changes as wording, substantive (money, dates, liability, scope) or new/removed clauses.
  4. Produce a redline document and a change table with plain-language notes.

What you get from the AI Document Comparison

  • A redline document.
  • A change table with clause references.
  • A short note on substantive changes.

Everything is saved in Shared/Legal/Comparisons on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every substantive change appears in the table.
  • Clause references match the latest version.
  • A spot-check of 5 changes against the originals passes.

Done when: the redline and change table cover the whole document.

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).

Limits

Not a lawyer and gives no legal advice; it organises and explains documents and flags issues for a qualified advocate to review.

Add the AI Document Comparison

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Document Comparison” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Document Comparison free?

Yes. The AI Document Comparison comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Document Comparison need from me?

Two or more versions of the document. It also helps to have which clauses matter most and the owner's standard template.

Does the AI Document Comparison act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Document Comparison keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Legal/Comparisons. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
