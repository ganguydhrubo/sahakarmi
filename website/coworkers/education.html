AI coworkers for education & training

Lesson plans, course material, quizzes, tutoring and training content for teachers, trainers and learners. 8 ready-made coworkers, free in Sahkarmi for Windows.

  • AI Learning Content Developer

    Turns a syllabus or expertise into structured course modules, notes and practice work.

  • AI Course Researcher

    Researches what a course should cover: syllabi, exam patterns, competitor courses and pricing.

  • AI Lesson Plan Creator

    Writes period-by-period lesson plans with activities, timing and homework.

  • AI Quiz & Assessment Generator

    Creates quizzes, tests and question papers with answer keys and marking schemes.

  • AI Study Material Organizer

    Sorts notes, PDFs and recordings by subject and chapter and builds revision packs.

  • AI Training Presentation Builder

    Builds clear slide decks with speaker notes for classes, workshops and staff trainings.

  • AI Knowledge Base Assistant

    Answers staff questions from your own documents with the source quoted, and keeps the FAQ current.

  • AI Educational Translator

    Translates study material, tests and trainings into Indian languages with consistent subject terms.

Questions

Are these education & training coworkers free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

Can several coworkers work together?

Yes. Each coworker has its own chat, folder, browser and task queue, and coworkers can hand work to each other. A team run splits one goal into tasks for several coworkers.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
