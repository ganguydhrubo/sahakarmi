AI Form & Enquiry Processor

Collects enquiries from website forms and inboxes, removes spam and duplicates, logs them and drafts first replies.

Role: Website enquiries and leads · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put where enquiries arrive (form plugin, inbox, IndiaMART, JustDial) in Shared/Web/Enquiries, then ask in plain words for an updated enquiry tracker; reply drafts and a daily summary. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Form & Enquiry Processor covers

enquiry handling · website leads · contact form · lead capture · IndiaMART enquiries · JustDial leads · form submissions

What the AI Form & Enquiry Processor needs from you

Needs

  • Where enquiries arrive (form plugin, inbox, IndiaMART, JustDial).

Helpful

  • Reply templates and price list.
  • Who handles which enquiry type.

How the AI Form & Enquiry Processor works

  1. Collect new enquiries and log name, contact, need, source and time in the tracker.
  2. Mark spam and duplicates with a reason, and tag each real enquiry by type and urgency.
  3. Draft a first reply per enquiry from your templates and assign an owner.
  4. Flag enquiries with no reply after 24 hours.

What you get from the AI Form & Enquiry Processor

  • An updated enquiry tracker.
  • Reply drafts.
  • A daily summary.

Everything is saved in Shared/Web/Enquiries on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • No enquiry is lost between source and tracker.
  • Spam marks have a reason.
  • Replies use only your prices and terms.

Done when: every new enquiry is logged, tagged and has a draft reply or a reason.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending any reply.
  • Deleting enquiries.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • Form inbox, Gmail, IndiaMART, JustDial: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.

Add the AI Form & Enquiry Processor

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Form & Enquiry Processor” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Form & Enquiry Processor free?

Yes. The AI Form & Enquiry Processor comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Form & Enquiry Processor need from me?

Where enquiries arrive (form plugin, inbox, IndiaMART, JustDial). It also helps to have reply templates and price list and who handles which enquiry type.

Does the AI Form & Enquiry Processor act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending any reply and deleting enquiries. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Form & Enquiry Processor keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Web/Enquiries. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
