AI Knowledge Base Builder

Gathers your scattered documents, chats and notes into an organised, searchable knowledge base with FAQs.

Role: Internal knowledge base · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put the documents and notes to include (Shared folders, exports, chat exports), and who will use the knowledge base in Shared/Knowledge Base, then ask in plain words for the knowledge base pages and index; an FAQ set and a conflicts and outdated-info list. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Knowledge Base Builder covers

knowledge base · knowledge management · internal wiki · FAQ builder · documentation hub · Notion · company handbook · information architecture

What the AI Knowledge Base Builder needs from you

Needs

  • The documents and notes to include (Shared folders, exports, chat exports).
  • Who will use the knowledge base.

Helpful

  • Preferred tool (Markdown in Shared, Notion, Google Sites).
  • Existing FAQs.
  • Topics to cover first.

How the AI Knowledge Base Builder works

  1. Inventory the sources and plan a structure (topics, sub-topics, page types).
  2. Write one page per topic from the sources, citing the source file, and merge duplicates.
  3. Build FAQ pages from repeated questions, and an index with search keywords.
  4. Flag outdated or conflicting information for the owner and set a review date per page.

What you get from the AI Knowledge Base Builder

  • The knowledge base pages and index.
  • An FAQ set.
  • A conflicts and outdated-info list.

Everything is saved in Shared/Knowledge Base on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every page cites its source files.
  • Index links all work.
  • No private personal data is copied into shared pages.

Done when: the structure, pages and FAQs are saved and conflicts are listed.

Asks your approval before

  • Publishing the knowledge base outside Shared.
  • Deleting old source files.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Hand-offs to other coworkers.

Services

  • Notion, Google Sites: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; optional; Markdown in Shared by default.

Add the AI Knowledge Base Builder

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Knowledge Base Builder” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Knowledge Base Builder free?

Yes. The AI Knowledge Base Builder comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Knowledge Base Builder need from me?

The documents and notes to include (Shared folders, exports, chat exports) and who will use the knowledge base. It also helps to have preferred tool (Markdown in Shared, Notion, Google Sites); existing FAQs and topics to cover first.

Does the AI Knowledge Base Builder act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before publishing the knowledge base outside Shared and deleting old source files. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Knowledge Base Builder keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Knowledge Base. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
