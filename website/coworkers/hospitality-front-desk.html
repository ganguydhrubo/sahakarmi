AI Hotel & Homestay Desk

Keeps OTA listings accurate, tracks bookings across channels, prepares guest records and drafts review replies.

Role: OTA listings, bookings and reviews · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Hotel & Homestay Desk is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

hotel front office · homestay manager · OTA listings · MakeMyTrip · Booking.com · Airbnb · Goibibo · review replies · booking reconciliation · revenue management

What you give it

Needs

  • The property's rooms, rates and policies.
  • Booking exports or OTA extranet access.

Helpful

  • Competitor properties.
  • Seasonal calendar.
  • Google Business Profile access.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Check each OTA listing for photos, amenities, policies and rates against the property's master sheet.
  2. Combine bookings from all channels into one calendar and flag overbookings.
  3. Prepare arrival lists and guest record sheets for check-in.
  4. Draft polite, specific replies to new reviews and list recurring complaints.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A channel booking calendar.
  • A listing fix list.
  • Review reply drafts and a complaint summary.

Everything is saved in Shared/Hospitality on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • No room is booked twice on the same night.
  • Rates and policies match the master sheet.
  • Replies never share guest details.

Done when: the booking calendar, listing fixes and review drafts are saved.

Asks your approval before

  • Changing rates or availability on any OTA.
  • Posting review replies.
  • Cancelling bookings or issuing refunds.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Web research with sources.
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • MakeMyTrip/Goibibo, Booking.com, Airbnb extranets: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.
  • Google Business Profile: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Hotel & Homestay Desk” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Hotel & Homestay Desk free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Hotel & Homestay Desk need from me?

The property's rooms, rates and policies and booking exports or OTA extranet access. It also helps to have competitor properties; seasonal calendar and Google Business Profile access.

Does the AI Hotel & Homestay Desk act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before changing rates or availability on any OTA; posting review replies and cancelling bookings or issuing refunds. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Hotel & Homestay Desk keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Hospitality. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
