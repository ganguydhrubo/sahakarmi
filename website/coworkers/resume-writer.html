AI Resume & Cover Letter Writer

Rewrites a resume and cover letter for a specific job so it passes ATS screening and reads well to a recruiter.

Role: Resumes and job applications · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Resume & Cover Letter Writer is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

resume writer · CV writing · cover letter · LinkedIn profile · ATS resume · Naukri profile · job application · biodata

What you give it

Needs

  • The current resume or career details.
  • The target job description or role.

Helpful

  • Achievements with numbers.
  • LinkedIn or Naukri profile.
  • Preferred format.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Pull the must-have skills and keywords from the job description.
  2. Rewrite the summary and experience as achievement bullets (action, what, result), using the person's real facts.
  3. Format a clean, ATS-friendly resume (simple layout, standard headings) in Word and PDF.
  4. Write a short cover letter for the job and suggest LinkedIn or Naukri headline updates.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • The tailored resume in Word and PDF.
  • A cover letter.
  • Profile headline suggestions.

Everything is saved in Shared/Writing/Resumes on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Nothing is invented: every claim comes from the person's details.
  • Key job-description skills appear where truthful.
  • Contact details are correct.

Done when: the resume, cover letter and suggestions are saved.

Asks your approval before

  • Applying to any job or sending the application.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Web research with sources.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Resume & Cover Letter Writer” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Resume & Cover Letter Writer free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Resume & Cover Letter Writer need from me?

The current resume or career details and the target job description or role. It also helps to have achievements with numbers; LinkedIn or Naukri profile and preferred format.

Does the AI Resume & Cover Letter Writer act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before applying to any job or sending the application. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Resume & Cover Letter Writer keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Writing/Resumes. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
