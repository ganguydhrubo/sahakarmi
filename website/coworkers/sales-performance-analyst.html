AI Sales Performance Analyst

Shows what sells, on which channel, at what real profit, and checks marketplace payouts.

Role: Channel sales and settlements · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put sales and settlement reports from each channel for the period in Shared/Ecommerce/Analysis, then ask in plain words for a sales and margin workbook; a settlement mismatch list and a one-page summary with actions. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Sales Performance Analyst covers

sales analysis · ecommerce analytics · channel P&L · settlement reconciliation · payout reconciliation · SKU profitability · sales MIS

What the AI Sales Performance Analyst needs from you

Needs

  • Sales and settlement reports from each channel for the period.

Helpful

  • Cost prices.
  • Ad spend reports.
  • The previous period's analysis.

How the AI Sales Performance Analyst works

  1. Combine sales, returns, fees and ad spend per SKU and channel for the period.
  2. Compute contribution margin per SKU and channel and rank winners and losers.
  3. Reconcile each payout with orders: missing payments, wrong fees, unpaid returns.
  4. Write a short summary with 3-5 actions (push, fix, reprice, drop).

What you get from the AI Sales Performance Analyst

  • A sales and margin workbook.
  • A settlement mismatch list.
  • A one-page summary with actions.

Everything is saved in Shared/Ecommerce/Analysis on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Totals match the channel reports.
  • Every mismatch has an order ID and amount.
  • Margin formulas recalculate.

Done when: the period's margin view and mismatch list are saved.

Asks your approval before

  • Raising tickets or disputes with a marketplace.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • Amazon, Flipkart, Meesho, Shopify reports: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; report downloads only.

Add the AI Sales Performance Analyst

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Sales Performance Analyst” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Sales Performance Analyst free?

Yes. The AI Sales Performance Analyst comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Sales Performance Analyst need from me?

Sales and settlement reports from each channel for the period. It also helps to have cost prices; ad spend reports and the previous period's analysis.

Does the AI Sales Performance Analyst act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before raising tickets or disputes with a marketplace. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Sales Performance Analyst keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Ecommerce/Analysis. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
