AI Customer Feedback Analyst

Reads reviews, surveys and complaints, groups them into themes and tells you what to fix first.

Role: Reviews and feedback analysis · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Customer Feedback Analyst is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

customer feedback analysis · voice of customer · review analysis · Google reviews · NPS · CSAT · complaint analysis · sentiment analysis

What you give it

Needs

  • Feedback exports (Google reviews, marketplace reviews, survey results, complaint logs).

Helpful

  • Product or branch list.
  • The previous analysis to compare.
  • Google Business Profile access.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Collect and de-duplicate feedback, keeping date, source, rating and product or branch.
  2. Tag each item with themes (quality, delivery, price, staff, packaging) and sentiment, including Hindi and Hinglish text.
  3. Count themes over time and by product or branch, and pick representative quotes.
  4. Rank issues by frequency and impact on ratings, and suggest fixes.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A tagged feedback sheet.
  • A themes report with quotes and trends.
  • A prioritised fix list.

Everything is saved in Shared/Sales/Feedback on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every item has a theme and sentiment.
  • Theme counts add up to the total.
  • Quotes are verbatim and traceable to their source.

Done when: all feedback is tagged and the report ranks the top issues with evidence.

Asks your approval before

  • Replying publicly to any review.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.

Services

  • Google Business Profile, Amazon/Flipkart reviews: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; reading only.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Customer Feedback Analyst” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Customer Feedback Analyst free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Customer Feedback Analyst need from me?

Feedback exports (Google reviews, marketplace reviews, survey results, complaint logs). It also helps to have product or branch list; the previous analysis to compare and Google Business Profile access.

Does the AI Customer Feedback Analyst act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before replying publicly to any review. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Customer Feedback Analyst keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Sales/Feedback. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
