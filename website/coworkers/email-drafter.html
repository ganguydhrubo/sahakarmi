AI Email Drafter

Drafts clear, polite business emails and letters for any situation, from tricky replies to formal notices.

Role: Business emails and letters · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put what the email must achieve and who it is to, and the thread or background in Shared/Writing/Emails, then ask in plain words for the email or letter draft and an alternative version where useful. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Email Drafter covers

email writing · business correspondence · formal letter · reply drafting · complaint letter · apology email · request letter · cover email · inbox management

What the AI Email Drafter needs from you

Needs

  • What the email must achieve and who it is to.
  • The thread or background.

Helpful

  • Tone (formal, warm, firm).
  • Language (English, Hindi, Bengali).
  • Facts and dates to include.
  • Email account access.

How the AI Email Drafter works

  1. Read the thread and note what the other person actually asked or needs.
  2. Draft a subject line and email with the ask or answer in the first two lines.
  3. Offer a shorter or firmer variant when the situation is sensitive.
  4. Save the draft in the mail account or in Shared, ready to send.

What you get from the AI Email Drafter

  • The email or letter draft.
  • An alternative version where useful.

Everything is saved in Shared/Writing/Emails on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Names, dates, amounts and attachments mentioned are correct.
  • It answers every question in the thread.
  • Tone matches the request.

Done when: the draft is saved and ready for the owner to send.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending any email or letter.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.

Services

  • Gmail / Outlook: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; drafts saved; sending needs approval.

Add the AI Email Drafter

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Email Drafter” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Email Drafter free?

Yes. The AI Email Drafter comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Email Drafter need from me?

What the email must achieve and who it is to and the thread or background. It also helps to have tone (formal, warm, firm); language (English, Hindi, Bengali); facts and dates to include and email account access.

Does the AI Email Drafter act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending any email or letter. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Email Drafter keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Writing/Emails. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
