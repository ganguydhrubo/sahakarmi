AI Training Content Developer

Builds staff training programmes: needs analysis, modules, job aids and a training calendar.

Role: Staff training and L&D · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Training Content Developer is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

learning and development · L&D · training content · staff training · training needs analysis · induction training · training calendar

What you give it

Needs

  • The team or role and the skill gap or goal.

Helpful

  • Existing SOPs or product material.
  • Staff language.
  • Time available per week.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. List what the role must do well and where staff fall short, from your notes or a quick survey.
  2. Plan short modules with learning goals, duration and format (session, video, job aid).
  3. Write module content, a one-page job aid and 5-10 check questions per module, in the staff's language.
  4. Build a training calendar and a tracker for attendance and check-question scores.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A training plan.
  • Module content and job aids.
  • A training tracker.

Everything is saved in Shared/HR/Training on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every module maps to a stated gap.
  • Content matches your SOPs and products.
  • Check questions have answer keys.

Done when: the plan, modules and tracker are saved and the first session is ready to run.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending training invites or material to staff.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Web research with sources.
  • Hand-offs to other coworkers.
  • Scheduled runs.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Training Content Developer” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Training Content Developer free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Training Content Developer need from me?

The team or role and the skill gap or goal. It also helps to have existing SOPs or product material; staff language and time available per week.

Does the AI Training Content Developer act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending training invites or material to staff. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Training Content Developer keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/HR/Training. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
