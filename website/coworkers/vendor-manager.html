AI Vendor Manager

Keeps the vendor list, tracks delivery and quality, and prepares vendor reviews and renewals.

Role: Suppliers and vendor performance · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Vendor Manager is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

vendor management · supplier management · vendor master · vendor onboarding · vendor scorecard · SLA tracking · supplier review

What you give it

Needs

  • The vendor list.
  • Purchase orders, GRNs or delivery records.

Helpful

  • Vendor contracts and rates.
  • Quality complaints.
  • GSTIN and MSME status of vendors.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Build the vendor master: GSTIN, PAN, MSME/Udyam number, bank, contact, terms and contract end date.
  2. Score each vendor on on-time delivery, quality rejects and price vs agreed rate.
  3. Flag contracts ending in 60 days, inactive GSTINs and MSME vendors near the 45-day payment limit.
  4. Draft review notes or renewal asks per vendor.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A vendor master.
  • A vendor scorecard.
  • Renewal and issue drafts.

Everything is saved in Shared/Operations/Vendors on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • GSTIN formats are valid and status checks are dated.
  • Scores trace to POs or GRNs.
  • Bank details are never changed without owner confirmation.

Done when: the master and scorecard are saved and every flag has a next step.

Asks your approval before

  • Contacting any vendor.
  • Changing vendor bank details.
  • Renewing or ending a contract.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • Tally / purchase software: from exports you put in the Shared folder.
  • GST portal (search taxpayer): in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; verifying vendor GSTIN status.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Vendor Manager” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Vendor Manager free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Vendor Manager need from me?

The vendor list and purchase orders, GRNs or delivery records. It also helps to have vendor contracts and rates; quality complaints and GSTIN and MSME status of vendors.

Does the AI Vendor Manager act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before contacting any vendor; changing vendor bank details and renewing or ending a contract. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Vendor Manager keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Operations/Vendors. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
