AI Lead Qualifier

Sorts incoming enquiries into hot, warm and cold with a reason, and drafts the right next reply for each.

Role: Inbound lead qualification · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Lead Qualifier is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

lead qualification · inbound leads · BANT · lead scoring · enquiry handling · IndiaMART enquiries · website form leads · WhatsApp enquiries · pre-sales

What you give it

Needs

  • The enquiry list (IndiaMART, website form, WhatsApp or email export).
  • What makes a good lead (budget, quantity, location, timeline).

Helpful

  • Price list and minimum order.
  • Service areas.
  • CRM access.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Read each enquiry and pull out product, quantity, location, timeline and any budget signal.
  2. Score it against your criteria and label it hot, warm, cold or spam with a one-line reason.
  3. Draft the next reply: qualifying questions for warm leads, a quotation request for hot ones, a polite close for poor fits.
  4. Update the lead sheet or CRM stage and hand hot leads to the sales follow-up coworker.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A scored enquiry sheet.
  • Reply drafts per lead.
  • A hot-lead list for today.

Everything is saved in Shared/Sales/Leads on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every enquiry has a label and reason.
  • Criteria are applied the same way to every lead.
  • No reply promises price or delivery outside the price list.

Done when: every new enquiry is scored with a drafted next step and hot leads are handed over.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending replies to leads.
  • Changing lead stages in the live CRM.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • IndiaMART Lead Manager, CRM (Zoho, HubSpot): in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Lead Qualifier” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Lead Qualifier free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Lead Qualifier need from me?

The enquiry list (IndiaMART, website form, WhatsApp or email export) and what makes a good lead (budget, quantity, location, timeline). It also helps to have price list and minimum order; service areas and CRM access.

Does the AI Lead Qualifier act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending replies to leads and changing lead stages in the live CRM. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Lead Qualifier keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Sales/Leads. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
