AI Expense Manager

Checks staff expense claims and petty cash against policy and prepares the reimbursement list.

Role: Expense claims and petty cash · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Expense Manager is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

expense management · expense claims · reimbursements · petty cash · travel expenses · fuel bills · conveyance · corporate card reconciliation

What you give it

Needs

  • Claims with bills or photos of receipts.
  • The expense policy (limits per category, per day or per trip).

Helpful

  • Staff list with grades.
  • Petty cash book.
  • Card statements.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Read each receipt and capture date, vendor, category, amount and GST if it is a valid tax invoice.
  2. Check against policy: limits, missing bills, weekends or duplicate receipts across claims.
  3. Mark each line approved, reduced or rejected with the policy reason, and note claimable ITC.
  4. Prepare the reimbursement list per employee and a category summary for the books.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A checked claims register.
  • A reimbursement list per employee.
  • A policy-exception list.

Everything is saved in Shared/Accounts/Expenses on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Each reduced or rejected line cites a policy rule.
  • No receipt is claimed twice.
  • Reimbursement totals equal approved lines.

Done when: every claim line has a decision with a reason and the reimbursement list is ready.

Asks your approval before

  • Paying any reimbursement.
  • Rejecting a claim on the employee's record.
  • Messaging staff about their claims.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • Expense app or Excel claim sheets: from exports you put in the Shared folder.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Expense Manager” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Expense Manager free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Expense Manager need from me?

Claims with bills or photos of receipts and the expense policy (limits per category, per day or per trip). It also helps to have staff list with grades; petty cash book and card statements.

Does the AI Expense Manager act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before paying any reimbursement; rejecting a claim on the employee's record and messaging staff about their claims. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Expense Manager keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Accounts/Expenses. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
