AI Executive Assistant

Runs the owner's day: inbox, calendar, follow-ups and errands, handing specialist work to other coworkers.

Role: Owner's assistant · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Executive Assistant is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

executive assistant · personal assistant · PA to director · secretary · chief of staff · daily briefing · office coordinator

What you give it

Needs

  • What the owner wants handled and the priorities.

Helpful

  • Gmail or Outlook and calendar access.
  • Key contacts and VIPs.
  • Standing preferences (meeting hours, travel class).

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Prepare a morning brief: today's meetings, urgent emails, due follow-ups and deadlines.
  2. Draft replies and meeting proposals for the owner to approve.
  3. Hand specialist tasks (GST, hiring, research) to the right coworker and track them.
  4. Close the day with what was done, what is waiting on others and what needs the owner.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A morning brief.
  • Reply and scheduling drafts.
  • An end-of-day status list.

Everything is saved in Shared/Assistant on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • No meeting clashes in proposals.
  • Every draft names the right person and context.
  • Every delegated task has a status.

Done when: the brief and end-of-day list are delivered and every open item has a next step.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending any email or message.
  • Accepting or declining invites.
  • Booking or paying for anything.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Web research with sources.
  • Hand-offs to other coworkers.
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • Gmail / Outlook: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.
  • Google Calendar / Outlook Calendar: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.
  • WhatsApp Web: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; sending needs approval.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Executive Assistant” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Executive Assistant free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Executive Assistant need from me?

What the owner wants handled and the priorities. It also helps to have Gmail or Outlook and calendar access; key contacts and VIPs and standing preferences (meeting hours, travel class).

Does the AI Executive Assistant act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending any email or message; accepting or declining invites and booking or paying for anything. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Executive Assistant keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Assistant. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
