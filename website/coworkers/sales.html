Coworkers

AI coworkers for sales & customers

Lead research, follow-ups, quotations, CRM upkeep and account care, with every outgoing message waiting for your approval. 12 ready-made coworkers, free in Sahkarmi for Windows.

  • AI Sales Follow-up

    Tracks leads and quotations and drafts polite follow-ups for each one.

  • AI Customer Support

    Drafts replies from your own policies and FAQs, and escalates what it can't answer.

  • AI Lead Researcher

    Finds businesses that fit your ideal customer and builds a sourced prospect list with a first-message draft for each.

  • AI Lead Qualifier

    Sorts incoming enquiries into hot, warm and cold with a reason, and drafts the right next reply for each.

  • AI CRM Administrator

    Cleans and de-duplicates your CRM or customer sheet, fixes fields and keeps stages honest.

  • AI Sales Ops Analyst

    Analyses the pipeline, win rates and salesperson performance, and builds the weekly sales review.

  • AI Account Manager

    Keeps a health view of your key customers, spots renewal and upsell chances, and prepares account reviews.

  • AI Proposal & Quotation Writer

    Turns a customer requirement into a priced quotation or proposal on your letterhead, with GST and terms.

  • AI Sales Deck Builder

    Builds a pitch or product deck tailored to one customer or segment, with proof points from your own material.

  • AI Customer Onboarding

    Runs a checklist for each new customer: documents, setup steps, welcome messages and the first check-in.

  • AI Customer Feedback Analyst

    Reads reviews, surveys and complaints, groups them into themes and tells you what to fix first.

  • AI Support Ticket Triage

    Sorts the whole support queue by type, urgency and SLA, routes each ticket and flags breaches before they happen.

Questions

Are these sales & customers coworkers free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

Can several coworkers work together?

Yes. Each coworker has its own chat, folder, browser and task queue, and coworkers can hand work to each other. A team run splits one goal into tasks for several coworkers.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
