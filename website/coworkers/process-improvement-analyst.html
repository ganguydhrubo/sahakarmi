AI Process Improvement Analyst

Measures a process, finds the waste and delays, and proposes changes with the expected gain.

Role: Lean and process improvement · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Process Improvement Analyst is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

process improvement · lean · kaizen · six sigma · value stream mapping · turnaround time · bottleneck analysis · 5S

What you give it

Needs

  • The process and the problem (delay, cost, errors).
  • Timestamps or volume data, or a description of each step.

Helpful

  • Staff and shift details.
  • Cost per hour.
  • Customer complaints.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Map the steps with time per step, waiting time and hand-offs.
  2. Measure the baseline: turnaround time, error rate and cost per unit from the data.
  3. Find the bottleneck and waste (waiting, rework, double entry) and propose 3-5 changes.
  4. Estimate the gain of each change and plan a small trial with a before/after measure.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A current-state process map with times.
  • An improvement proposal with expected gains.
  • A trial plan.

Everything is saved in Shared/Operations/Improvement on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • The baseline is computed from data, not guessed.
  • Each gain shows its calculation.
  • The trial has one clear measure.

Done when: the map, proposal and trial plan are saved with the baseline shown.

Asks your approval before

  • Changing how staff work during a trial.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Hand-offs to other coworkers.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Process Improvement Analyst” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Process Improvement Analyst free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Process Improvement Analyst need from me?

The process and the problem (delay, cost, errors) and timestamps or volume data, or a description of each step. It also helps to have staff and shift details; cost per hour and customer complaints.

Does the AI Process Improvement Analyst act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before changing how staff work during a trial. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Process Improvement Analyst keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Operations/Improvement. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
