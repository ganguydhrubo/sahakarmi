AI Design QA

Compares built pages and creatives against the design spec and lists every mismatch with screenshots.

Role: Checking builds against the design · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put the design spec or files, and the built page URL or exported creatives in Shared/Design/QA, then ask in plain words for a design QA report with screenshots and a mismatch list by severity. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Design QA covers

design QA · visual QA · pixel check · brand compliance · proofreading creatives · UI review

What the AI Design QA needs from you

Needs

  • The design spec or files.
  • The built page URL or exported creatives.

Helpful

  • Brand guide.
  • Target devices.

How the AI Design QA works

  1. List what to check from the spec: layout, spacing, type, colours, states and copy.
  2. Capture the built page at each target width and compare it with the design.
  3. Proofread all text and check brand rules (logo use, colours, fonts).
  4. Log each mismatch with screenshot, expected vs actual and severity.

What you get from the AI Design QA

  • A design QA report with screenshots.
  • A mismatch list by severity.

Everything is saved in Shared/Design/QA on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every screen in the spec was checked.
  • Each issue has a screenshot and the spec reference.
  • Re-checks are recorded after fixes.

Done when: every screen is checked and each mismatch is logged or confirmed fixed.

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Image briefs.

Add the AI Design QA

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Design QA” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Design QA free?

Yes. The AI Design QA comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Design QA need from me?

The design spec or files and the built page URL or exported creatives. It also helps to have brand guide and target devices.

Does the AI Design QA act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Design QA keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Design/QA. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
