AI Technical SEO Analyst

Crawls your site for broken links, slow pages, indexing and schema problems, and writes the fix list for your developer.

Role: Site health and technical SEO · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put the website URL in Shared/Marketing/SEO, then ask in plain words for a crawl report workbook; a ranked technical fix list for the developer and suggested schema snippets. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Technical SEO Analyst covers

technical SEO · site audit · crawl errors · Core Web Vitals · page speed · sitemap · robots.txt · schema markup · indexing issues · redirects

What the AI Technical SEO Analyst needs from you

Needs

  • The website URL.

Helpful

  • Google Search Console access.
  • The platform (WordPress, Shopify, Wix, custom).
  • A developer contact for the fix list.

How the AI Technical SEO Analyst works

  1. Crawl the site politely with a local script: status codes, redirects, canonical tags, titles, noindex, image sizes.
  2. Check robots.txt, the XML sitemap and Search Console coverage for pages not indexed and why.
  3. Test key templates in PageSpeed Insights for Core Web Vitals on mobile.
  4. Validate structured data (LocalBusiness, Product, FAQ) and write a fix list ranked by impact with how to fix each.

What you get from the AI Technical SEO Analyst

  • A crawl report workbook.
  • A ranked technical fix list for the developer.
  • Suggested schema snippets.

Everything is saved in Shared/Marketing/SEO on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every issue lists example URLs.
  • Crawl stays within the site and respects robots.txt.
  • Speed figures cite the test date and device.

Done when: the crawl report and fix list are saved with examples for each issue.

Asks your approval before

  • Changing site files, redirects or settings.
  • Submitting removals in Search Console.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Web research with sources.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Files in its folder and Shared.

Services

  • Google Search Console, PageSpeed Insights: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.

Add the AI Technical SEO Analyst

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Technical SEO Analyst” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Technical SEO Analyst free?

Yes. The AI Technical SEO Analyst comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Technical SEO Analyst need from me?

The website URL. It also helps to have Google Search Console access; the platform (WordPress, Shopify, Wix, custom) and a developer contact for the fix list.

Does the AI Technical SEO Analyst act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before changing site files, redirects or settings and submitting removals in Search Console. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Technical SEO Analyst keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Marketing/SEO. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
