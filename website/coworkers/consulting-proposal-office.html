AI Consulting Proposal Office

Assembles proposals, statements of work and client deliverables from the firm's templates and past work.

Role: Proposals and client deliverables · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put the client brief or RFP, and the firm's templates and past proposals in Shared in Shared/Consulting, then ask in plain words for a proposal or SOW document; a requirement compliance matrix and assembled client deliverables. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Consulting Proposal Office covers

proposal writer · RFP response · tender document · statement of work · consulting deliverable · client report · engagement letter · bid management

What the AI Consulting Proposal Office needs from you

Needs

  • The client brief or RFP.
  • The firm's templates and past proposals in Shared.

Helpful

  • Pricing guidelines.
  • Team CVs.
  • Case studies.

How the AI Consulting Proposal Office works

  1. Extract every requirement, deadline and evaluation criterion from the brief or RFP into a compliance matrix.
  2. Draft the proposal from templates: understanding, approach, plan, team, pricing and assumptions.
  3. Ask research or writing coworkers for sections when useful and merge them into one document.
  4. For deliverables, assemble analyses into the client format with a consistent style and an executive summary.

What you get from the AI Consulting Proposal Office

  • A proposal or SOW document.
  • A requirement compliance matrix.
  • Assembled client deliverables.

Everything is saved in Shared/Consulting on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every RFP requirement maps to a section.
  • Pricing totals recalculate.
  • No confidential detail from another client is reused.

Done when: the proposal and compliance matrix are complete and ready for partner review.

Asks your approval before

  • Submitting a proposal or bid.
  • Sending deliverables to the client.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Web research with sources.
  • Hand-offs to other coworkers.

Services

  • GeM / tender portals: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; read-only downloads.

Add the AI Consulting Proposal Office

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Consulting Proposal Office” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Consulting Proposal Office free?

Yes. The AI Consulting Proposal Office comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Consulting Proposal Office need from me?

The client brief or RFP and the firm's templates and past proposals in Shared. It also helps to have pricing guidelines; team CVs and case studies.

Does the AI Consulting Proposal Office act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before submitting a proposal or bid and sending deliverables to the client. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Consulting Proposal Office keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Consulting. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
