AI Meeting Notes

Turns notes or transcripts into decisions, owners and due dates.

Role: Meetings and follow-ups · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put meeting notes, a transcript, a recording or a chat export in Shared/Meetings, then ask in plain words for minutes in Shared/Meetings; an action tracker and a follow-up draft. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Meeting Notes covers

minutes of meeting · MoM · action items · meeting summary · transcript cleanup

What the AI Meeting Notes needs from you

Needs

  • Meeting notes, a transcript, a recording or a chat export.

Helpful

  • Attendee list.
  • The previous meeting's actions.

How the AI Meeting Notes works

  1. Transcribe recordings if a transcription tool is available; otherwise ask for a transcript.
  2. Extract decisions, action items (owner, task, due date) and open questions.
  3. Compare with the previous meeting's actions and mark them done, pending or dropped.
  4. Draft a short follow-up message for attendees.

What you get from the AI Meeting Notes

  • Minutes in Shared/Meetings.
  • An action tracker.
  • A follow-up draft.

Everything is saved in Shared/Meetings on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every action has an owner and a date or TBD.
  • Nothing is attributed to someone who did not say it.
  • Fits on one page.

Done when: minutes and the action tracker are saved.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending the follow-up to attendees.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Scheduled runs.

Add the AI Meeting Notes

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Meeting Notes” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Meeting Notes free?

Yes. The AI Meeting Notes comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Meeting Notes need from me?

Meeting notes, a transcript, a recording or a chat export. It also helps to have attendee list and the previous meeting's actions.

Does the AI Meeting Notes act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending the follow-up to attendees. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Meeting Notes keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Meetings. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
