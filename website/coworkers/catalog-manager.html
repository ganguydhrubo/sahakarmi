AI Catalog Manager

Keeps one clean product catalog and fills bulk upload sheets for every channel.

Role: Catalog data and bulk uploads · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put the product master or current listings exports in Shared/Ecommerce/Catalog, then ask in plain words for a SKU master; filled bulk upload sheets per channel and an errors and fixes log. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Catalog Manager covers

catalog management · cataloguing · bulk listing · flat file · SKU master · product attributes · variants · HSN codes

What the AI Catalog Manager needs from you

Needs

  • The product master or current listings exports.

Helpful

  • The channels' bulk templates.
  • Barcodes or GTINs.
  • HSN and GST rates.

How the AI Catalog Manager works

  1. Build a SKU master: SKU, parent/variant, attributes, HSN, GST rate, weight, dimensions, images, price.
  2. Find gaps and errors: missing attributes, duplicate SKUs, wrong variant links, mismatched prices across channels.
  3. Fill each channel's bulk template from the master and check it against the template rules.
  4. After upload, read the error report and fix the rows.

What you get from the AI Catalog Manager

  • A SKU master.
  • Filled bulk upload sheets per channel.
  • An errors and fixes log.

Everything is saved in Shared/Ecommerce/Catalog on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • No duplicate SKUs.
  • Mandatory template fields are filled.
  • HSN and GST rate present for every SKU.

Done when: the master is clean and every channel sheet passes its template checks.

Asks your approval before

  • Uploading or changing listings on any channel.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.

Services

  • Amazon Seller Central, Flipkart Seller Hub, Meesho, Shopify: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; template downloads and uploads.

Add the AI Catalog Manager

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Catalog Manager” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Catalog Manager free?

Yes. The AI Catalog Manager comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Catalog Manager need from me?

The product master or current listings exports. It also helps to have the channels' bulk templates; barcodes or GTINs and HSN and GST rates.

Does the AI Catalog Manager act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before uploading or changing listings on any channel. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Catalog Manager keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Ecommerce/Catalog. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
