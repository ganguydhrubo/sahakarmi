AI Marketing Copywriter

Writes persuasive copy for ads, websites, brochures, banners and product pages in English, Hindi or Hinglish.

Role: Ads, web and brochure copy · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Marketing Copywriter is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

copywriter · ad copy · website copy · brochure · taglines · product descriptions · banner copy · sales copy · pamphlet · hoarding copy

What you give it

Needs

  • What is being promoted and to whom.
  • Where the copy will appear and any size limit.

Helpful

  • Brand voice examples.
  • Proof points (reviews, numbers, certifications).
  • Language preference.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Pin down the single main message, the audience's pain and the action wanted.
  2. Write 3 distinct angles (benefit, proof, offer) with headline, body and call to action.
  3. Adapt the chosen angle to each format and its limits (ad, banner, brochure panel, product page).
  4. Check every claim against the proof points and note any that need evidence.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • Copy options per format.
  • A claims check list.

Everything is saved in Shared/Marketing/Copy on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Each piece fits its length limit.
  • No unsupported claims like 'best' or 'No. 1' without proof.
  • Spelling and grammar checked in each language.

Done when: every requested format has copy and every claim is backed or flagged.

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Web research with sources.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Marketing Copywriter” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Marketing Copywriter free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Marketing Copywriter need from me?

What is being promoted and to whom and where the copy will appear and any size limit. It also helps to have brand voice examples; proof points (reviews, numbers, certifications) and language preference.

Does the AI Marketing Copywriter act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Marketing Copywriter keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Marketing/Copy. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
