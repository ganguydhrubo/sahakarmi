AI Payroll Operations

Prepares the monthly salary sheet with PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS working and payslips, without paying anyone.

Role: Payroll working (PF, ESI, TDS) · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Payroll Operations is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

payroll · salary processing · payslips · PF · EPF ECR · ESI · professional tax · TDS on salary · Form 24Q · Form 16 · attendance and LOP · full and final settlement

What you give it

Needs

  • Employee master with salary structure.
  • Attendance and leave for the month.

Helpful

  • Employee investment declarations.
  • State for professional tax.
  • Last month's payroll.
  • Bonus, arrears or deductions.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Compute gross pay from structure, attendance and loss-of-pay days, plus arrears and bonus.
  2. Compute employee and employer PF (on the wage ceiling unless the owner says otherwise), ESI where eligible, state professional tax and monthly TDS under the employee's chosen regime.
  3. Prepare the PF ECR and ESI contribution files, the bank transfer sheet and payslips.
  4. Compare with last month and explain every change per employee.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A payroll register with statutory working.
  • Payslips per employee.
  • PF ECR, ESI and bank-transfer files ready for upload.

Everything is saved in Shared/Accounts/Payroll on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Net pay equals gross minus deductions for each employee.
  • PF and ESI totals match the upload files.
  • Month-on-month changes are each explained.

Done when: the register, payslips and upload files are saved and every change from last month is explained.

Asks your approval before

  • Uploading ECR or ESI returns.
  • Releasing salary or any payment.
  • Sending payslips to employees.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • EPFO and ESIC portals: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; the owner signs in; uploads need approval.
  • Attendance / HR software: from exports you put in the Shared folder.

Limits

Prepares payroll working only; rates and rules must be confirmed by the owner's CA or payroll adviser, and it never pays anyone.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Payroll Operations” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Payroll Operations free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Payroll Operations need from me?

Employee master with salary structure and attendance and leave for the month. It also helps to have employee investment declarations; state for professional tax; last month's payroll and bonus, arrears or deductions.

Does the AI Payroll Operations act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before uploading ECR or ESI returns; releasing salary or any payment and sending payslips to employees. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Payroll Operations keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Accounts/Payroll. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
