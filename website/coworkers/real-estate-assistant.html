AI Real Estate Assistant

Writes property listings, follows up leads, schedules site visits and keeps a RERA and property document checklist per deal.

Role: Listings, leads and site visits · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Real Estate Assistant is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

real estate agent assistant · property listing · 99acres · MagicBricks · Housing.com · lead follow-up · site visit scheduling · RERA documents · broker office

What you give it

Needs

  • Property details (location, size, price, photos) or the lead list.

Helpful

  • Portal access (99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com).
  • Project RERA registration number.
  • Builder brochures.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Write listing copy per portal with carpet area, price, amenities and the RERA number where applicable.
  2. Track leads by budget, location and stage, and draft follow-ups and site-visit confirmations.
  3. Look up the project on the state RERA portal and note registration, completion date and promoter details.
  4. Keep a per-deal document checklist (title, approvals, OC/CC, agreement draft, KYC) and mark what is missing.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • Listing copy per portal.
  • A lead and site-visit tracker.
  • A per-deal document checklist.

Everything is saved in Shared/Real Estate on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Area is stated as carpet area per RERA where required.
  • RERA details are quoted with the portal link and date.
  • Every lead has a next step.

Done when: listings, the lead tracker and each deal's checklist are saved and up to date.

Asks your approval before

  • Publishing or changing a listing.
  • Sending messages to leads.
  • Sharing a client's documents.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Web research with sources.
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.
  • State RERA portal: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; read-only project look-ups.

Limits

Not a lawyer: document checklists are for preparation and a lawyer must verify title and agreements.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Real Estate Assistant” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Real Estate Assistant free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Real Estate Assistant need from me?

Property details (location, size, price, photos) or the lead list. It also helps to have portal access (99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com); project RERA registration number and builder brochures.

Does the AI Real Estate Assistant act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before publishing or changing a listing; sending messages to leads and sharing a client's documents. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Real Estate Assistant keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Real Estate. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
