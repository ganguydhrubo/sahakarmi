AI Transcript Editor

Cleans raw interview, podcast or video transcripts into readable text, subtitles and quotable highlights.

Role: Transcripts and subtitles · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put the raw transcript or auto-captions (or the recording if a transcription tool is installed), and the style wanted (clean verbatim or full verbatim) in Shared/Writing/Transcripts, then ask in plain words for a clean transcript (Word or Markdown); SRT/VTT subtitle files and a highlights list with timestamps. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Transcript Editor covers

transcript cleanup · transcription editing · subtitles · SRT · captions · interview transcript · podcast transcript · YouTube captions · verbatim

What the AI Transcript Editor needs from you

Needs

  • The raw transcript or auto-captions (or the recording if a transcription tool is installed).
  • The style wanted (clean verbatim or full verbatim).

Helpful

  • Speaker names.
  • Glossary of names and terms.
  • The target language for subtitles.

How the AI Transcript Editor works

  1. Transcribe the recording with a local tool if available; otherwise work from the given transcript.
  2. Label speakers, fix names and terms from the glossary, and remove fillers for clean verbatim.
  3. Split into paragraphs with timestamps, and produce SRT/VTT subtitles with readable line lengths.
  4. Pull a summary and a list of quotable highlights with timestamps.

What you get from the AI Transcript Editor

  • A clean transcript (Word or Markdown).
  • SRT/VTT subtitle files.
  • A highlights list with timestamps.

Everything is saved in Shared/Writing/Transcripts on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • No words are added that the speaker did not say.
  • Subtitle timings stay in order and within the media length.
  • Unclear audio is marked [inaudible] with a timestamp.

Done when: the transcript, subtitles and highlights are saved.

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).

Add the AI Transcript Editor

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Transcript Editor” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Transcript Editor free?

Yes. The AI Transcript Editor comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Transcript Editor need from me?

The raw transcript or auto-captions (or the recording if a transcription tool is installed) and the style wanted (clean verbatim or full verbatim). It also helps to have speaker names; glossary of names and terms and the target language for subtitles.

Does the AI Transcript Editor act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Transcript Editor keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Writing/Transcripts. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
