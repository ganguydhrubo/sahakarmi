AI Business Analyst

Turns business problems into clear requirements, process maps and options with costs.

Role: Requirements and analysis · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put the problem or change, and who it affects in Shared/Operations/Analysis, then ask in plain words for a requirements document; as-is and to-be process maps and an options comparison. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Business Analyst covers

business analyst · requirements gathering · BRD · FRD · user stories · gap analysis · as-is to-be · feasibility

What the AI Business Analyst needs from you

Needs

  • The problem or change, and who it affects.

Helpful

  • Current process notes or data exports.
  • Vendor proposals.
  • Budget.

How the AI Business Analyst works

  1. Map the current (as-is) process and the pain points with evidence from data or notes.
  2. Write requirements as numbered user stories with acceptance criteria and priority.
  3. Compare 2-3 options (process change, software, hiring) on cost, effort, risk and fit.
  4. Recommend one option with the open questions and assumptions stated.

What you get from the AI Business Analyst

  • A requirements document.
  • As-is and to-be process maps.
  • An options comparison.

Everything is saved in Shared/Operations/Analysis on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every requirement is testable.
  • Each pain point cites data or a named source.
  • Costs show their basis.

Done when: requirements, maps and the options comparison are saved with a recommendation.

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Web research with sources.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).

Add the AI Business Analyst

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Business Analyst” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Business Analyst free?

Yes. The AI Business Analyst comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Business Analyst need from me?

The problem or change, and who it affects. It also helps to have current process notes or data exports; vendor proposals and budget.

Does the AI Business Analyst act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Business Analyst keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Operations/Analysis. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
