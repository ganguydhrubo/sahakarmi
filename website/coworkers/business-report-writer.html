AI Business Report Writer

Turns data and notes into clear business reports, project reports and board or bank documents.

Role: Reports and business documents · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put the purpose and reader of the report, and the data, notes and findings to use in Shared/Writing/Reports, then ask in plain words for the report in Word and PDF; the tables and charts used and a source and assumption list. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Business Report Writer covers

report writing · business report · project report · DPR · annual report · board note · executive summary · white paper · bank loan project report

What the AI Business Report Writer needs from you

Needs

  • The purpose and reader of the report.
  • The data, notes and findings to use.

Helpful

  • A required format (for example the bank's DPR format).
  • Past reports.
  • Charts or tables to include.

How the AI Business Report Writer works

  1. Agree the structure for the reader: summary first, then findings, analysis and recommendations.
  2. Pull figures from the data, build tables and charts, and ask the spreadsheet coworker for heavy analysis.
  3. Write each section plainly, with an executive summary of one page or less.
  4. Format with headings, numbered tables and figures, and an appendix of sources.

What you get from the AI Business Report Writer

  • The report in Word and PDF.
  • The tables and charts used.
  • A source and assumption list.

Everything is saved in Shared/Writing/Reports on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every figure ties to the source data.
  • The summary matches the body.
  • Required format sections are all present.

Done when: the report is saved in both formats with sources and assumptions listed.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending the report outside the business.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Hand-offs to other coworkers.

Add the AI Business Report Writer

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Business Report Writer” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Business Report Writer free?

Yes. The AI Business Report Writer comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Business Report Writer need from me?

The purpose and reader of the report and the data, notes and findings to use. It also helps to have a required format (for example the bank's DPR format); past reports and charts or tables to include.

Does the AI Business Report Writer act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending the report outside the business. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Business Report Writer keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Writing/Reports. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
