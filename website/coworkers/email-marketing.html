AI Email & WhatsApp Campaigns

Writes newsletters and automated sequences, segments the list and sends only to people who opted in.

Role: Email marketing and automation · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Email & WhatsApp Campaigns is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

email marketing · newsletter · email automation · drip campaign · welcome series · abandoned cart · Mailchimp · Brevo · Zoho Campaigns · WhatsApp broadcast · list segmentation

What you give it

Needs

  • The campaign goal and offer.
  • The contact list with consent status.

Helpful

  • Email tool access.
  • Brand template.
  • Past campaign results.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Clean the list: remove unsubscribed, bounced and no-consent contacts, and segment by behaviour or interest.
  2. Write subject lines (with an A/B variant), preview text and body, or a WhatsApp template within its rules.
  3. Build automated sequences (welcome, follow-up, win-back) with timing and exit conditions.
  4. Set up the campaign in the tool, send a test to the owner, and after sending report opens, clicks and unsubscribes.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • Campaign copy and sequence map.
  • A cleaned, segmented list.
  • A results report after sending.

Everything is saved in Shared/Marketing/Email on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • No contact without consent is in any send list.
  • Every email has a working unsubscribe link and the sender's address.
  • Links and personalisation tags tested.

Done when: the campaign is built and tested, and after approved sending the results are reported.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending any campaign or broadcast.
  • Turning on an automation.
  • Importing contacts into the tool.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • Mailchimp, Brevo, Zoho Campaigns, WhatsApp Business: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; sending needs approval.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Email & WhatsApp Campaigns” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Email & WhatsApp Campaigns free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Email & WhatsApp Campaigns need from me?

The campaign goal and offer and the contact list with consent status. It also helps to have email tool access; brand template and past campaign results.

Does the AI Email & WhatsApp Campaigns act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending any campaign or broadcast; turning on an automation and importing contacts into the tool. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Email & WhatsApp Campaigns keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Marketing/Email. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
