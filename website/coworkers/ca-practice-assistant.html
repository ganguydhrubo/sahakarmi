AI CA Practice Assistant

Runs a CA or tax practitioner's office work: client document collection, due-date tracking, working-file setup and status reports.

Role: CA and tax-practice office · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI CA Practice Assistant is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

CA office · article assistant · practice management · compliance calendar · client document collection · ITR documents · TDS due dates · audit file preparation

What you give it

Needs

  • The client list and the services per client (ITR, GST, TDS, audit, ROC).

Helpful

  • Practice software exports.
  • Document request templates.
  • The income-tax and GST due-date calendar.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Build a compliance calendar per client from official due dates, citing the notification for each date.
  2. Send document checklists per service (Form 16, AIS/26AS, bank statements, invoices) as drafts and track what has arrived.
  3. Set up each client's working folder with a standard index and pass GST work to the GST Accountant coworker if present.
  4. Produce a weekly status report: pending documents, work in progress and filings due in 7 days.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A compliance calendar.
  • A document-tracking sheet per client.
  • A weekly status report.

Everything is saved in Shared/CA Practice on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Due dates match official sources and extensions.
  • Every client service has a status.
  • Client files stay separate from each other.

Done when: the calendar, document tracker and status report are current.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending document requests to clients.
  • Filing or submitting anything on a government portal.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Web research with sources.
  • Scheduled runs.
  • Hand-offs to other coworkers.

Services

  • Practice management software: from exports you put in the Shared folder.
  • Income-tax, GST and MCA portals: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; read-only downloads by the owner.

Limits

Office support only; professional judgement, signing and filing stay with the CA.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “CA Practice Assistant” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI CA Practice Assistant free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI CA Practice Assistant need from me?

The client list and the services per client (ITR, GST, TDS, audit, ROC). It also helps to have practice software exports; document request templates and the income-tax and GST due-date calendar.

Does the AI CA Practice Assistant act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending document requests to clients and filing or submitting anything on a government portal. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI CA Practice Assistant keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/CA Practice. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
