AI Sales Ops Analyst

Analyses the pipeline, win rates and salesperson performance, and builds the weekly sales review.

Role: Pipeline and revenue analysis (RevOps) · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Sales Ops Analyst is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

sales operations · RevOps · revenue operations · pipeline analysis · sales forecast · win rate · sales targets · incentive calculation · territory analysis · sales MIS

What you give it

Needs

  • CRM deal export or sales register with dates, stages, values and owners.

Helpful

  • Targets per salesperson or branch.
  • Incentive scheme.
  • Last quarter's data.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Compute pipeline by stage, value and age, plus conversion rate between stages and average deal cycle.
  2. Compare actuals with targets per salesperson, branch and product.
  3. Forecast the month from weighted pipeline and run-rate, and show deals at risk.
  4. Work out incentives from the scheme where given, and write the weekly review with three actions.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A sales review workbook with charts.
  • A one-page weekly review.
  • An incentive working sheet if a scheme is given.

Everything is saved in Shared/Sales/Reports on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Totals tie to the CRM export or sales register.
  • Conversion rates use the same date window.
  • Incentive figures recompute from the scheme rules.

Done when: the review workbook and one-pager are saved with figures tied to the source.

Asks your approval before

  • Sharing salesperson performance with the team.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • Zoho CRM, HubSpot, Tally sales register: from exports you put in the Shared folder.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Sales Ops Analyst” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Sales Ops Analyst free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Sales Ops Analyst need from me?

CRM deal export or sales register with dates, stages, values and owners. It also helps to have targets per salesperson or branch; incentive scheme and last quarter's data.

Does the AI Sales Ops Analyst act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sharing salesperson performance with the team. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Sales Ops Analyst keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Sales/Reports. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
