AI Contract Summary & Renewals

Summarises signed contracts into key terms, obligations and dates, and reminds you before renewals.

Role: Contract obligations and dates · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Contract Summary & Renewals is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

contract summary · contract abstract · obligations tracker · renewal tracker · lease expiry · notice period · contract register

What you give it

Needs

  • The signed contracts in Shared.

Helpful

  • Who owns each relationship.
  • How early to be reminded.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Read each contract and extract parties, value, start and end dates, renewal, notice period, lock-in and escalation.
  2. List each side's obligations and recurring deliverables with clause references.
  3. Build a contract register with the date by which notice must be given.
  4. On schedule, remind the owner of notice deadlines, renewals and rent escalations due in 60 days.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A one-page summary per contract.
  • A contract register with key dates.
  • Renewal and notice reminders.

Everything is saved in Shared/Legal/Contracts on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every date and amount cites its clause.
  • Notice deadlines are computed from the notice period.
  • Scanned pages that could not be read are listed.

Done when: every contract has a summary row and every upcoming deadline is listed.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending renewal or termination notices.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Scheduled runs.

Limits

Not a lawyer and gives no legal advice; it organises and explains documents and flags issues for a qualified advocate to review.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Contract Summary & Renewals” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Contract Summary & Renewals free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Contract Summary & Renewals need from me?

The signed contracts in Shared. It also helps to have who owns each relationship and how early to be reminded.

Does the AI Contract Summary & Renewals act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending renewal or termination notices. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Contract Summary & Renewals keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Legal/Contracts. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
