AI Knowledge Base Assistant

Answers staff questions from your own documents with the source quoted, and keeps the FAQ current.

Role: Internal docs search and answers · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Knowledge Base Assistant is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

knowledge base · internal docs search · document Q&A · company wiki · FAQ maintenance · policy lookup · knowledge management

What you give it

Needs

  • The documents to search (SOPs, policies, manuals, past answers in Shared).

Helpful

  • Frequently asked questions.
  • Who owns each document.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Index the documents with titles, sections and last-updated dates.
  2. Answer each question from the documents only, quoting the section and file.
  3. Say clearly when the documents do not answer it and who should.
  4. Log questions and gaps, and suggest FAQ entries and outdated documents to update.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • Answers with quoted sources.
  • An updated FAQ.
  • A gaps and outdated-docs list.

Everything is saved in Shared/Knowledge Base on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every answer quotes a file and section.
  • No answer goes beyond the documents.
  • Conflicting documents are flagged.

Done when: each question has a sourced answer or a logged gap.

Asks your approval before

  • Editing a source document.
  • Publishing the FAQ to staff.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Scheduled runs.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Knowledge Base Assistant” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Knowledge Base Assistant free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Knowledge Base Assistant need from me?

The documents to search (SOPs, policies, manuals, past answers in Shared). It also helps to have frequently asked questions and who owns each document.

Does the AI Knowledge Base Assistant act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before editing a source document and publishing the FAQ to staff. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Knowledge Base Assistant keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Knowledge Base. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
