AI Account Manager

Keeps a health view of your key customers, spots renewal and upsell chances, and prepares account reviews.

Role: Key accounts and customer success · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Account Manager is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

account management · key account manager · customer success · customer retention · renewals · upsell · cross-sell · QBR · churn risk · repeat orders

What you give it

Needs

  • The customer list with order or subscription history.

Helpful

  • Support tickets or complaints.
  • Contract and renewal dates.
  • Notes from calls.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Score each account's health from order frequency, value trend, overdue payments and complaints.
  2. Flag churn risks (orders dropped, long silence) and renewals due in the next 60 days.
  3. Find upsell and cross-sell chances from what similar customers buy.
  4. Prepare a short account review per key customer and draft check-in messages.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • An account health sheet.
  • Account review notes for key customers.
  • Check-in and renewal message drafts.

Everything is saved in Shared/Sales/Accounts on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Health scores use the same rules for every account.
  • Each risk or opportunity cites the order data.
  • Renewal dates match contracts.

Done when: every key account has a health score and next action, and due renewals have drafts.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending messages to customers.
  • Offering discounts or contract changes.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Scheduled runs.
  • Hand-offs to other coworkers.

Services

  • CRM and order history: from exports you put in the Shared folder.
  • WhatsApp Business / email: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; sending needs approval.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Account Manager” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Account Manager free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Account Manager need from me?

The customer list with order or subscription history. It also helps to have support tickets or complaints; contract and renewal dates and notes from calls.

Does the AI Account Manager act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending messages to customers and offering discounts or contract changes. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Account Manager keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Sales/Accounts. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
