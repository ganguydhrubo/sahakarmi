AI Customer Review Analyst

Reads your product reviews and ratings, finds what customers love and hate, and drafts replies.

Role: Reviews and ratings · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put review exports or links to the product and store pages in Shared/Ecommerce/Reviews, then ask in plain words for a tagged review sheet; a themes and fixes report and reply drafts. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Customer Review Analyst covers

review analysis · ratings analysis · voice of customer · sentiment analysis · review replies · Google reviews

What the AI Customer Review Analyst needs from you

Needs

  • Review exports or links to the product and store pages.

Helpful

  • Seller-panel access.
  • Competitor review pages.
  • Return reasons.

How the AI Customer Review Analyst works

  1. Collect reviews per product with date, rating and text, in any language.
  2. Tag each review by theme (quality, size, delivery, packaging, value) and sentiment.
  3. Compare themes with competitors and list fixes for the product, listing or packaging.
  4. Draft polite public replies to negative reviews where the platform allows.

What you get from the AI Customer Review Analyst

  • A tagged review sheet.
  • A themes and fixes report.
  • Reply drafts.

Everything is saved in Shared/Ecommerce/Reviews on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Theme counts re-add to the review total.
  • Each fix cites example reviews.
  • Replies promise nothing outside your policy.

Done when: every review is tagged and the report lists fixes by product.

Asks your approval before

  • Posting any public reply.
  • Contacting a reviewer.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Web research with sources.

Services

  • Amazon, Flipkart, Google Business Profile, Shopify reviews: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.

Add the AI Customer Review Analyst

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Customer Review Analyst” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Customer Review Analyst free?

Yes. The AI Customer Review Analyst comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Customer Review Analyst need from me?

Review exports or links to the product and store pages. It also helps to have seller-panel access; competitor review pages and return reasons.

Does the AI Customer Review Analyst act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before posting any public reply and contacting a reviewer. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Customer Review Analyst keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Ecommerce/Reviews. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
