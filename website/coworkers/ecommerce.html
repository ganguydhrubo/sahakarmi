Coworkers

AI coworkers for e-commerce & retail

Product listings, catalogues, inventory, orders, returns and marketplace seller work for online and retail shops. 14 ready-made coworkers, free in Sahkarmi for Windows.

  • AI Inventory Planner

    Reads stock exports, flags low and dead stock, and suggests reorder quantities.

  • AI Marketplace Seller Assistant

    Writes product listings and checks prices, reviews and returns reports.

  • AI Product Listing Writer

    Writes product pages for your own online store: titles, descriptions, specs and FAQs.

  • AI Product Researcher

    Finds product ideas with real demand and checks competition, margin and sourcing before you invest.

  • AI Catalog Manager

    Keeps one clean product catalog and fills bulk upload sheets for every channel.

  • AI Pricing Researcher

    Tracks competitor prices and works out the price that keeps your margin after all fees.

  • AI Order Manager

    Watches orders across channels, keeps dispatch on time and flags COD and delivery problems.

  • AI Returns Processor

    Tracks returns and RTOs, checks what came back and files claims for lost or damaged items.

  • AI Customer Review Analyst

    Reads your product reviews and ratings, finds what customers love and hate, and drafts replies.

  • AI E-commerce SEO

    Improves how your store and products show up on Google and marketplace search.

  • AI Product Image Brief Creator

    Plans product photo shoots and writes image briefs that meet each marketplace's rules.

  • AI Sales Performance Analyst

    Shows what sells, on which channel, at what real profit, and checks marketplace payouts.

  • AI Retail Store Operations

    Runs daily store routines: opening checklists, cash and sales closing, stock counts and staff rosters.

  • AI Distributor & Dealer Operations

    Tracks dealer orders, secondary sales, schemes and claims across your distributor network.

Questions

Are these e-commerce & retail coworkers free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

Can several coworkers work together?

Yes. Each coworker has its own chat, folder, browser and task queue, and coworkers can hand work to each other. A team run splits one goal into tasks for several coworkers.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
