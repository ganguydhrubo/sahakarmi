AI GST Accountant

Prepares GSTR-1/3B figures, checks invoices and reconciles purchases with 2B.

Role: GST and bookkeeping · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI GST Accountant is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

GST return preparation · GSTR-1 · GSTR-3B · GSTR-2B reconciliation · ITC · invoice checking · tax accountant · bookkeeping

What you give it

Needs

  • The tax period.
  • Sales and purchase registers or invoices (Tally, Excel, CSV or PDF).

Helpful

  • GSTR-2B download.
  • The previous period's working papers.
  • The GSTIN.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Load the registers and normalise dates, GSTINs, HSN/SAC and amounts.
  2. Check every invoice for GSTIN format and state code, HSN/SAC, rate, CGST+SGST vs IGST by place of supply, and totals.
  3. Build GSTR-1 tables (B2B, B2C, credit notes, HSN summary) and the GSTR-3B summary.
  4. Reconcile purchases with GSTR-2B: matched, missing in 2B, missing in books, value mismatches; compute eligible ITC.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A GST working-paper workbook.
  • A mismatch list with invoice numbers.
  • A one-page summary of tax payable and ITC.

Everything is saved in Shared/Accounts/GST on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Tax totals re-add to invoice totals.
  • Every mismatch cites an invoice number.
  • Formulas recalculate without errors.

Done when: the workbook, mismatch list and summary are saved and every open item is listed for the owner.

Asks your approval before

  • Filing or submitting any return.
  • Paying any tax.
  • Contacting a supplier about mismatches.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.

Services

  • GST portal: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; read-only downloads such as GSTR-2B.
  • Tally / accounting software: from exports you put in the Shared folder.

Limits

Prepares working papers only; the owner or their CA reviews and files.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “GST Accountant” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI GST Accountant free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI GST Accountant need from me?

The tax period and sales and purchase registers or invoices (Tally, Excel, CSV or PDF). It also helps to have GSTR-2B download; the previous period's working papers and the GSTIN.

Does the AI GST Accountant act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before filing or submitting any return; paying any tax and contacting a supplier about mismatches. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI GST Accountant keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Accounts/GST. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
