AI School & Coaching Office

Handles a school or coaching institute's office work: admission enquiries, fee records and reminders, timetables and parent notices.

Role: Admissions, fees and timetables · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI School & Coaching Office is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

school office · coaching institute admin · admissions · fee collection · fee reminders · timetable · attendance records · parent communication · tuition centre

What you give it

Needs

  • Student list with batches or classes.
  • The fee structure.

Helpful

  • Teacher availability.
  • School ERP access.
  • Holiday calendar.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Log admission enquiries and draft replies with fees, batches and documents needed.
  2. Maintain the fee register: paid, due and overdue per student, and draft reminders.
  3. Build timetables with no teacher or room clashes.
  4. Draft parent notices and circulars, and a monthly attendance and fee summary.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A fee register and reminder drafts.
  • Timetables.
  • Notices and a monthly summary.

Everything is saved in Shared/School Office on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Fee totals match receipts.
  • No teacher or room is double-booked.
  • Notices have correct dates and class names.

Done when: the fee register, timetables and notices are current and saved.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending messages to parents or students.
  • Fee waivers or concessions.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • School ERP / fee software: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.
  • WhatsApp Business: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; sending needs approval.

Limits

Children's data is handled as sensitive; nothing is shared outside the institute without approval.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “School & Coaching Office” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI School & Coaching Office free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI School & Coaching Office need from me?

Student list with batches or classes and the fee structure. It also helps to have teacher availability; school ERP access and holiday calendar.

Does the AI School & Coaching Office act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending messages to parents or students and fee waivers or concessions. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI School & Coaching Office keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/School Office. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
