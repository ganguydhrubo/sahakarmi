Coworkers

AI coworkers for websites

Website content, audits, landing pages, accessibility and speed checks, and site upkeep. 11 ready-made coworkers, free in Sahkarmi for Windows.

  • AI Browser Task Agent

    Does repetitive work on any website in Sahkarmi's browser: look-ups, data entry, downloads and form filling, with a log of every step.

  • AI Website Auditor

    Audits a website for speed, mobile use, SEO basics, accessibility, security headers and broken pages, and ranks the fixes.

  • AI Website Content Manager

    Updates pages, posts, products and menus in WordPress, Shopify, Wix or Webflow, drafting first and publishing after approval.

  • AI Website QA Tester

    Tests a website's links, forms, checkout and mobile layout before and after changes, with screenshots of every issue.

  • AI Accessibility Reviewer

    Checks pages against WCAG 2.2 AA with automated scans and manual keyboard and screen-reader checks, and explains each fix.

  • AI Website SEO Auditor

    Finds technical and on-page SEO problems and local SEO gaps, and writes a fix plan per page.

  • AI Website Migration Planner

    Plans moves between platforms, hosts or domains with a URL redirect map, content inventory and launch checklist.

  • AI Analytics Reporting

    Builds monthly website reports from GA4 and Search Console: traffic, sources, conversions, queries and what changed.

  • AI Digital Asset Migration

    Moves images, documents and product media between websites, drives and platforms, keeping names, alt text and links intact.

  • AI Form & Enquiry Processor

    Collects enquiries from website forms and inboxes, removes spam and duplicates, logs them and drafts first replies.

  • AI Website Documentation

    Documents how a website is built and run: hosting, domain, CMS, plugins, forms, backups and how-to guides for staff.

Questions

Are these websites coworkers free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

Can several coworkers work together?

Yes. Each coworker has its own chat, folder, browser and task queue, and coworkers can hand work to each other. A team run splits one goal into tasks for several coworkers.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
