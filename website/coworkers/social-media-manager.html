AI Social Media Manager

Publishes approved posts on your own pages, answers comments and DMs from your FAQs, and reports what worked.

Role: Publishing and engagement · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

The sweets look good, the photo is good, and the post goes up three days after the festival. This coworker keeps the calendar, prepares the post on time, and collects the comments that need an answer. You approve each post and reply.

Example: a first task

“Our calendar and photos for this week are in Shared/Marketing/Social. Prepare the posts for Instagram and the Facebook Page, show me each one, and collect new comments that need a reply.”

What comes back

  • Each post ready for your approval with the account, time, photo and caption.
  • A log of comments and messages with a drafted reply from your FAQ.
  • A weekly report of reach and engagement, taken from the platform's own insights.

More you can ask

  • Show me comments that ask for price or delivery.
  • Which posts did best last month, and what is common in them?
  • Draft replies from our FAQ for today's messages.

Good to know

  • You sign in yourself in Sahkarmi's browser, and it works only on your brand's own accounts.
  • Publishing, replying, hiding a comment and boosting a post all need your approval.
  • Complaints and serious leads are passed to you, not answered by itself.

What the AI Social Media Manager covers

social media manager · social media executive · post scheduling · comment moderation · DM replies · Instagram · Facebook Page · LinkedIn Page · YouTube · social media report

What the AI Social Media Manager needs from you

Needs

  • The brand's own accounts to manage.
  • Approved posts or the content calendar.

Helpful

  • FAQs and price list for replies.
  • Brand voice examples.
  • Posting times that worked before.

How the AI Social Media Manager works

  1. Take approved posts from the calendar (or ask the social-media-writer coworker for them) and check media, captions and links.
  2. Schedule or publish each post on the right account at the planned time and record the live URL.
  3. Go through new comments and DMs: draft replies from the FAQs, hide spam, and escalate complaints or leads.
  4. Pull weekly reach, engagement and follower numbers per post and note the top and bottom performers.

What you get from the AI Social Media Manager

  • Published post links.
  • A comment and DM log with drafted replies.
  • A weekly performance report.

Everything is saved in Shared/Marketing/Social on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Each post went to the intended account and is live at its URL.
  • Replies only use facts from the FAQs.
  • Report numbers match the platform insights.

Done when: the week's posts are live with verified URLs, the inbox is handled or escalated, and the report is saved.

Asks your approval before

  • Publishing or scheduling posts.
  • Replying to comments or DMs.
  • Deleting or hiding comments.
  • Boosting any post.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Scheduled runs.
  • Hand-offs to other coworkers.

Services

  • Meta Business Suite, LinkedIn, YouTube Studio, X: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; only the brand's own accounts.

Questions

Is the AI Social Media Manager free?

Yes. The AI Social Media Manager comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Social Media Manager need from me?

The brand's own accounts to manage and approved posts or the content calendar. It also helps to have FAQs and price list for replies; brand voice examples and posting times that worked before.

Does the AI Social Media Manager act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before publishing or scheduling posts; replying to comments or DMs; deleting or hiding comments and boosting any post. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Social Media Manager keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Marketing/Social. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
