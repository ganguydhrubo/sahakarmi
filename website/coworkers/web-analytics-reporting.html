AI Analytics Reporting

Builds monthly website reports from GA4 and Search Console: traffic, sources, conversions, queries and what changed.

Role: GA4 and Search Console reports · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put the website and the reporting period in Shared/Web/Analytics, then ask in plain words for a report workbook with charts and a one-page summary with actions. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Analytics Reporting covers

Google Analytics 4 · GA4 report · Search Console report · website analytics · traffic report · conversion tracking · monthly marketing report

What the AI Analytics Reporting needs from you

Needs

  • The website and the reporting period.

Helpful

  • GA4 and Search Console access.
  • Goals and key events.
  • The previous report.

How the AI Analytics Reporting works

  1. Export users, sessions, sources, landing pages and key events from GA4, and queries and pages from Search Console.
  2. Compare with the previous period and the same period last year.
  3. Explain the main changes with likely reasons and check that key events are firing.
  4. Write a one-page summary with three recommended actions.

What you get from the AI Analytics Reporting

  • A report workbook with charts.
  • A one-page summary with actions.

Everything is saved in Shared/Web/Analytics on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Figures match the exports with the date range stated.
  • Percent changes are calculated on the same basis.
  • Tracking gaps are flagged, not filled in.

Done when: the report and summary are saved for the period.

Tools and services

  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • Google Analytics 4: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; read-only exports.
  • Google Search Console: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; read-only exports.

Add the AI Analytics Reporting

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Analytics Reporting” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Analytics Reporting free?

Yes. The AI Analytics Reporting comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Analytics Reporting need from me?

The website and the reporting period. It also helps to have GA4 and Search Console access; goals and key events and the previous report.

Does the AI Analytics Reporting act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Analytics Reporting keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Web/Analytics. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
