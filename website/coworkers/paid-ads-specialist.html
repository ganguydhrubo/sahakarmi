AI Paid Ads Specialist

Plans and builds Google and Meta ad campaigns: keywords or audiences, ad copy, budgets and tracking, ready for your approval.

Role: Google and Meta ad campaigns · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put what to promote, where (cities or pincodes) and the monthly budget in INR, and the landing page in Shared/Marketing/Ads, then ask in plain words for a campaign plan with budget split; ad copy and creative briefs and draft campaigns in the ad account. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Paid Ads Specialist covers

Google Ads · PPC · search ads · Performance Max · Meta ads · Facebook ads · Instagram ads · campaign setup · negative keywords · ad copy · media planning

What the AI Paid Ads Specialist needs from you

Needs

  • What to promote, where (cities or pincodes) and the monthly budget in INR.
  • The landing page.

Helpful

  • Ad account access.
  • Past campaign results.
  • Target cost per lead or sale.

How the AI Paid Ads Specialist works

  1. Set the goal and tracking: conversion actions, UTM tags and whether leads come by form, call or WhatsApp.
  2. Build the structure: campaigns, ad groups or ad sets, keywords with match types and negatives, or audiences and placements.
  3. Write ad copy within platform limits (headlines, descriptions, primary text) and creative briefs.
  4. Split the budget, set bids and location targeting, and prepare everything as drafts in the account.

What you get from the AI Paid Ads Specialist

  • A campaign plan with budget split.
  • Ad copy and creative briefs.
  • Draft campaigns in the ad account.

Everything is saved in Shared/Marketing/Ads on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Headlines and descriptions are within character limits.
  • Daily budgets add up to the approved monthly budget.
  • Conversion tracking is confirmed working before launch.

Done when: campaigns are drafted with tracking checked and are waiting for the owner's approval to launch.

Asks your approval before

  • Launching or unpausing any campaign.
  • Increasing any budget or bid.
  • Adding a payment method.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Web research with sources.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Image briefs.

Services

  • Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; launching or spending needs approval.

Add the AI Paid Ads Specialist

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Paid Ads Specialist” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Paid Ads Specialist free?

Yes. The AI Paid Ads Specialist comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Paid Ads Specialist need from me?

What to promote, where (cities or pincodes) and the monthly budget in INR and the landing page. It also helps to have ad account access; past campaign results and target cost per lead or sale.

Does the AI Paid Ads Specialist act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before launching or unpausing any campaign; increasing any budget or bid and adding a payment method. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Paid Ads Specialist keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Marketing/Ads. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
