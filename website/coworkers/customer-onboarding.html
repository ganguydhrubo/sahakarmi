AI Customer Onboarding

Runs a checklist for each new customer: documents, setup steps, welcome messages and the first check-in.

Role: New customer onboarding · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Customer Onboarding is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

customer onboarding · client onboarding · KYC collection · welcome kit · implementation checklist · account setup · handover from sales

What you give it

Needs

  • The new customer and what they bought.
  • Your onboarding steps or the sales handover notes.

Helpful

  • Required documents (GSTIN, PAN, agreement).
  • Training material or FAQs.
  • Who owns each step internally.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Create the customer's onboarding checklist with owners and dates from the template.
  2. Draft the welcome message and the list of documents and details needed from the customer.
  3. Track documents received and check them (GSTIN active, names match, agreement signed).
  4. Schedule the first-week and first-month check-ins and hand open issues to the right coworker or person.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • An onboarding checklist per customer.
  • Welcome and document-request drafts.
  • An onboarding status tracker.

Everything is saved in Shared/Sales/Onboarding on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every step has an owner and date.
  • Documents are checked against the requirement list.
  • The customer's name and plan match the order.

Done when: the checklist is complete or every open step has an owner and date.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending messages to the customer.
  • Activating accounts or services.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Scheduled runs.
  • Hand-offs to other coworkers.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.

Services

  • WhatsApp Business / email: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; sending needs approval.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Customer Onboarding” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Customer Onboarding free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Customer Onboarding need from me?

The new customer and what they bought and your onboarding steps or the sales handover notes. It also helps to have required documents (GSTIN, PAN, agreement); training material or FAQs and who owns each step internally.

Does the AI Customer Onboarding act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending messages to the customer and activating accounts or services. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Customer Onboarding keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Sales/Onboarding. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
