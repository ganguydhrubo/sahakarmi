AI Marketing Analytics

Joins GA4, ad, social and CRM numbers into one monthly view of which channels bring leads and sales.

Role: Website and funnel analytics · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Marketing Analytics is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

marketing analytics · Google Analytics 4 · GA4 · attribution · funnel analysis · UTM tracking · channel performance · marketing dashboard · Looker Studio

What you give it

Needs

  • GA4 access or exports.
  • The conversions that matter (form, call, WhatsApp click, purchase).

Helpful

  • Ad platform exports.
  • CRM leads and sales by source.
  • UTM naming rules.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Check tracking: key events firing, UTMs consistent, self-referrals and spam traffic excluded.
  2. Pull sessions, conversions and revenue by channel, campaign and landing page for the month.
  3. Join with CRM data to get leads, qualified leads and sales by source, and cost per sale where spend is known.
  4. Build the monthly dashboard and write the three biggest insights with actions.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A monthly marketing dashboard workbook.
  • A tracking issues list.
  • An insights note.

Everything is saved in Shared/Marketing/Analytics on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Channel totals match GA4 totals for the same dates.
  • Joins between GA4 and CRM state the match rate.
  • Tracking gaps are disclosed next to affected numbers.

Done when: the dashboard and insights note are saved with tracking caveats stated.

Asks your approval before

  • Changing GA4 or tag settings.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.
  • Ads and CRM exports: from exports you put in the Shared folder.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Marketing Analytics” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Marketing Analytics free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Marketing Analytics need from me?

GA4 access or exports and the conversions that matter (form, call, WhatsApp click, purchase). It also helps to have ad platform exports; CRM leads and sales by source and UTM naming rules.

Does the AI Marketing Analytics act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before changing GA4 or tag settings. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Marketing Analytics keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Marketing/Analytics. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
