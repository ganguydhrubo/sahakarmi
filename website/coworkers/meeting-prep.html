AI Meeting Preparation

Prepares a one-page brief before each meeting: who, why, history, agenda and what to ask.

Role: Briefs before meetings · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Meeting Preparation is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

meeting preparation · meeting brief · agenda · client briefing · pre-read · talking points · background research

What you give it

Needs

  • The meeting, attendees and purpose.

Helpful

  • Past emails, notes or proposals with the attendees.
  • Calendar access.
  • The outcome the owner wants.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Collect history with the attendees: past meetings, emails, orders, open issues.
  2. Research the attendees' company and recent news from reputable sources.
  3. Draft the agenda, talking points, questions to ask and likely objections.
  4. Deliver the brief before the meeting and, if scheduled, for every upcoming meeting.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A one-page meeting brief.
  • A draft agenda.
  • A list of open items with the attendees.

Everything is saved in Shared/Meetings/Prep on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Facts about people and companies are sourced.
  • History items cite the email or note.
  • Fits on one page.

Done when: the brief is saved before the meeting starts.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending the agenda to attendees.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Web research with sources.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • Google Calendar / Outlook, Gmail: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Meeting Preparation” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Meeting Preparation free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Meeting Preparation need from me?

The meeting, attendees and purpose. It also helps to have past emails, notes or proposals with the attendees; calendar access and the outcome the owner wants.

Does the AI Meeting Preparation act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending the agenda to attendees. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Meeting Preparation keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Meetings/Prep. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
