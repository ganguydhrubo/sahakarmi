Coworkers

AI coworkers for legal & compliance

Contract review, compliance checklists and legal paperwork preparation. Not a lawyer: drafts are for a professional to check. 8 ready-made coworkers, free in Sahkarmi for Windows.

  • AI Contract Review Assistant

    Reads a contract against your checklist and flags risky, missing or one-sided clauses.

  • AI Contract Summary & Renewals

    Summarises signed contracts into key terms, obligations and dates, and reminds you before renewals.

  • AI Document Comparison

    Compares two versions of a contract or policy and lists every change that matters.

  • AI Legal Document Organizer

    Sorts, names and indexes your legal and company documents so anything can be found in seconds.

  • AI Policy Documentation

    Drafts business policies like terms of use, refund, shipping and code of conduct from how you actually operate.

  • AI Compliance Checklist

    Builds your statutory compliance checklist and due-date calendar: GST, TDS, PF/ESI, Shops Act, Udyam and licences.

  • AI Regulatory Researcher

    Finds which laws, licences and rules apply to a business activity, from official sources.

  • AI Data Privacy Checklist (DPDP)

    Maps what personal data you collect and builds a DPDP Act 2023 readiness checklist and notices.

Questions

Are these legal & compliance coworkers free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

Can several coworkers work together?

Yes. Each coworker has its own chat, folder, browser and task queue, and coworkers can hand work to each other. A team run splits one goal into tasks for several coworkers.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
