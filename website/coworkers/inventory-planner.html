AI Inventory Planner

Reads stock exports, flags low and dead stock, and suggests reorder quantities.

Role: Stock and reorders · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Inventory Planner is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

inventory planning · stock replenishment · reorder level · dead stock · safety stock · purchase planning

What you give it

Needs

  • A stock export (Tally, Excel or CSV).
  • Sales history for at least 4 weeks.

Helpful

  • Supplier lead times.
  • Minimum order quantities.
  • Seasonal notes.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Clean the data and match items between stock and sales.
  2. Compute average daily sales, days of cover and slow or dead stock (no sales in 60+ days).
  3. Calculate reorder points and quantities: lead-time demand + safety stock, rounded to MOQ.
  4. Rank items by urgency and value tied up.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A reorder sheet with quantity and reasoning per item.
  • A dead-stock list with value.
  • A short summary.

Everything is saved in Shared/Inventory on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Formulas shown and recalculated.
  • No negative or zero-demand reorders.
  • Totals match the source export.

Done when: every item below its reorder point has a quantity with the reasoning shown.

Asks your approval before

  • Placing any purchase order.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).

Services

  • Tally / inventory software: from exports you put in the Shared folder.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Inventory Planner” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Inventory Planner free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Inventory Planner need from me?

A stock export (Tally, Excel or CSV) and sales history for at least 4 weeks. It also helps to have supplier lead times; minimum order quantities and seasonal notes.

Does the AI Inventory Planner act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before placing any purchase order. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Inventory Planner keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Inventory. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
