AI Quiz & Assessment Generator

Creates quizzes, tests and question papers with answer keys and marking schemes.

Role: Tests, quizzes and answer keys · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Quiz & Assessment Generator is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

quiz generator · question paper · assessment · MCQ · test series · marking scheme · answer key · worksheet

What you give it

Needs

  • The topics and the learners' level.
  • Question types and total marks or count.

Helpful

  • A blueprint or exam pattern.
  • Source material.
  • Google Forms or LMS access.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Build a blueprint: topics x difficulty x question type, with marks.
  2. Write questions with one clear correct answer, plausible distractors and an explanation.
  3. Prepare the paper, the answer key and the marking scheme, plus an online quiz version if asked.
  4. After a test, analyse scores per question and topic.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • The question paper.
  • An answer key with explanations and marking scheme.
  • A score analysis after the test.

Everything is saved in Shared/Education/Assessments on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Each question has exactly one correct answer or a clear scheme.
  • Marks total matches the blueprint.
  • No repeated questions.

Done when: the paper and answer key are saved and match the blueprint.

Asks your approval before

  • Publishing a quiz to students or sharing it with parents.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.

Services

  • Google Forms, Moodle, Google Classroom: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Quiz & Assessment Generator” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Quiz & Assessment Generator free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Quiz & Assessment Generator need from me?

The topics and the learners' level and question types and total marks or count. It also helps to have a blueprint or exam pattern; source material and Google Forms or LMS access.

Does the AI Quiz & Assessment Generator act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before publishing a quiz to students or sharing it with parents. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Quiz & Assessment Generator keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Education/Assessments. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
