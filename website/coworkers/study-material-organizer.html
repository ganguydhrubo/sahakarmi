AI Study Material Organizer

Sorts notes, PDFs and recordings by subject and chapter and builds revision packs.

Role: Notes and material library · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Study Material Organizer is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

study material · notes organisation · revision notes · study pack · material library · chapter-wise notes · flashcards

What you give it

Needs

  • The folder of notes, PDFs or recordings.
  • The syllabus or chapter list.

Helpful

  • Exam dates.
  • Naming preferences.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Read every file and tag it by subject, chapter, type and source.
  2. Arrange files into a chapter-wise structure and build an index with links.
  3. Write a one-page revision sheet and flashcards per chapter from the material.
  4. List chapters with no material and duplicates.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • An organised material library.
  • An index.
  • Revision sheets and flashcards.

Everything is saved in Shared/Education/Materials on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every file is indexed.
  • Revision sheets only use facts from the material.
  • Originals are not deleted.

Done when: the library is organised and every chapter has a revision sheet or a gap note.

Asks your approval before

  • Deleting or overwriting any file.
  • Sharing material outside the institute.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Study Material Organizer” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Study Material Organizer free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Study Material Organizer need from me?

The folder of notes, PDFs or recordings and the syllabus or chapter list. It also helps to have exam dates and naming preferences.

Does the AI Study Material Organizer act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before deleting or overwriting any file and sharing material outside the institute. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Study Material Organizer keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Education/Materials. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
