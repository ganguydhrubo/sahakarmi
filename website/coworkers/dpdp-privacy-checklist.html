AI Data Privacy Checklist (DPDP)

Maps what personal data you collect and builds a DPDP Act 2023 readiness checklist and notices.

Role: Personal data and DPDP readiness · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Data Privacy Checklist (DPDP) is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

data privacy · DPDP Act · personal data inventory · privacy notice · consent · data retention · data breach plan · data fiduciary

What you give it

Needs

  • Where the business collects personal data (website, app, forms, billing, CCTV, staff records).

Helpful

  • The current privacy policy.
  • Vendors that process data (payment, courier, CRM).
  • Existing consent forms.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Build a data map: what data, from whom, why, where stored, who can access, shared with whom, how long kept.
  2. Check each flow against DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025 duties: notice, consent, purpose, retention, security, breach reporting, rights and grievance handling.
  3. Draft a plain-language notice and consent text, a retention schedule and a breach response checklist.
  4. List gaps by priority with the phased compliance dates from official sources.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A personal data map.
  • A DPDP gap checklist.
  • Draft notice, consent text and breach checklist.

Everything is saved in Shared/Legal/Privacy on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every data flow has purpose, storage and retention.
  • Obligations cite the Act or Rules section.
  • Deadlines cite official notifications.

Done when: the data map and gap checklist are saved with drafts for every gap that needs one.

Asks your approval before

  • Publishing a privacy notice.
  • Deleting any personal data.
  • Notifying anyone of a breach.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Web research with sources.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.

Limits

Not a lawyer and gives no legal advice; it organises and explains documents and flags issues for a qualified advocate to review.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Data Privacy Checklist (DPDP)” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Data Privacy Checklist (DPDP) free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Data Privacy Checklist (DPDP) need from me?

Where the business collects personal data (website, app, forms, billing, CCTV, staff records). It also helps to have the current privacy policy; vendors that process data (payment, courier, CRM) and existing consent forms.

Does the AI Data Privacy Checklist (DPDP) act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before publishing a privacy notice; deleting any personal data and notifying anyone of a breach. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Data Privacy Checklist (DPDP) keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Legal/Privacy. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
