AI Operations Planner

Turns a repeating process into a checklist with owners and timing.

Role: Operations · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put the process to document and who is involved in Shared/Operations, then ask in plain words for an SOP checklist; a run tracker and a list of steps coworkers can automate. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Operations Planner covers

SOP · process documentation · operations · checklist · workflow design · month-end

What the AI Operations Planner needs from you

Needs

  • The process to document and who is involved.

Helpful

  • Existing notes or recordings of the process.
  • Tools used.

How the AI Operations Planner works

  1. Interview the owner (briefly) or read notes to map trigger, steps, owners, tools and timing.
  2. Find dependencies, hand-offs and failure points.
  3. Write a step-by-step checklist with a check after each risky step.
  4. Mark which steps a coworker can take over and set up the tracker for each run.

What you get from the AI Operations Planner

  • An SOP checklist.
  • A run tracker.
  • A list of steps coworkers can automate.

Everything is saved in Shared/Operations on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every step has an owner and timing.
  • No step depends on something not yet done.
  • The checklist can be followed by someone new.

Done when: the SOP and tracker are saved and the owner knows which steps are automated.

Asks your approval before

  • Assigning work to people outside the team.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Hand-offs to other coworkers.
  • Scheduled runs.

Add the AI Operations Planner

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Operations Planner” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Operations Planner free?

Yes. The AI Operations Planner comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Operations Planner need from me?

The process to document and who is involved. It also helps to have existing notes or recordings of the process and tools used.

Does the AI Operations Planner act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before assigning work to people outside the team. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Operations Planner keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Operations. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
