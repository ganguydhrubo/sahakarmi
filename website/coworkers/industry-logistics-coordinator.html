AI Logistics Coordinator

Tracks shipments across couriers and transporters, prepares dispatch and e-way bill details, and flags delays.

Role: Shipments and dispatch documents · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Logistics Coordinator is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

logistics coordinator · dispatch · shipment tracking · e-way bill · courier tracking · Shiprocket · Delhivery · transporter · LR / bilty · POD

What you give it

Needs

  • The dispatch list (invoice, consignee, items, vehicle or AWB numbers).

Helpful

  • Courier or aggregator access.
  • E-way bill portal access.
  • Transporter contacts.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Check each dispatch for e-way bill need (invoice value, distance) and prepare Part A/B details: GSTINs, HSN, value, vehicle and transporter ID.
  2. Track every AWB or LR and update status and expected delivery in the tracker.
  3. Flag delays, RTOs and missing PODs and draft messages to the transporter or customer.
  4. Send a daily dispatch and delivery summary.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A shipment tracker.
  • E-way bill detail sheets per dispatch.
  • A daily delay and POD report.

Everything is saved in Shared/Logistics on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • E-way bill details match the invoice.
  • Every shipment has a current status with check time.
  • Delays list the AWB/LR number.

Done when: every open shipment has a current status and each dispatch has its documents ready.

Asks your approval before

  • Generating or cancelling an e-way bill.
  • Booking a pickup or paying freight.
  • Messaging customers.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • Shiprocket, Delhivery, Blue Dart and other courier sites: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.
  • E-way bill portal: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; the owner generates the bill.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Logistics Coordinator” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Logistics Coordinator free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Logistics Coordinator need from me?

The dispatch list (invoice, consignee, items, vehicle or AWB numbers). It also helps to have courier or aggregator access; e-way bill portal access and transporter contacts.

Does the AI Logistics Coordinator act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before generating or cancelling an e-way bill; booking a pickup or paying freight and messaging customers. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Logistics Coordinator keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Logistics. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
