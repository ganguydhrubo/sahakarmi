Coworkers

AI coworkers for research & data

Web and document research, data collection and cleaning, spreadsheet analysis and sourced reports. 12 ready-made coworkers, free in Sahkarmi for Windows.

  • AI Research Analyst

    Answers business questions with sources, and keeps facts apart from opinion.

  • AI Market Sizing Analyst

    Estimates market size, demand and pricing for a product or city with a transparent, sourced model.

  • AI Web Research Assistant

    Finds facts, documents, contacts and answers on the web quickly, and always shows where each came from.

  • AI Competitor Intelligence

    Watches your competitors' prices, products, offers, hiring and news on a schedule and tells you what changed.

  • AI Data Collector

    Collects structured data from websites, PDFs and portals into a clean table, with a source for every row.

  • AI Data Cleaner

    Fixes messy spreadsheets: duplicates, mixed formats, broken dates, spelling variants and missing values.

  • AI Spreadsheet Analyst

    Answers questions from your Excel or Google Sheets data with formulas, pivots and charts you can reuse.

  • AI Dashboard Builder

    Builds a dashboard of your key numbers with clear charts that refresh from your exports.

  • AI Survey Analyst

    Designs short surveys, then analyses the responses with charts, cross-tabs and coded open answers.

  • AI Report Generator

    Turns the same exports into the same report every week or month automatically, so nobody rebuilds it by hand.

  • AI Document & Data Comparer

    Compares versions of reports, price lists, spec sheets and spreadsheets and lists every change that matters.

  • AI Knowledge Base Builder

    Gathers your scattered documents, chats and notes into an organised, searchable knowledge base with FAQs.

Questions

Are these research & data coworkers free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

Can several coworkers work together?

Yes. Each coworker has its own chat, folder, browser and task queue, and coworkers can hand work to each other. A team run splits one goal into tasks for several coworkers.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
