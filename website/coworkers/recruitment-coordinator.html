AI Recruitment Coordinator

Keeps the hiring pipeline moving: tracks every candidate's stage and schedules interviews.

Role: Hiring pipeline and interviews · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Recruitment Coordinator is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

recruitment coordinator · interview scheduling · candidate pipeline · applicant tracking · recruiter · talent acquisition coordinator · hiring tracker

What you give it

Needs

  • The open roles.
  • The candidate list (portal export, sheet or CVs in Shared/Hiring).
  • Interviewers and their free slots.

Helpful

  • Naukri, LinkedIn or Indeed recruiter access.
  • Interview rounds per role.
  • Message templates.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Build or update the pipeline tracker: candidate, role, source, stage, last contact, next step, owner.
  2. Flag candidates stuck in one stage for 5+ days and interviews with no feedback recorded.
  3. Match candidate availability with interviewer slots and draft invites with date, time (IST), mode and link or address.
  4. Draft status updates and polite regret messages, and record feedback and offers as the owner reports them.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • An updated pipeline tracker.
  • Interview schedule and invite drafts.
  • A weekly pipeline summary per role.

Everything is saved in Shared/Hiring/Pipeline on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • No candidate is duplicated across sources.
  • No interviewer is double-booked.
  • Every invite has date, time, mode and contact person.

Done when: every active candidate has a current stage and next step, and due interviews have invite drafts.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending any message or invite to a candidate.
  • Creating calendar events for interviewers.
  • Moving a candidate to rejected on a portal.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; reading applicants and updating stages.
  • Google Calendar / Outlook: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.
  • Gmail / WhatsApp Business: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; sending needs approval.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Recruitment Coordinator” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Recruitment Coordinator free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Recruitment Coordinator need from me?

The open roles; the candidate list (portal export, sheet or CVs in Shared/Hiring) and interviewers and their free slots. It also helps to have Naukri, LinkedIn or Indeed recruiter access; interview rounds per role and message templates.

Does the AI Recruitment Coordinator act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending any message or invite to a candidate; creating calendar events for interviewers and moving a candidate to rejected on a portal. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Recruitment Coordinator keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Hiring/Pipeline. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
