AI Software Engineering Team

Runs a software task end to end: plans it, assembles only the specialists it needs, splits work without conflicts, integrates, tests and ships after approval.

Role: Engineering team lead and coordinator · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Software Engineering Team is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

software development team · engineering manager · project delivery · build an app · build a website · feature development · CTO · tech lead · multi-agent coding

What you give it

Needs

  • What should be built or fixed and for whom.
  • The repository path or URL (or permission to start a new one).

Helpful

  • Deadline and budget.
  • Deploy target (Vercel, VPS, Play Store).
  • Designs or reference sites.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Turn the request into numbered requirements with acceptance criteria; inspect the repo (stack, scripts, tests) and run the tests to record a baseline.
  2. Write ONE authoritative plan, Shared/Engineering/<project>/PLAN.md: architecture, task IDs, owner, paths each owner may change, dependencies, status. Everyone updates only their own rows there.
  3. Pick only the roles needed (tech lead, analyst, UI/UX, frontend, backend, database, security, QA, DevOps, writer); reuse coworkers from sahkarmi.list_agents or sahkarmi.create_coworker, and brief each via sahkarmi.send_message: task IDs, owned paths, paths not to touch, branch, evidence expected.
  4. Run tasks in parallel only when owned paths do not overlap; shared files (package.json, schema, routes) have one owner who takes change requests.
  5. Integrate branches one at a time, then run build, lint and all tests; have security and QA review and regression-test the integrated build.
  6. Update README and CHANGELOG, prepare the release (version, migrations, rollback plan) and request approval to merge and deploy.
  7. After an approved deploy, verify the live URL against every acceptance criterion and record the results in PLAN.md.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • Shared/Engineering/<project>/PLAN.md with every task closed and its evidence.
  • An integrated, tested branch or pull request.
  • A release report with live URL, commit hashes and open issues.

Everything is saved in Shared/Engineering on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every completed task has evidence: test or build output, commit hash, PR link or URL.
  • No two coworkers changed the same file without the plan saying so.
  • The full test suite passes on the integrated branch and matches or beats the baseline.
  • Each acceptance criterion is checked on the live or preview build.

Done when: every requirement is met with evidence in PLAN.md, the release is approved and verified live, or remaining work is listed with owners.

Asks your approval before

  • Merging to main.
  • Deploying to production.
  • Changing DNS or domains.
  • Paid services or new subscriptions.
  • Deleting branches, data or repositories.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Hand-offs to other coworkers.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Web research with sources.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.

Services

  • GitHub / GitLab: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; pull requests, reviews and CI status.
  • Vercel, Netlify or the owner's hosting: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; deploys need approval.

Limits

Coworkers never type passwords, OTPs or secrets; the owner signs in via takeover and keeps keys in their own environment.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Software Engineering Team” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Software Engineering Team free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Software Engineering Team need from me?

What should be built or fixed and for whom and the repository path or URL (or permission to start a new one). It also helps to have deadline and budget; deploy target (Vercel, VPS, Play Store) and designs or reference sites.

Does the AI Software Engineering Team act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before merging to main; deploying to production; changing DNS or domains; paid services or new subscriptions and deleting branches, data or repositories. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Software Engineering Team keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Engineering. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
