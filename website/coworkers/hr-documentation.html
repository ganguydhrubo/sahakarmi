HR & hiring

AI HR Documentation

Drafts offer and appointment letters, HR letters and policies like leave and attendance.

Role: Letters and HR policies · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

The new person joins on Monday and the letter is still last year's file with another name in it. This coworker fills your own template, checks that the salary parts add up to the CTC, and lists what it was not sure about.

Example: a first task

“Make an offer letter and an appointment letter for Riya Sen, accounts assistant in Kolkata, joining on 1 December 2026. Her CTC and details are in Shared/HR/Documents/riya.txt and our letter template is in the same folder.”

What comes back

  • The offer letter and the appointment letter in Word and PDF, on your letterhead.
  • A CTC breakup sheet (basic, HRA, allowances, employer PF and ESI) that adds up to the stated CTC.
  • A points-to-confirm list for anything it had to assume.

More you can ask

  • Compare our leave policy with the new draft and list the changes.
  • Make a joining checklist for the first day.
  • Prepare an experience letter from the same template.

Good to know

  • For leave and attendance policies it checks the terms against your state's rules and the Labour Codes, and lists its sources.
  • Drafts only, not legal advice: have an HR or labour-law professional check new policies and termination letters.
  • It asks before any letter is sent to an employee or candidate, or a policy is shared with staff.

What the AI HR Documentation covers

offer letter · appointment letter · HR policy · leave policy · employee handbook · experience letter · relieving letter · salary structure

What the AI HR Documentation needs from you

Needs

  • The document needed.
  • Employee or policy details (name, role, CTC, dates, state of work).

Helpful

  • The company's existing templates.
  • The state Shops & Establishment rules that apply.
  • Letterhead.

How the AI HR Documentation works

  1. Start from your template, or a clean standard structure for the document type.
  2. Fill in details and a CTC breakup (basic, HRA, allowances, employer PF/ESI) that re-adds to the stated CTC.
  3. For policies, check leave, working-hour and holiday terms against the applicable state rules and Labour Codes, with sources.
  4. Produce Word and PDF versions on the letterhead and list points the owner must confirm.

What you get from the AI HR Documentation

  • The letter or policy in Word and PDF.
  • A CTC breakup sheet where relevant.
  • A points-to-confirm list with sources.

Everything is saved in Shared/HR/Documents on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Names, dates and amounts match the inputs.
  • CTC components total correctly.
  • Statutory minimums cited with source and date.

Done when: the document is saved in both formats and every assumption is listed.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending any letter to an employee or candidate.
  • Publishing a policy to staff.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Web research with sources.

Limits

Drafts only; not legal advice. Policies and termination letters should be reviewed by an HR or labour-law professional.

Questions

Is the AI HR Documentation free?

Yes. The AI HR Documentation comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI HR Documentation need from me?

The document needed and employee or policy details (name, role, CTC, dates, state of work). It also helps to have the company's existing templates; the state Shops & Establishment rules that apply and letterhead.

Does the AI HR Documentation act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending any letter to an employee or candidate and publishing a policy to staff. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI HR Documentation keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/HR/Documents. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
