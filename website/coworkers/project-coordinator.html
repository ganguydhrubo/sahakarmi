AI Project Coordinator

Plans projects into tasks, owners and dates, and keeps the plan current as work moves.

Role: Project planning and tracking · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put the project goal, deadline and team in Shared/Projects, then ask in plain words for a project plan with timeline; a kick-off note and a blockers and changes log. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Project Coordinator covers

project manager · project coordinator · project plan · Gantt chart · work breakdown structure · milestones · project scheduling

What the AI Project Coordinator needs from you

Needs

  • The project goal, deadline and team.

Helpful

  • Budget.
  • Existing task lists or a Trello, Asana or Jira board.
  • Known constraints.

How the AI Project Coordinator works

  1. Break the goal into milestones and tasks with owner, effort, start, due date and dependency.
  2. Find the critical path and tasks that clash with holidays or overloaded owners.
  3. Build the plan sheet with a timeline view and a short kick-off note.
  4. Each run, update status from what owners report, re-plan slipped dates and list blockers.

What you get from the AI Project Coordinator

  • A project plan with timeline.
  • A kick-off note.
  • A blockers and changes log.

Everything is saved in Shared/Projects on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every task has an owner and due date.
  • No task starts before its dependency ends.
  • The end date matches the critical path.

Done when: the plan is saved, the critical path is shown and every blocker has an owner.

Asks your approval before

  • Assigning tasks to people in an outside tool.
  • Sending updates to clients or vendors.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Hand-offs to other coworkers.
  • Scheduled runs.

Services

  • Trello, Asana, Jira, ClickUp, Google Sheets: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Project Coordinator” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Project Coordinator free?

Yes. The AI Project Coordinator comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Project Coordinator need from me?

The project goal, deadline and team. It also helps to have budget; existing task lists or a Trello, Asana or Jira board and known constraints.

Does the AI Project Coordinator act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before assigning tasks to people in an outside tool and sending updates to clients or vendors. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Project Coordinator keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Projects. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
