Education & training

AI Learning Content Developer

Turns a syllabus or expertise into structured course modules, notes and practice work.

Role: Course and module content · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put the topic or syllabus, and the learners (age, level, language) and the outcome in Shared/Education/Courses, then ask in plain words for a course outline with outcomes; lesson content per module and practice sets with answer keys. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Learning Content Developer covers

learning content developer · instructional designer · course content · e-learning · module writing · curriculum design · content developer

What the AI Learning Content Developer needs from you

Needs

  • The topic or syllabus.
  • The learners (age, level, language) and the outcome.

Helpful

  • The teacher's notes, slides or recordings.
  • Board or exam (CBSE, ICSE, state board, NEET, JEE).
  • Hours available.

How the AI Learning Content Developer works

  1. Write measurable learning outcomes and split the syllabus into modules and lessons.
  2. Write each lesson: explanation, worked examples, Indian real-life examples and a summary.
  3. Add practice questions with answers and a short image or diagram brief where it helps.
  4. Package modules as Word or PDF, with a course outline and teacher notes.

What you get from the AI Learning Content Developer

  • A course outline with outcomes.
  • Lesson content per module.
  • Practice sets with answer keys.

Everything is saved in Shared/Education/Courses on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every outcome is covered by a lesson and a practice question.
  • Facts and formulas checked against standard textbooks or sources.
  • Reading level fits the learners.

Done when: every module is written and checked, and the outline is saved.

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Web research with sources.
  • Image briefs.
  • Hand-offs to other coworkers.

Add the AI Learning Content Developer

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Learning Content Developer” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Learning Content Developer free?

Yes. The AI Learning Content Developer comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Learning Content Developer need from me?

The topic or syllabus and the learners (age, level, language) and the outcome. It also helps to have the teacher's notes, slides or recordings; board or exam (CBSE, ICSE, state board, NEET, JEE) and hours available.

Does the AI Learning Content Developer act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Learning Content Developer keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Education/Courses. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
