AI Contract Review Assistant

Reads a contract against your checklist and flags risky, missing or one-sided clauses.

Role: Contract first-read · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Contract Review Assistant is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

contract review · agreement review · vendor agreement · NDA review · clause risk · redlines · service agreement · lease agreement

What you give it

Needs

  • The contract (Word or PDF).
  • Which side the owner is on and what matters most (payment, liability, exit).

Helpful

  • Your standard positions or playbook.
  • Earlier signed versions with the same party.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Extract parties, term, payment, deliverables, liability, indemnity, termination, IP, confidentiality, jurisdiction and stamp duty notes.
  2. Compare each clause with your checklist and mark it OK, review or missing.
  3. Explain each flagged clause in plain English or Hindi with the exact clause number and text.
  4. Suggest negotiation questions and alternative wording for the advocate to consider.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A clause-by-clause review table.
  • A plain-language risk summary.
  • Questions for the advocate and counterparty.

Everything is saved in Shared/Legal/Contract Reviews on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every flag quotes the clause number and text.
  • No clause is reported missing without a full-text search.
  • Suggestions are marked as for advocate review.

Done when: every key clause is covered in the table and the summary lists the top risks.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending comments or redlines to the other party.
  • Signing or e-signing anything.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Web research with sources.

Limits

Not a lawyer and gives no legal advice; it organises and explains documents and flags issues for a qualified advocate to review.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Contract Review Assistant” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Contract Review Assistant free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Contract Review Assistant need from me?

The contract (Word or PDF) and which side the owner is on and what matters most (payment, liability, exit). It also helps to have the owner's standard positions or playbook and earlier signed versions with the same party.

Does the AI Contract Review Assistant act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending comments or redlines to the other party and signing or e-signing anything. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Contract Review Assistant keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Legal/Contract Reviews. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
