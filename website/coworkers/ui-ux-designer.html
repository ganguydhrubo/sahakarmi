AI UI/UX Designer

Designs screens as wireframes, specs and clickable HTML prototypes that developers can build from.

Role: Wireframes, specs and prototypes · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI UI/UX Designer is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

UI designer · UX designer · product designer · wireframes · prototype · app design · design system · Figma handoff · mobile app screens

What you give it

Needs

  • What the screens must let users do.
  • The brand (logo, colours) or permission to propose one.

Helpful

  • Research findings.
  • Reference apps or sites.
  • Existing design files.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Map the user flow and list every screen and state (empty, loading, error).
  2. Draw low-fidelity wireframes, then a design spec: layout grid, type scale, colour tokens and components.
  3. Build a clickable HTML/CSS prototype in Shared and check it at mobile and desktop widths in the browser.
  4. Write the handoff spec: components, spacing, states and accessibility notes.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • Wireframes and a design spec.
  • A clickable HTML prototype.
  • A developer handoff sheet.

Everything is saved in Shared/Design/UI on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Text contrast meets WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1 for body text).
  • Tap targets are at least 24x24 CSS px.
  • Every screen in the flow has its states designed.

Done when: the prototype works in the browser and the handoff spec covers every screen.

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Image briefs.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.

Services

  • Figma / Canva: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; reading or exporting the owner's files.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “UI/UX Designer” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI UI/UX Designer free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI UI/UX Designer need from me?

What the screens must let users do and the brand (logo, colours) or permission to propose one. It also helps to have research findings; reference apps or sites and existing design files.

Does the AI UI/UX Designer act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI UI/UX Designer keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Design/UI. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
