AI Travel Researcher

Researches trains, flights and hotels and builds a ready itinerary for business trips.

Role: Business travel planning · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Travel Researcher is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

travel planning · business travel · itinerary · flight search · train booking research · IRCTC · hotel research · travel desk

What you give it

Needs

  • Cities, dates and purpose of the trip.
  • Budget and travel class.

Helpful

  • Meeting addresses.
  • Preferred airlines or hotel chains.
  • IRCTC or booking site access.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Compare 2-3 options for each leg: time, duration, fare, refund rules, and train availability or waitlist.
  2. Shortlist hotels near the meetings on price, reviews and GST invoice availability.
  3. Build a day-by-day itinerary with buffers, local transport and meeting addresses.
  4. After approval, book on your account and save tickets and invoices.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • An options comparison.
  • A day-by-day itinerary.
  • Tickets and invoices saved when booked.

Everything is saved in Shared/Assistant/Travel on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Fares and timings are dated and from the booking site.
  • Connections have realistic buffers.
  • Names match ID documents.

Done when: the itinerary is saved and approved bookings are confirmed with PNRs.

Asks your approval before

  • Booking or paying for any ticket or hotel.
  • Cancelling a booking.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Web research with sources.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.

Services

  • IRCTC, MakeMyTrip, airline and hotel sites: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; booking needs approval.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Travel Researcher” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Travel Researcher free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Travel Researcher need from me?

Cities, dates and purpose of the trip and budget and travel class. It also helps to have meeting addresses; preferred airlines or hotel chains and IRCTC or booking site access.

Does the AI Travel Researcher act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before booking or paying for any ticket or hotel and cancelling a booking. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Travel Researcher keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Assistant/Travel. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
