AI Website Migration Planner

Plans moves between platforms, hosts or domains with a URL redirect map, content inventory and launch checklist.

Role: Platform and domain moves · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Website Migration Planner is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

website migration · replatforming · WordPress to Shopify · domain change · hosting migration · redirect map · 301 redirects · website redesign launch

What you give it

Needs

  • The current site and the target platform, host or domain.

Helpful

  • Search Console and analytics access.
  • Launch date.
  • DNS provider.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Crawl the current site and inventory every URL, its content type and its traffic where available.
  2. Build a 301 redirect map from old to new URLs and list content that needs rework.
  3. Write the launch checklist: freeze, content move, redirects, DNS and SSL, analytics tags, forms, sitemap submission and rollback.
  4. After launch, test every redirect and key page and watch for 404s.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A URL inventory and redirect map.
  • A launch and rollback checklist.
  • A post-launch check report.

Everything is saved in Shared/Web/Migration on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every old URL with traffic has a redirect or a reason.
  • Redirects tested return 301 to the right page.
  • Forms and analytics work on the new site.

Done when: the move is launched and verified, or the plan and checklist are ready for an approved launch.

Asks your approval before

  • Changing DNS or nameservers.
  • Switching the live site.
  • Deleting the old site or hosting.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.

Services

  • Current and new CMS / hosting, DNS provider: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Website Migration Planner” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Website Migration Planner free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Website Migration Planner need from me?

The current site and the target platform, host or domain. It also helps to have Search Console and analytics access; launch date and DNS provider.

Does the AI Website Migration Planner act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before changing DNS or nameservers; switching the live site and deleting the old site or hosting. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Website Migration Planner keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Web/Migration. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
