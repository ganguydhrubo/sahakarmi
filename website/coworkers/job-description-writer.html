AI Job Description Writer

Writes clear job descriptions and portal-ready job ads with realistic pay and requirements.

Role: Job descriptions and job ads · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Job Description Writer is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

job description · JD writing · job ad · job posting copy · role profile · KRA · key result areas

What you give it

Needs

  • The role, team and location.
  • What the person will do in the first 90 days.

Helpful

  • Salary range.
  • An existing JD to improve.
  • Similar roles from competitors.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Read 5-10 comparable live ads for the role and city to see typical titles, pay and must-haves.
  2. Write the JD: purpose, KRAs with measures, must-have vs nice-to-have skills, work hours, location and pay.
  3. Make short portal versions (Naukri, LinkedIn, apna or WhatsApp) within each format's length, in English or Hindi.
  4. List requirements that may shrink the applicant pool for no reason (degree, years) for the owner to confirm.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A full JD document.
  • Portal-ready job ad versions.
  • A note on pay and requirement benchmarks with sources.

Everything is saved in Shared/Hiring/Job Descriptions on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • No age, gender, religion, caste or marital-status preferences.
  • Must-haves are testable.
  • Pay and benchmarks cite their source ads.

Done when: the JD and every requested ad version are saved with the benchmark note.

Asks your approval before

  • Posting the job on any portal.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Web research with sources.

Services

  • Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed, apna: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; reading comparable ads only.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Job Description Writer” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Job Description Writer free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Job Description Writer need from me?

The role, team and location and what the person will do in the first 90 days. It also helps to have salary range; an existing JD to improve and similar roles from competitors.

Does the AI Job Description Writer act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before posting the job on any portal. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Job Description Writer keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Hiring/Job Descriptions. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
