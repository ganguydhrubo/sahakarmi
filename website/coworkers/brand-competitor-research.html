AI Brand & Competitor Researcher

Compares how you and your competitors talk, price, advertise and get reviewed, and suggests how to stand apart.

Role: Positioning and competitor messaging · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Brand & Competitor Researcher is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

brand research · competitor analysis · positioning · messaging audit · Meta Ad Library · brand audit · USP · share of voice

What you give it

Needs

  • Your brand and 3-6 competitors (or ask to find them).

Helpful

  • Your customer personas.
  • Markets or cities.
  • Your brand guidelines.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Collect each brand's website promise, offers and prices, social tone, running ads and review themes, with dates and links.
  2. Lay out a messaging grid: who they target, main claim, proof, price position, visual style.
  3. Find gaps no one owns and claims everyone repeats.
  4. Suggest positioning lines and message tests for your brand.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A competitor messaging grid with screenshots.
  • A positioning note with suggested lines.
  • A source list.

Everything is saved in Shared/Marketing/Research on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every claim about a competitor has a dated link or screenshot.
  • Facts and suggestions are separate.
  • Your own brand is assessed by the same rules.

Done when: the grid and positioning note are saved with sources for every competitor point.

Tools and services

  • Web research with sources.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Image briefs.

Services

  • Meta Ad Library, Google Ads Transparency Center: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; public data.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Brand & Competitor Researcher” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Brand & Competitor Researcher free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Brand & Competitor Researcher need from me?

Your brand and 3-6 competitors (or ask to find them). It also helps to have your customer personas; markets or cities and your brand guidelines.

Does the AI Brand & Competitor Researcher act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Brand & Competitor Researcher keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Marketing/Research. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
