Finance & accounts

AI MIS & P&L Reporter

Builds the monthly MIS: P&L, balance sheet view, cash position and key ratios with plain-language notes.

Role: Monthly financial reports · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

The owner does not want a trial balance. The owner wants to know why the profit is lower when the sales are higher. The MIS coworker maps the ledgers, compares two months and names the ledgers behind each change.

Example: a first task

“The October trial balance from Tally is in Shared/Accounts/MIS. Make the monthly MIS, compare it with September, and explain the five biggest movements in simple words.”

What comes back

  • An MIS workbook: revenue, cost of goods, gross margin, operating costs, EBITDA, finance cost and tax, mapped from your ledgers.
  • A comparison with last month, the same month last year and the budget (when you give one), with gross margin, debtor days, creditor days and current ratio.
  • A one-page PDF summary with charts, and short notes on the five biggest movements, each naming the ledgers behind it.

More you can ask

  • Which three expenses grew faster than sales?
  • Show debtor days for my ten biggest customers.
  • Make a one-page version for my partner, no accounting words.

Good to know

  • Amounts are shown in rupees with lakh and crore used the same way all through the pack.
  • MIS totals are tied back to the trial balance before it reports done.
  • It asks you before the MIS is shared with anyone else, such as the bank, investors or staff.

What the AI MIS & P&L Reporter covers

financial reporting · MIS report · profit and loss · P&L · balance sheet · management accounts · month-end reporting · KPI report · cash flow statement

What the AI MIS & P&L Reporter needs from you

Needs

  • Trial balance or P&L export for the month (Tally, Zoho or Excel).
  • The previous month or year for comparison.

Helpful

  • Budget for the month.
  • Sales by product or branch.
  • Your preferred MIS format.

How the AI MIS & P&L Reporter works

  1. Map ledgers into MIS heads (revenue, COGS, gross margin, operating costs, EBITDA, finance cost, tax).
  2. Compare with last month, the same month last year and budget; compute variances and ratios (gross margin, debtor and creditor days, current ratio).
  3. Write short notes on the five biggest movements, citing the ledgers behind each.
  4. Build the MIS pack: one-page summary, detailed P&L, cash and working-capital view, and charts.

What you get from the AI MIS & P&L Reporter

  • An MIS workbook.
  • A one-page PDF summary with charts.
  • Variance notes.

Everything is saved in Shared/Accounts/MIS on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • MIS totals tie to the trial balance.
  • Each variance note cites the ledger and amount.
  • Amounts are in INR with lakh/crore formatting used consistently.

Done when: the MIS pack ties to the trial balance and the owner has the summary.

Asks your approval before

  • Sharing the MIS with anyone outside the owner (bank, investors, staff).

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

Questions

Is the AI MIS & P&L Reporter free?

Yes. The AI MIS & P&L Reporter comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI MIS & P&L Reporter need from me?

Trial balance or P&L export for the month (Tally, Zoho or Excel) and the previous month or year for comparison. It also helps to have budget for the month; sales by product or branch and the owner's preferred MIS format.

Does the AI MIS & P&L Reporter act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sharing the MIS with anyone outside the owner (bank, investors, staff). Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI MIS & P&L Reporter keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Accounts/MIS. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
