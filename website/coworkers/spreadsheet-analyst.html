AI Spreadsheet Analyst

Answers questions from your Excel or Google Sheets data with formulas, pivots and charts you can reuse.

Role: Excel and Google Sheets analysis · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

The file has 4,000 rows and one simple question: which items are not moving? This coworker answers it in a new sheet with live formulas, so next month you paste new rows and the answer changes by itself. Your own sheet stays as it was.

Example: a first task

“stock.xlsx in Shared/Research/Spreadsheets has all our items with last sale date. Show which items did not sell for 90 days and how much money is stuck. Do not change my sheet.”

What comes back

  • A workbook with a new analysis sheet, using live formulas and a pivot.
  • A short answer with the main numbers.
  • A note on how to refresh it next month.

More you can ask

  • Which ten items block the most money?
  • Make a chart of monthly sales by city.
  • Explain this formula to me in simple words.

Good to know

  • Totals are checked against your raw rows before it reports done.
  • It asks before writing over your own sheet.
  • Dates saved as text are a common problem; it finds them and tells you.

What the AI Spreadsheet Analyst covers

Excel analyst · Google Sheets · spreadsheet analysis · pivot tables · VLOOKUP · XLOOKUP · formulas · macros · data analysis · MIS executive

What the AI Spreadsheet Analyst needs from you

Needs

  • The spreadsheet or export.
  • The question to answer.

Helpful

  • How the result will be used.
  • Google Sheets access.
  • An existing template.

How the AI Spreadsheet Analyst works

  1. Understand the sheet's structure and check data types and ranges.
  2. Build the analysis with live formulas and pivot tables in a new sheet, not by pasting values.
  3. Add charts and a summary block that answers the question in plain words.
  4. Document what each formula or sheet does so the owner can reuse it next month.

What you get from the AI Spreadsheet Analyst

  • The workbook with analysis sheets.
  • A short answer with key numbers.
  • Notes on how to refresh it.

Everything is saved in Shared/Research/Spreadsheets on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Formulas recalculate without errors.
  • Totals match the raw data.
  • Source data is untouched.

Done when: the question is answered in the workbook and the summary.

Asks your approval before

  • Overwriting your original sheet.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.

Services

  • Google Sheets: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.

Questions

Is the AI Spreadsheet Analyst free?

Yes. The AI Spreadsheet Analyst comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Spreadsheet Analyst need from me?

The spreadsheet or export and the question to answer. It also helps to have how the result will be used; Google Sheets access and an existing template.

Does the AI Spreadsheet Analyst act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before overwriting the owner's original sheet. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Spreadsheet Analyst keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Research/Spreadsheets. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
