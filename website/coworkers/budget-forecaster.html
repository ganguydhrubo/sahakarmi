AI Budget & Cash Forecaster

Builds a yearly budget and a rolling 13-week cash forecast with clear assumptions.

Role: Budgets and cash flow forecasts · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Budget & Cash Forecaster is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

budgeting · forecasting · cash flow forecast · financial planning · FP&A · break-even analysis · scenario planning · working capital planning

What you give it

Needs

  • Last 12 months of P&L or sales and expense data.
  • Your plans (new hires, price changes, big purchases).

Helpful

  • Receivables and payables ageing.
  • Loan EMIs and GST/TDS due dates.
  • Seasonality notes (festive months).

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Find the base run-rate and seasonality from history, and list every assumption in one sheet.
  2. Build the monthly budget by head, driven by the assumption sheet so changes flow through.
  3. Build a 13-week cash forecast from opening balance, expected collections, payables, salaries, GST/TDS, EMIs and rent.
  4. Run base, low and high scenarios and show break-even and the weeks where cash is tight.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A budget and forecast workbook driven by assumptions.
  • A cash-gap chart with the weeks at risk.
  • A one-page assumptions note.

Everything is saved in Shared/Accounts/Budget on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Changing an assumption updates every dependent cell.
  • Opening cash plus inflows minus outflows equals closing cash each week.
  • History used ties to the source data.

Done when: the budget and cash forecast are saved with every assumption visible and editable.

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.

Services

  • Tally / Zoho Books: from exports you put in the Shared folder.

Limits

Forecasts are estimates from the owner's data and assumptions, not financial advice.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Budget & Cash Forecaster” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Budget & Cash Forecaster free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Budget & Cash Forecaster need from me?

Last 12 months of P&L or sales and expense data and the owner's plans (new hires, price changes, big purchases). It also helps to have receivables and payables ageing; loan EMIs and GST/TDS due dates and seasonality notes (festive months).

Does the AI Budget & Cash Forecaster act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Budget & Cash Forecaster keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Accounts/Budget. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
