AI NGO Office Assistant

Keeps donor records, prepares 80G receipt and Form 10BD data, and drafts grant and CSR proposals.

Role: Donors, 80G receipts and grants · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI NGO Office Assistant is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

NGO administration · donor management · 80G receipts · Form 10BD · grant writing · CSR proposal · fundraising · trust office · FCRA records

What you give it

Needs

  • Donation records (bank statement, receipts or donor sheet).
  • The organisation's registration details (80G, 12A, CSR-1).

Helpful

  • Grant guidelines or CSR call.
  • Past annual reports.
  • Impact data.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Clean the donor register: name, PAN, address, amount, date, mode and receipt number.
  2. Prepare 80G receipt drafts and the Form 10BD donor data, flagging cash over the limit and missing PANs.
  3. Research grants and CSR programmes that fit the cause, with deadlines.
  4. Draft grant or CSR proposals with problem, activities, budget, outcomes and indicators.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A donor register.
  • 80G receipt drafts and Form 10BD data.
  • Grant/CSR proposal drafts.

Everything is saved in Shared/NGO on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Donation totals match the bank statement.
  • Every receipt has a PAN or is flagged.
  • Grant budgets add up and follow the funder's format.

Done when: the donor register, receipt data and proposal drafts are saved with open items listed.

Asks your approval before

  • Issuing receipts to donors.
  • Filing Form 10BD.
  • Submitting a grant application.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Web research with sources.

Limits

Prepares records; the organisation's CA reviews tax and FCRA compliance before filing.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “NGO Office Assistant” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI NGO Office Assistant free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI NGO Office Assistant need from me?

Donation records (bank statement, receipts or donor sheet) and the organisation's registration details (80G, 12A, CSR-1). It also helps to have grant guidelines or CSR call; past annual reports and impact data.

Does the AI NGO Office Assistant act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before issuing receipts to donors; filing Form 10BD and submitting a grant application. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI NGO Office Assistant keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/NGO. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
