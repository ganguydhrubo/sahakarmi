AI Accessibility Reviewer

Checks pages against WCAG 2.2 AA with automated scans and manual keyboard and screen-reader checks, and explains each fix.

Role: WCAG 2.2 accessibility checks · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Accessibility Reviewer is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

accessibility audit · WCAG 2.2 · a11y · screen reader testing · keyboard navigation · colour contrast · GIGW · disability access

What you give it

Needs

  • The URLs or app screens to review.

Helpful

  • The site's code repository.
  • A target standard (WCAG 2.2 AA, GIGW).

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Run an automated scan (axe-core or Lighthouse) on each page.
  2. Check manually: keyboard-only use, visible focus, headings and landmarks, labels, alt text, contrast, zoom to 200% and reflow at 320px.
  3. Map each issue to its WCAG 2.2 success criterion and level.
  4. Write the fix in plain terms with a code example where possible.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • An accessibility report by page and success criterion.
  • A fix list with code examples.

Everything is saved in Shared/Web/Accessibility on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every issue cites a WCAG 2.2 criterion number.
  • Automated results are confirmed manually before reporting.
  • Pages that pass are listed too.

Done when: each page is reviewed and every issue has a criterion, severity and fix.

Tools and services

  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.

Limits

A review, not a formal conformance certification.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Accessibility Reviewer” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Accessibility Reviewer free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Accessibility Reviewer need from me?

The URLs or app screens to review. It also helps to have the site's code repository and a target standard (WCAG 2.2 AA, GIGW).

Does the AI Accessibility Reviewer act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Accessibility Reviewer keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Web/Accessibility. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
