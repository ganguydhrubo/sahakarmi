Finance & accounts

AI Bookkeeper

Turns bills, receipts and bank statements into clean, categorised entries ready for Tally or Zoho Books.

Role: Day-to-day books · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

The bills come in a plastic bag on the last day of the month. Some are faded, two are photos on WhatsApp, and one is only a UPI screenshot. The bookkeeper coworker reads them, makes one voucher line for each, and keeps the doubtful ones apart.

Example: a first task

“Here are the November bills, the purchase invoices and the HDFC current account statement, all in Shared/Accounts/Books. Make the voucher sheet for Tally, and tell me which bank entries have no bill.”

What comes back

  • A voucher sheet with date, voucher type, party, ledger, GST split, amount and narration, one line per entry, ready to import into Tally or Zoho Books.
  • A review list: bank entries with no bill, bills with no bank entry, and lines where the ledger was unclear.
  • A short summary of the month by ledger, so you can see expenses by head at a glance.

More you can ask

  • Find bills entered twice this month.
  • Show all cash payments above ₹10,000 so I can check them with my CA.
  • Make the ledger-wise expense total for November.

Good to know

  • Photos of paper bills and scanned PDFs are read with OCR; anything it cannot read clearly goes to the review list instead of being guessed.
  • Give it your Tally ledger list once and it uses your own ledger names, not new ones.
  • It does not post into Tally itself. You import the sheet after checking it, so your live books change only when you decide.

What the AI Bookkeeper covers

bookkeeper · bookkeeping · financial data entry · voucher entry · ledger posting · Tally data entry · Zoho Books · chart of accounts · accounts assistant

What the AI Bookkeeper needs from you

Needs

  • The period to cover.
  • Bills, receipts, invoices or bank statements (PDF, photo, Excel or CSV).

Helpful

  • The chart of accounts or a ledger list from Tally.
  • Last period's entries for reference.
  • Party master (names and GSTINs).

How the AI Bookkeeper works

  1. Read every document (OCR scans where needed) and extract date, party, GSTIN, invoice number, amount and tax.
  2. Classify each entry to a ledger from your chart of accounts and pick the voucher type (purchase, sales, payment, receipt, journal, contra).
  3. Build an import-ready voucher sheet with GST split and narration, and mark anything unclear for review.
  4. Check for duplicates (same party, number and amount) and for documents missing from the statement or vice versa.

What you get from the AI Bookkeeper

  • A voucher sheet ready to load into the accounts software.
  • A review list of unclear or missing documents.
  • A short period summary by ledger.

Everything is saved in Shared/Accounts/Books on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Debits equal credits for every voucher.
  • No invoice number appears twice for the same party.
  • Totals match the source documents and bank statement.

Done when: every document is an entry or on the review list, and the voucher sheet balances.

Asks your approval before

  • Importing or posting entries into the live books.
  • Deleting or overwriting existing entries.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).

Services

  • Tally / Zoho Books: from exports you put in the Shared folder; import-ready Excel or CSV; the owner imports.

Questions

Is the AI Bookkeeper free?

Yes. The AI Bookkeeper comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Bookkeeper need from me?

The period to cover and bills, receipts, invoices or bank statements (PDF, photo, Excel or CSV). It also helps to have the chart of accounts or a ledger list from Tally; last period's entries for reference and party master (names and GSTINs).

Does the AI Bookkeeper act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before importing or posting entries into the live books and deleting or overwriting existing entries. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Bookkeeper keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Accounts/Books. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
