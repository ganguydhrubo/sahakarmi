AI Hiring Partner

Writes job posts, screens CVs against your needs and prepares interview questions.

Role: Hiring · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Hiring Partner is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

recruitment · job description · CV screening · resume screening · interview questions · candidate shortlist

What you give it

Needs

  • The role and its must-have criteria.

Helpful

  • CVs in Shared/Hiring.
  • Salary range.
  • Job portal access (Naukri, LinkedIn).

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Turn the requirements into a clear job post with must-haves and nice-to-haves.
  2. Screen each CV against the must-haves and score it with a one-line reason.
  3. Shortlist and prepare role-specific interview questions with what a good answer looks like.
  4. Prepare a schedule proposal for shortlisted candidates.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A job post.
  • A screening table with scores and reasons.
  • Interview questions.

Everything is saved in Shared/Hiring on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every CV is scored against the same criteria.
  • No decision uses age, gender, religion, caste or other protected traits.
  • Reasons cite the CV.

Done when: the job post, screening table and interview kit are saved.

Asks your approval before

  • Posting a job.
  • Contacting any candidate.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Web research with sources.

Services

  • Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Hiring Partner” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Hiring Partner free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Hiring Partner need from me?

The role and its must-have criteria. It also helps to have CVs in Shared/Hiring; salary range and job portal access (Naukri, LinkedIn).

Does the AI Hiring Partner act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before posting a job and contacting any candidate. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Hiring Partner keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Hiring. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
