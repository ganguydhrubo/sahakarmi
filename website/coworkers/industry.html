Coworkers

AI coworkers for industry specialists

Specialists for particular trades and sectors, built around the documents and steps those businesses use. 10 ready-made coworkers, free in Sahkarmi for Windows.

  • AI Clinic Front Office

    Runs a clinic's front-office paperwork: appointment lists, reminders, bills, receipts and insurance document checklists.

  • AI Real Estate Assistant

    Writes property listings, follows up leads, schedules site visits and keeps a RERA and property document checklist per deal.

  • AI Logistics Coordinator

    Tracks shipments across couriers and transporters, prepares dispatch and e-way bill details, and flags delays.

  • AI Manufacturing Planner

    Plans production from orders, maintains bills of materials, works out material needs and keeps quality and batch records.

  • AI Construction Site Office

    Prepares BOQs and estimates, compiles daily site reports, tracks material and labour, and drafts running bills.

  • AI Hotel & Homestay Desk

    Keeps OTA listings accurate, tracks bookings across channels, prepares guest records and drafts review replies.

  • AI Consulting Proposal Office

    Assembles proposals, statements of work and client deliverables from the firm's templates and past work.

  • AI School & Coaching Office

    Handles a school or coaching institute's office work: admission enquiries, fee records and reminders, timetables and parent notices.

  • AI NGO Office Assistant

    Keeps donor records, prepares 80G receipt and Form 10BD data, and drafts grant and CSR proposals.

  • AI CA Practice Assistant

    Runs a CA or tax practitioner's office work: client document collection, due-date tracking, working-file setup and status reports.

Questions

Are these industry specialists coworkers free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

Can several coworkers work together?

Yes. Each coworker has its own chat, folder, browser and task queue, and coworkers can hand work to each other. A team run splits one goal into tasks for several coworkers.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
