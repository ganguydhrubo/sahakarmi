Finance & accounts

AI Procurement Documentation

Compares supplier quotations, drafts purchase orders and keeps a clean purchase file for each buy.

Role: Quotes, POs and purchase records · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put what is being bought (item, specification, quantity, needed-by date), and supplier quotations or a list of suppliers to request from in Shared/Accounts/Procurement, then ask in plain words for a comparative statement; a draft purchase order and a purchase file checklist (quote, PO, GRN, invoice). Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Procurement Documentation covers

procurement · purchase executive · purchase order · quotation comparison · comparative statement · vendor onboarding · GRN · supplier KYC · IndiaMART sourcing

What the AI Procurement Documentation needs from you

Needs

  • What is being bought (item, specification, quantity, needed-by date).
  • Supplier quotations or a list of suppliers to request from.

Helpful

  • Approved vendor list.
  • The PO template and terms.
  • Budget for the purchase.

How the AI Procurement Documentation works

  1. Collect quotations and normalise them: unit price, GST, freight, payment terms, delivery time and warranty.
  2. Build a comparative statement with landed cost per unit and a recommended supplier with reasons.
  3. Check the chosen supplier's GSTIN status and that required KYC (PAN, bank details, MSME certificate) is on file.
  4. Draft the PO in your template and set up the purchase file to track GRN and invoice.

What you get from the AI Procurement Documentation

  • A comparative statement.
  • A draft purchase order.
  • A purchase file checklist (quote, PO, GRN, invoice).

Everything is saved in Shared/Accounts/Procurement on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Landed cost includes tax and freight for every quote.
  • PO quantity, rate and terms match the chosen quote.
  • Supplier GSTIN checked and noted.

Done when: the comparison and draft PO are saved and the purchase file lists what is still pending.

Asks your approval before

  • Sending enquiries or the PO to any supplier.
  • Committing to any purchase.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Web research with sources.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.

Services

  • IndiaMART / TradeIndia: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; finding suppliers; enquiries need approval.

Add the AI Procurement Documentation

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Procurement Documentation” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Procurement Documentation free?

Yes. The AI Procurement Documentation comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Procurement Documentation need from me?

What is being bought (item, specification, quantity, needed-by date) and supplier quotations or a list of suppliers to request from. It also helps to have approved vendor list; the PO template and terms and budget for the purchase.

Does the AI Procurement Documentation act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before sending enquiries or the PO to any supplier and committing to any purchase. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Procurement Documentation keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Accounts/Procurement. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
