Coworkers

AI coworkers for personal productivity

Inbox, calendar, notes, reminders and personal admin, so routine computer work takes less of your day. 10 ready-made coworkers, free in Sahkarmi for Windows.

  • AI Executive Assistant

    Runs the owner's day: inbox, calendar, follow-ups and errands, handing specialist work to other coworkers.

  • AI Calendar Planner

    Finds meeting times, blocks focus time and keeps the week's calendar realistic.

  • AI Email Organizer

    Sorts the inbox into what needs you, what can wait and what to archive, and drafts the replies.

  • AI Travel Researcher

    Researches trains, flights and hotels and builds a ready itinerary for business trips.

  • AI Expense Documentation

    Collects bills and receipts, extracts the details and prepares expense and reimbursement sheets.

  • AI Personal Task Planner

    Turns a messy to-do list into a realistic daily and weekly plan, and replans when things slip.

  • AI File Organizer

    Cleans up messy folders: consistent names, a sensible structure and duplicates found.

  • AI Document Formatter

    Turns rough drafts into clean, consistent documents on your letterhead, in Word and PDF.

  • AI Meeting Preparation

    Prepares a one-page brief before each meeting: who, why, history, agenda and what to ask.

  • AI Follow-up Reminder

    Remembers what you promised and what others owe you, and reminds you before it slips.

Questions

Are these personal productivity coworkers free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

Can several coworkers work together?

Yes. Each coworker has its own chat, folder, browser and task queue, and coworkers can hand work to each other. A team run splits one goal into tasks for several coworkers.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
