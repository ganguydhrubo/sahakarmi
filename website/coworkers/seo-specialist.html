AI SEO Specialist

Finds the keywords your customers search, maps them to pages and rewrites titles, headings and content to rank.

Role: Keyword research and on-page SEO · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

A homestay has a nice website that nobody finds, because the page says “Welcome” and the guest types “homestay near Mukteshwar with parking”. This coworker finds the words people really type and gives you the new title and text for each page.

Example: a first task

“Our homestay website is open in the browser. We want guests from Delhi and Lucknow. Find what they type, match it to our five pages, and give me the new title, description and headings.”

What comes back

  • A keyword map: what people type, what they want and which page should answer.
  • New titles, descriptions, headings and text for each page, ready to paste.
  • A short list of what to do first.

A keyword map (made-up sample)

More you can ask

  • Which two of my pages are fighting for the same words?
  • Rewrite titles and descriptions for my five main pages.
  • Make a list of facts to add to my Google Business Profile.

Good to know

  • It includes Hindi and “near me” searches when your customers use them.
  • Every volume or rank figure carries its tool and date; without a tool it gives no number.
  • It asks before changing a live page or your Google Business Profile. No one can promise a rank.

What the AI SEO Specialist covers

SEO · search engine optimisation · keyword research · on-page SEO · local SEO · Google Business Profile · meta titles · content optimisation · Google Search Console

What the AI SEO Specialist needs from you

Needs

  • The website and the products, services and cities you serve.

Helpful

  • Google Search Console access.
  • Competitor websites.
  • Pages you most want to rank.

How the AI SEO Specialist works

  1. Build a keyword list from Search Console queries, Google suggestions, People Also Ask and competitors, including Hindi and 'near me' terms.
  2. Group keywords by intent and map each group to one existing or new page.
  3. Audit each mapped page: title, meta description, H1/H2s, content depth, internal links, local details.
  4. Write the improved titles, metas, headings and content outlines, and a priority list.

What you get from the AI SEO Specialist

  • A keyword map (keyword, intent, page).
  • On-page fixes per page with new copy.
  • A prioritised SEO action list.

Everything is saved in Shared/Marketing/SEO on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • No two pages target the same keyword group.
  • Titles under about 60 characters and metas under about 155.
  • Volume or rank figures cite their tool and date.

Done when: every priority page has a target keyword and ready-to-paste fixes.

Asks your approval before

  • Changing live website pages.
  • Editing the Google Business Profile.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Web research with sources.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.

Services

  • Google Search Console, Google Business Profile: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.
  • Google Keyword Planner: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in; needs a Google Ads account.

Add the AI SEO Specialist

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “SEO Specialist” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI SEO Specialist free?

Yes. The AI SEO Specialist comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI SEO Specialist need from me?

The website and the products, services and cities you serve. It also helps to have Google Search Console access; competitor websites and pages you most want to rank.

Does the AI SEO Specialist act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before changing live website pages and editing the Google Business Profile. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI SEO Specialist keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Marketing/SEO. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
