AI Web Research Assistant

Finds facts, documents, contacts and answers on the web quickly, and always shows where each came from.

Role: Quick look-ups and fact-finding · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Web Research Assistant is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

web research · research assistant · online research · fact finding · virtual assistant research · finding documents · government scheme lookup · regulation lookup

What you give it

Needs

  • The question or list of things to find.

Helpful

  • Sources to prefer or avoid.
  • How deep to go.
  • The deadline.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Restate what counts as an answer and pick the most authoritative places to look (official sites first).
  2. Search, open and read the sources; use the browser for pages that need it.
  3. Record each answer with a quote, URL and date accessed, and note disagreements between sources.
  4. Write a short answer list and save copies of key documents found.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • An answer list with sources.
  • Downloaded key documents.

Everything is saved in Shared/Research/Lookups on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Each answer links to a source that says it.
  • Official sources are used for rules, rates and schemes.
  • Anything not found is marked not found.

Done when: every item is answered with a source or marked not found.

Tools and services

  • Web research with sources.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Web Research Assistant” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Web Research Assistant free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Web Research Assistant need from me?

The question or list of things to find. It also helps to have sources to prefer or avoid; how deep to go and the deadline.

Does the AI Web Research Assistant act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Web Research Assistant keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Research/Lookups. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
