Coworkers

AI coworkers for marketing & growth

Social posts, SEO, campaigns, ads reports and competitor tracking, drafted and checked before anything is published. 14 ready-made coworkers, free in Sahkarmi for Windows.

  • AI Social Media Writer

    Plans a weekly content calendar and writes posts for Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn.

  • AI Social Media Manager

    Publishes approved posts on your own pages, answers comments and DMs from your FAQs, and reports what worked.

  • AI Content Strategist

    Plans what to publish across blog, social, email and video for a quarter, tied to your customers and goals.

  • AI SEO Specialist

    Finds the keywords your customers search, maps them to pages and rewrites titles, headings and content to rank.

  • AI Technical SEO Analyst

    Crawls your site for broken links, slow pages, indexing and schema problems, and writes the fix list for your developer.

  • AI Paid Ads Specialist

    Plans and builds Google and Meta ad campaigns: keywords or audiences, ad copy, budgets and tracking, ready for your approval.

  • AI Ads Performance Analyst

    Reports ad spend, leads and cost per result each week and lists exactly what to pause, fix or scale.

  • AI Email & WhatsApp Campaigns

    Writes newsletters and automated sequences, segments the list and sends only to people who opted in.

  • AI Community & Reviews Manager

    Looks after your WhatsApp community, groups and Google reviews: replies, moderation, and asking happy customers for reviews.

  • AI Influencer Researcher

    Finds Instagram and YouTube creators who fit your brand and city, checks their real engagement, and drafts outreach.

  • AI Brand & Competitor Researcher

    Compares how you and your competitors talk, price, advertise and get reviewed, and suggests how to stand apart.

  • AI Marketing Copywriter

    Writes persuasive copy for ads, websites, brochures, banners and product pages in English, Hindi or Hinglish.

  • AI Landing Page Optimizer

    Reviews a landing page for clarity, speed and trust, rewrites it and plans A/B tests to lift enquiries.

  • AI Marketing Analytics

    Joins GA4, ad, social and CRM numbers into one monthly view of which channels bring leads and sales.

Questions

Are these marketing & growth coworkers free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

Can several coworkers work together?

Yes. Each coworker has its own chat, folder, browser and task queue, and coworkers can hand work to each other. A team run splits one goal into tasks for several coworkers.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
