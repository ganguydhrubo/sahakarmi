AI E-commerce SEO

Improves how your store and products show up on Google and marketplace search.

Role: Store search visibility · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI E-commerce SEO is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

ecommerce SEO · store SEO · keyword research · meta titles · collection pages · product schema · marketplace search ranking

What you give it

Needs

  • The store URL or product list.

Helpful

  • Google Search Console access.
  • Store admin access.
  • Competitor stores.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Research keywords buyers use, including Hindi and Hinglish terms, and map them to product and collection pages.
  2. Audit pages: titles, meta descriptions, headings, image alt text, duplicate or thin content, broken links.
  3. Check product structured data and page speed basics with a crawl script.
  4. Write fixes page by page and track rankings or Search Console clicks over time.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A keyword-to-page map.
  • An SEO audit with fixes per page.
  • Rewritten titles and meta descriptions.

Everything is saved in Shared/Ecommerce/SEO on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Titles and metas within length limits.
  • Every fix names its URL.
  • No keyword stuffing or claims beyond the product.

Done when: the audit and keyword map are saved and every priority page has a fix.

Asks your approval before

  • Publishing changes to the live store.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Web research with sources.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).

Services

  • Google Search Console: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.
  • Shopify, WooCommerce admin: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “E-commerce SEO” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI E-commerce SEO free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI E-commerce SEO need from me?

The store URL or product list. It also helps to have Google Search Console access; store admin access and competitor stores.

Does the AI E-commerce SEO act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before publishing changes to the live store. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI E-commerce SEO keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Ecommerce/SEO. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.
