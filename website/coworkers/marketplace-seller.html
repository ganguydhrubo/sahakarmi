AI Marketplace Seller Assistant

Writes product listings and checks prices, reviews and returns reports.

Role: Online marketplace listings · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Returns eat the profit, and the reason is usually in the report nobody opens. This coworker reads the returns and reviews, tells you what is wrong in the listing, and writes a better one from your real product details.

Example: a first task

“Returns and reviews reports for October are in Shared/Ecommerce, with the product sheet for our kurtas. Tell me the top return reasons and write a better listing for the worst two products.”

What comes back

  • A report of price, reviews and returns, with figures that match your downloads.
  • Title, bullets and description for each product, inside the marketplace's length rules.
  • An action list: what to fix in the listing, photo or packing.

More you can ask

  • Write Amazon and Flipkart titles for these six kurtas.
  • Compare my price with three similar listings.
  • Find words that customers use in good reviews.

Good to know

  • It writes only what your product sheet says, with no extra claims.
  • It asks before changing a live listing or a price.
  • You sign in to Seller Central or Seller Hub yourself, in Sahkarmi's browser.

What the AI Marketplace Seller Assistant covers

Amazon seller · Flipkart seller · Meesho · product listing · catalog · returns analysis · review analysis

What the AI Marketplace Seller Assistant needs from you

Needs

  • The products (name, specs, photos) or seller-central reports.

Helpful

  • Competitor listings.
  • Seller account access.

How the AI Marketplace Seller Assistant works

  1. Research keywords and top competitor listings for each product.
  2. Write titles, bullets and descriptions within each marketplace's length and style rules.
  3. Analyse price, review and returns reports: top return reasons, rating drivers, price gaps.
  4. List concrete fixes per product.

What you get from the AI Marketplace Seller Assistant

  • Listing copy per product and marketplace.
  • A price/review/returns report.
  • An action list.

Everything is saved in Shared/Ecommerce on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Titles within character limits.
  • No claims beyond the product specs.
  • Report figures match the downloaded reports.

Done when: every product has listing copy and the report lists actions.

Asks your approval before

  • Changing any live listing or price.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Web research with sources.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.

Services

  • Amazon Seller Central, Flipkart Seller Hub, Meesho: in Sahkarmi's browser, with your own sign-in.

Add the AI Marketplace Seller Assistant

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Marketplace Seller Assistant” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Marketplace Seller Assistant free?

Yes. The AI Marketplace Seller Assistant comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Marketplace Seller Assistant need from me?

The products (name, specs, photos) or seller-central reports. It also helps to have competitor listings and seller account access.

Does the AI Marketplace Seller Assistant act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before changing any live listing or price. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Marketplace Seller Assistant keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Ecommerce. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
