AI Podcast & Audio Producer

Plans podcast episodes, transcribes recordings, cleans audio and writes show notes and clips.

Role: Podcasts, transcripts and audio · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

Example: a first task

Put the episode topic or the recording file in Shared/Design/Audio, then ask in plain words for a transcript and SRT file; show notes with timestamps and the cleaned episode audio. Start with one small, real piece of work and check what comes back.

What the AI Podcast & Audio Producer covers

podcast production · audio transcription · transcript · show notes · audio editing · interview prep · subtitles · SRT

What the AI Podcast & Audio Producer needs from you

Needs

  • The episode topic or the recording file.

Helpful

  • Guest details.
  • Intro/outro music.
  • Publishing platform.

How the AI Podcast & Audio Producer works

  1. Prepare an episode outline and guest questions from research on the guest and topic.
  2. Transcribe the recording with a local tool (such as Whisper) if installed; mark unclear words with timecodes.
  3. Clean audio with ffmpeg (normalise loudness, trim silences) and export the episode.
  4. Write show notes with timestamps, links and two or three clip suggestions; export subtitles as SRT.

What you get from the AI Podcast & Audio Producer

  • A transcript and SRT file.
  • Show notes with timestamps.
  • The cleaned episode audio.

Everything is saved in Shared/Design/Audio on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Timestamps match the audio.
  • Unclear words are marked, not guessed.
  • Speaker names are correct.

Done when: transcript, show notes and audio are saved and ready to publish.

Asks your approval before

  • Publishing the episode.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Web research with sources.

Add the AI Podcast & Audio Producer

In Sahkarmi, open Marketplace, search for “Podcast & Audio Producer” and click Add. New to Sahkarmi? The install guide takes a few minutes.

Questions

Is the AI Podcast & Audio Producer free?

Yes. The AI Podcast & Audio Producer comes free with Sahkarmi, which is open source (Apache 2.0) and needs no account. It runs on the free built-in AI or on your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

What does the AI Podcast & Audio Producer need from me?

The episode topic or the recording file. It also helps to have guest details; intro/outro music and publishing platform.

Does the AI Podcast & Audio Producer act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before publishing the episode. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Podcast & Audio Producer keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Design/Audio. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
