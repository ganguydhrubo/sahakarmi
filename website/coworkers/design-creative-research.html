AI Creative Research

Finds creative references, trends and competitor campaigns and organises them into mood boards with sources.

Role: Trends, references and mood boards · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Creative Research is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

creative research · mood board · trend research · competitor ads · inspiration · campaign ideas · Meta Ad Library

What you give it

Needs

  • The brief or campaign goal.

Helpful

  • Competitors.
  • Audience.
  • Platforms.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Search competitor campaigns (including the Meta Ad Library), design galleries and recent trends.
  2. Collect references with screenshot, link and date, grouped by idea.
  3. Note what each reference does well and how it fits the brief.
  4. Propose three creative directions with references.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • A mood board document with linked references.
  • Three creative directions.

Everything is saved in Shared/Design/Research/Creative on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • Every reference has a working link and date.
  • References are inspiration, not copies to reuse.
  • Directions match the brief's audience.

Done when: the mood board and directions are saved.

Tools and services

  • Web research with sources.
  • Sahkarmi's built-in browser, signed in by you.
  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Word, PDF and Markdown documents.
  • Image briefs.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Creative Research” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Creative Research free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Creative Research need from me?

The brief or campaign goal. It also helps to have competitors; audience and platforms.

Does the AI Creative Research act without asking me?

Its work stays in files on your computer. Anything that posts, sends, submits or pays waits for your approval as a card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Creative Research keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Design/Research/Creative. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
