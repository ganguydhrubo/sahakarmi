AI Digital Asset Organizer

Sorts, renames, de-duplicates and catalogues photos, videos and design files so the team can find them.

Role: Organising design and media files · Ready to use with Sahkarmi's built-in tools.

AI Digital Asset Organizer is one of 175 ready-made coworkers in Sahkarmi, a free desktop app that gives you a team of AI coworkers on your own Windows PC. How well a task goes depends on the AI model and the sites or files involved; start with a small task and check the result.

What it covers

digital asset management · DAM · file organisation · photo library · media library · file naming · duplicate files

What you give it

Needs

  • The folder(s) of assets.

Helpful

  • Preferred naming convention.
  • Product or campaign list.

How it works

It restates the goal in one line, looks in the chat and the Shared folder for what it needs before asking you, and lists its plan. Then:

  1. Scan the folders and list files with type, size, dimensions, date and duplicates (by hash).
  2. Propose a folder structure and naming convention and show a before/after preview.
  3. After approval, move and rename files and write a catalogue sheet with tags.
  4. Keep a move log so every change can be undone.

Finally it runs its checks and reports what was done, the evidence and what is still open.

What you get

  • An asset catalogue sheet.
  • A reorganised folder.
  • A move log for undo.

Everything is saved in Shared/Design/Assets on your computer.

Checks before it reports done

  • File count before equals file count after plus removed duplicates.
  • No file is deleted, only moved.
  • The move log can restore the original layout.

Done when: assets are organised, catalogued and the undo log is saved.

Asks your approval before

  • Moving or renaming files.
  • Deleting duplicates.

Each request appears as an approval card in Sahkarmi (and in Telegram, if you connect it).

Tools and services

  • Files in its folder and Shared.
  • Code and commands on your PC (per your permissions).
  • Spreadsheets (CSV, Excel, Sheets exports).
  • Scheduled runs.

Add it in four steps

  1. Download and install Sahkarmi for Windows (free, no account). The install guide covers every step.
  2. Pick an AI: the free built-in models, or connect Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini in Settings, AI and models.
  3. Open Marketplace, search for “Digital Asset Organizer” and click Add.
  4. Give it one small task in plain words, in English, Hindi or Bengali, and approve or reject each card.

Questions

Is the AI Digital Asset Organizer free?

Yes. Sahkarmi and all its ready-made coworkers are free and open source (Apache 2.0), with no account or subscription. Coworkers run on the free built-in AI models, or on a Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini account you connect; paid plans are billed by those providers, not by Sahkarmi.

What does the AI Digital Asset Organizer need from me?

The folder(s) of assets. It also helps to have preferred naming convention and product or campaign list.

Does the AI Digital Asset Organizer act without asking me?

It is set up to ask for your approval before moving or renaming files and deleting duplicates. Each request shows as an approval card you accept or reject.

Where does the AI Digital Asset Organizer keep my files?

On your own computer, in Shared/Design/Assets. Sahkarmi has no cloud account; your files go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of the task.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
