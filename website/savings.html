          How the numbers are worked out

          With your current numbers, for the option shown in the task table, typical case.

            Assumptions and limits
            • Time with AI = AI minutes + your review minutes, plus the share of runs you redo by hand at full manual time. If that is not faster than doing it by hand, no saving is counted.
            • Cautious uses 1.5 times the AI and review time and 1.5 times the redo share. Best case uses 0.75 times the time and half the redo share.
            • Free plans have usage limits that vendors do not publish as fixed numbers, so they are counted for only part of your runs (your setting, 50% to start). Paid plans also have limits; heavy use may still hit them.
            • A plan only counts for the kind of work its vendor's pricing page lists: chat, acting in a browser, or coding. Nothing here promises a tool will do your task correctly. Check what it produces.
            • Setup time (writing prompts, testing, learning the tool) is counted once, in the first year, at your hourly value.
            • Value of time is not cash. Unless saved hours replace paid work, the money figure is productivity. The plan price is real money either way.
            • Plans you already pay for are included in every option at no extra cost, so nothing is counted twice.
            • Best value is the option with the highest typical net benefit, but a cheaper option within 5% of it is picked instead.
            • Taxes: rupee prices are shown as listed on each vendor's India page, which do not say whether GST is included. US dollar prices can have 18% GST added (on by default).
