HR on autopilot: attendance, leave and salaries

An HR team of AI coworkers checks attendance, tracks leave, works out PF, ESI and salaries, and onboards new joiners, with your approval.

Published 12 November 2026 · English

What is said in this film

A team of forty-two. And HR? Just one person. That's you.

Attendance, leave, salaries, new joiners, interviews, and people leaving. Every month, the same work, the same Excel.

This is Sahkarmi. An HR team of AI coworkers, on your Windows PC.

First, attendance. Hand over the biometric file. Late arrivals and unapproved absences stand out on their own.

And leave: who has how many days left, one list for everyone.

Then salaries. PF, ESI, overtime and deductions worked out, and a payslip for every employee. Paying them? Only you do that.

New joiner? A checklist for PAN, Aadhaar and bank details. What's in, what's missing, at a glance.

For anything missing, a WhatsApp reminder is ready. It asks you before sending.

Interviews? It matches the candidate's free time with the interviewer's, and the invite is ready.

And when someone leaves, the relieving letter and the full and final settlement too.

Aadhaar and bank details stay on your PC, in the HR folder. There's no Sahkarmi server. And Sahkarmi is free.

Search Sahkarmi on the Microsoft Store. It's free. And send this to a friend who handles HR.
