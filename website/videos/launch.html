Meet Sahkarmi: an AI team that asks before it pays

Asha, the accounts coworker, matches bills against the bank statement on your PC and stops to ask you before any money moves.

Published 11 November 2026 · English

What is said in this film

Your AI accountant is asking: should I pay eighteen thousand five hundred rupees?

Every business owner has two jobs. The real work. And the desk work: bills, payments, taxes, waiting all day.

This is Sahkarmi. A team of AI coworkers that actually works on your Windows PC.

Ask Asha, your accounts coworker, to match October's bills against the bank statement. She reads the files, checks every line...

and before anything that moves money, she stops and asks you.

Your PC stays on at the office. You don't. One tap on Telegram: approve, or reject.

Customer replies. Festival posts. Tax prep. At every step that matters, you decide.

Free AI is built in, with no account. There's no Sahkarmi server. Your files stay on your PC.

Search Sahkarmi on the Microsoft Store. It's free. And send this to a friend who runs a business.
