Gmail and WhatsApp inbox, sorted by AI coworkers

Neha reads your whole Gmail inbox and WhatsApp Web, sorts what is urgent and drafts the replies. You sign in yourself and approve what goes out.

Published 11 November 2026 · English

What is said in this film

Nine in the morning. Two hundred and fourteen emails, sixty-three WhatsApps. And you haven't even had your tea.

Important emails get buried. Customers keep waiting for a reply. And there's never time to send that offer.

This is Sahkarmi. AI coworkers on your Windows PC that run your Gmail and WhatsApp Web in the browser side panel.

You sign in yourself: your password in Gmail, a QR scan in WhatsApp. No API, nothing to buy.

Neha, your email coworker, reads the whole inbox and sorts it: urgent, payments, customers, newsletters. Most urgent on top.

She drafts a reply to every important email. Before anything is sent, you take one look.

Then WhatsApp Web, in another tab of the same browser. A customer asks a question, the answer comes from your FAQ, and she asks you before sending.

Sending a Diwali offer? Tara cleans the list: only customers who said yes. She writes the message, and sends it only after you approve.

Sahkarmi is free, with free AI built in. There's no Sahkarmi server. Your Gmail and WhatsApp, with your own logins.

Search Sahkarmi on the Microsoft Store. It's free. And send this to a friend who runs a business.
