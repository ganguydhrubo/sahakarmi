What is Sahkarmi

An AI team that works on your own computer

Sahkarmi (सहकर्मी, “coworker”) is a free desktop app for small businesses and their owners. You get a team of AI coworkers. Each one has its own workspace, its own browser, its own memory and its own task queue.

Give them work in plain English, हिन्दी or বাংলা. They reply in your language and understand INR, lakh and crore, GST, IST and DD/MM/YYYY dates. You stay in control of anything that spends money or talks to customers.

How it works

Ask. They work. You approve.

  1. Give a task

    Message a coworker, attach files or schedule it to repeat: once, hourly, daily, weekdays or weekly.

  2. They do the work

    Coworkers read files, browse in their own browser tab and pass work to each other. Every step is shown as it happens.

  3. You stay in charge

    Before a payment, a customer message, a post or a form submission, they file a request in your Approvals inbox. For logins and OTPs, you take over the browser.

Inside the app

Built for real business work

  • Own browser per coworker

    A built-in browser with a Take over button. While you are in control, coworkers pause.

  • Approvals inbox

    Approve or reject with a note. The decision goes back to the coworker as a message.

  • Task queue

    See what is queued, running and done. Pause, resume, cancel or stop.

  • Shared files

    One folder the whole team can read and write, next to each coworker’s own folder.

  • Schedules

    Daily reports, weekly follow-ups or a one-off reminder at a set time.

  • Three languages

    The whole app in English, हिन्दी and বাংলা, with light and dark themes.

AI providers

Free AI built in. Use your own sign-ins if you have them.

Sahkarmi finds the AI tools installed on your computer and reuses their existing login. It never copies your tokens.

Privacy and safety

Local-first, and honest about it

  • Your coworkers, chats, files and settings are stored on your computer. There is no Sahkarmi cloud account.

  • When a coworker thinks, the text it needs is sent to the AI provider you chose (OpenCode, Gemini, Claude or ChatGPT) under that provider’s terms.

  • Sahkarmi is a development preview. Coworkers can use files, commands and the network on your PC. Run tasks you trust and keep backups.

  • The code is open source under the Apache License 2.0, so anyone can check what it does.

Get started

Up and running in a few minutes

  1. Download

    Get the Windows installer (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit) from the download button or GitHub Releases.

  2. Install

    The installer is not code-signed yet. If Windows SmartScreen warns you, choose More info → Run anyway.

  3. Say namaste

    Open Sahkarmi, pick a coworker and give the first task. The free built-in AI works right away.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Sahkarmi free?

Yes. The app is free and open source, and it includes free AI models through the bundled OpenCode, with no login or card. If you connect a paid plan such as ChatGPT or Claude, that provider bills you directly.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) is the main build. macOS (Apple silicon) and Linux (AppImage) builds are also published on GitHub Releases.

Can a coworker pay, send or post without me?

Coworkers are instructed to stop and file an approval request before payments, messages to customers or vendors, publishing and form submissions, and you approve or reject each one. In the current preview this relies on the AI following those instructions, so watch new tasks closely and never share card details or OTPs with a coworker.

Where is my data stored?

On your computer. Sahkarmi has no cloud account of its own. The text a coworker works on is sent to the AI provider you chose, under that provider’s privacy terms.

Do I need ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini?

No. The free built-in AI works without any account. If you already use Codex (ChatGPT), Claude Code or Gemini CLI on the same computer, Sahkarmi can use those logins too.

Which languages does it support?

The app interface is in English, Hindi and Bengali, and coworkers reply in the language you write in.

Who makes Sahkarmi?

Sahkarmi is built in India. It started from the open-source OpenBot v0.1.11 by Norbert Bodziony (Apache License 2.0) and is developed in the open on GitHub.
