What Sahkarmi Does

Built for real Indian businesses

One desktop app for a team of AI coworkers that work on your own PC, with the AI plans you already have.

  • Coworkers work as a team

    Each coworker has its own name, memory, instructions, and workspace. Coworkers send each other messages, hand off tasks, and share files in the team folder.

  • Use the AI plan you have

    Connect ChatGPT Plus (Codex), Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Grok, or run 100% free with bundled OpenCode or local Ollama. Zero middleman markup.

  • Stored on your computer

    Workspaces, conversations, files, and accounting documents stay on your machine. Zero cloud storage, zero tracking, total confidentiality for your financial data.

  • Change the provider, keep the coworker

    Switch between OpenAI, Claude, DeepSeek, and Google Gemini with one click. Your coworkers keep their entire memory, workspace history, and instructions intact.

  • A built-in isolated browser

    Each coworker can browse supplier directories, GST portals, and WhatsApp Web with an isolated session. Take over with 1 click whenever you need to enter OTP or CAPTCHA.

  • Queue the next task

    Send work while a coworker is busy. Background task queues and mailbox routines keep working even while you attend client calls or step away from your desk.

Safety & Governance

They ask before they pay, send, or post.

Sahkarmi is designed for business owners who want AI assistance without risking unauthorized financial transactions or unapproved customer messages.

  • Zero Unauthorized Payments

    Coworkers cannot initiate bank transfers, UPI transactions, or card payments. Every transaction stops for your explicit sign-off in the Approvals inbox.

  • Message Review Before Sending

    Replies to sensitive customer complaints or vendor pricing negotiations require your one-click approval before being posted to WhatsApp or email.

  • 1-Click Browser Takeover

    Whenever a bank login, OTP, or CAPTCHA appears, hit Take Over. The coworker pauses while you log in securely; your passwords are never shared.

Answers

Frequently asked questions

Is Sahkarmi free?
Yes. The app is free and open source, and it includes free AI models through the bundled OpenCode, with no login or card. If you connect a paid plan such as ChatGPT or Claude, that provider bills you directly.
Which computers does it run on?
Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) is the main build. macOS (Apple silicon) and Linux (AppImage) builds are also published on GitHub Releases.
Can a coworker pay, send or post without me?
Coworkers are instructed to stop and file an approval request before payments, messages to customers or vendors, publishing and form submissions, and you approve or reject each one. In the current preview this relies on the AI following those instructions, so watch new tasks closely and never share card details or OTPs with a coworker.
Where is my data stored?
On your computer. Sahkarmi has no cloud account of its own. The text a coworker works on is sent to the AI provider you chose, under that provider’s privacy terms.
Do I need ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini?
No. The free built-in AI works without any account. If you already use Codex (ChatGPT), Claude Code or Gemini CLI on the same computer, Sahkarmi can use those logins too.
Which languages does it support?
The app interface is in English, Hindi and Bengali, and coworkers reply in the language you write in.
Who makes Sahkarmi?
Sahkarmi is built in India by the Sahkarmi Team (Apache License 2.0) and is developed in the open on GitHub.
