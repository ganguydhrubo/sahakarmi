What Sahkarmi is

Not another chat window. A team that gets work done, with you in charge.

Sahkarmi (सहकर्मी, “coworker”) gives you named coworkers, each with a role, a folder, a memory and a queue of tasks. You give them work in plain language. They read files, browse the web in their own tab, hand work to each other, and stop to ask you before anything that spends money or talks to a customer.

  • Who it is for

    People who already pay for an AI

    If you use Claude Code, Codex (ChatGPT) or Google Antigravity, Sahkarmi reuses that sign-in and gives it a workspace, a browser, schedules and approvals.

  • Who it is for

    Small businesses and solo operators

    The default team covers accounts, sales, support, research, content, code, operations and security. Rename them, give them your own instructions, add more.

  • Who it is for

    Anyone who wants AI without a cloud account

    The app works on first launch with free models and no login. Your chats and files never leave your PC except to the AI provider you choose.

How it works

Ask. They work. You approve.

  1. 1

    Pick a coworker and give a task

    Message Asha, Vikram or any coworker in English, Hindi or Bengali. Attach files, or switch on Run on schedule for hourly, daily or weekly work.

  2. 2

    They work where you can see it

    Every step shows in the thread: files read, commands run, pages opened in the coworker's own browser tab. Pause, cancel or stop at any time.

  3. 3

    You decide the risky bits

    Before a payment, a customer message, a post or a form, the coworker files a request in Approvals and waits. For logins and OTPs you press Take over and type them yourself.

Features

Everything you get. Free.

Every feature here is in the app you download today.

  • Nine coworkers to start

    Mitra (orchestrator), Asha (accounts), Vikram (sales), Meera (support), Arjun (research), Kavya (content), Dev (code), Tara (ops) and Kabir (security). Rename, edit or delete any of them, with an 8-second undo.

  • A browser per coworker

    A built-in Chromium tab with a Take over button. While you are in control, the coworker's browser tools pause; hand back when you are done.

  • Approvals inbox

    Approve or reject with a note. Your decision goes back to the coworker as a message, so nothing is blocked while you are away.

  • Task queue, schedules and routines

    See what is queued, running and done. Run a task once, hourly, daily, on weekdays or weekly, and keep routines you reuse.

  • Shared files and memory

    One Shared folder the whole team can read and write, each coworker's own folder, and a memory page you can inspect.

  • Search everything

    Ctrl+K finds coworkers and any message in the workspace and jumps straight to it. Ctrl+1 to 9 open coworkers, Ctrl+N makes one.

  • Free AI built in, your sign-ins reused

    The bundled OpenCode runtime brings free models with no login. Claude Code, Codex (ChatGPT) and Google Antigravity connect with your existing accounts; tokens are never copied.

  • Backup model

    If a paid provider answers nothing or hits its quota, the coworker moves to the free model, tells you why, and sends your message again.

  • Your language, your size

    Interface in English, Hindi and Bengali, with nine more Indian languages partly translated. Light and dark themes, three interface sizes, Enter or Ctrl+Enter to send.

Privacy, security and permissions

Your PC. Your data. Your rules.

  • No account, no analytics

    Coworkers, chats, files, usage history and the activity log are stored on your computer under your user profile. Sahkarmi has no cloud service and sends no analytics; the only outbound traffic of its own is the update check against GitHub Releases.

  • What goes to an AI provider

    When a coworker thinks, the text and files it needs are sent to the provider you chose for that coworker (OpenCode's free models, Claude, ChatGPT or Google) under that provider's terms. Nothing goes to Sahkarmi.

  • What coworkers can do on your PC

    Coworkers can read and write files, run commands, use the network and drive their browser tab. The Permissions page in Settings sets how much they may do on their own.

  • Approvals are instructions, not a wall

    Coworkers are told to stop before payments, customer messages, publishing and form submissions. Today that depends on the model following instructions. Never share card details or OTPs with a coworker; type them yourself with Take over.

  • Open source, verifiable builds

    Apache License 2.0. Every release is built by GitHub Actions from a tagged commit, with SHA-256 checksums, an SBOM and a build provenance attestation attached.

  • Report a vulnerability privately

    Use GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on the repository (Security, then Report a vulnerability). The security policy explains what to include and the current security model.

This website notes each click on a Download button (installer version, browser, operating system, languages and time zone) so the team can see how many people download Sahkarmi. It uses no cookies and stores no IP address, and it sends nothing when your browser asks not to be tracked.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Sahkarmi free?

Yes. The app is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0. It includes free AI models through the bundled OpenCode runtime, with no login and no card. Those free models have usage limits set by OpenCode. If you connect a paid plan such as ChatGPT, Claude or Google Antigravity, that provider bills you directly; Sahkarmi charges nothing.

Which computers does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). That is the build the project releases and tests. macOS and Linux builds are paused for now and will be published once they have been tested on real hardware; watch the GitHub repository for the announcement.

Can a coworker pay, send or post without me?

Coworkers are instructed to stop and file an approval request before payments, messages to customers or vendors, publishing and form submissions, and you approve or reject each one in the Approvals inbox. In the current preview this relies on the AI following those instructions, not on a hard technical block, so watch new tasks closely and never share card details or OTPs with a coworker.

Where is my data stored, and does Sahkarmi collect anything?

Coworkers, chats, files and settings stay on your computer. Sahkarmi has no cloud account and sends no analytics. The text a coworker works on is sent to the AI provider you chose, under that provider's terms, and the installed app checks GitHub Releases for updates.

Do I need ChatGPT, Claude or Google Antigravity?

No. The free built-in AI works without any account. If you already use Codex (ChatGPT) or Claude Code on the same computer, Sahkarmi reuses those sign-ins and never copies your tokens. Google Antigravity connects with one Log in with Google or an API key.

Which languages does it support?

The interface is available in English, Hindi and Bengali, plus nine more Indian languages that are partly translated and fall back to English where a line is missing. Coworkers reply in the language you write in.

Is it safe to install an unsigned app?

The Windows installer is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen shows a warning; choose More info, then Run anyway. Every release publishes SHA-256 checksums and a build provenance attestation so you can verify the file you downloaded was built from the public source by GitHub Actions.

Who makes Sahkarmi, and how do I report a problem?

Sahkarmi is built in India by the Sahkarmi Team and developed in the open on GitHub. Report bugs as GitHub issues with the app version and steps to reproduce, and report security problems privately through the repository's security policy.
