A free AI agent for Mac, on Apple Silicon and Intel

Sahkarmi for Mac is a desktop AI agent app for macOS. Install it on your MacBook, iMac or Mac mini from a DMG file or with Homebrew, pick a free or paid AI, and give its AI coworkers tasks that run on your Mac: in their own browsers and in your files. It is free and open source, and available for Mac from version 0.37.0.

What it does on a Mac

A chatbot answers questions; you still do the work. An AI agent takes the steps itself. Ask a Sahkarmi coworker to compare three suppliers' prices and it opens their sites in its own browser, collects the prices into a sheet in your folder, and tells you what it found and what it could not check.

  • AI coworkers for Mac

    A team of coworkers, each with its own job, chat, folder and task list. They hand work to each other, and one goal can be split between several of them.

  • Has its own browser

    Each coworker browses in its own profile and stays signed in to its own accounts, separate from Safari, Chrome and the other coworkers.

  • Works in folders

    Each coworker has a working folder, and the team shares one Shared folder. Results are plain files you can open and check.

  • Runs on a schedule

    Routines run hourly, daily, on weekdays or weekly while Sahkarmi is open.

  • Asks first

    Posting, sending, submitting and paying wait for your approval.

  • Uses the AI you choose

    Free AI models are built in. You can also connect your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini plan.

Which Mac it runs on

  • macOS 12 (Monterey) or later.
  • Apple Silicon: M1, M2, M3 and M4 Macs.
  • Intel Macs that run macOS 12 or later.
  • The DMG file is about 300 MB, and the installed app takes about 870 MB. Coworkers need an internet connection while they think.

Not sure which Mac you have? Open the Apple menu, then About This Mac. "Chip Apple M…" means Apple Silicon. "Processor … Intel" means Intel.

Download Sahkarmi for Mac

One download, free, no account. Pick the file for the chip in your Mac:

Apple Silicon (M1 to M4) 300 MB Intel Mac 300 MB

Then open the DMG file and drag Sahkarmi into the Applications folder. The install guide has every step, and shows how to verify the checksum.

Install with Homebrew

If you use Homebrew, one brew install line installs Sahkarmi on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs. The app opens straight away, with no first-open steps.

brew install --cask ganguydhrubo/sahkarmi/sahkarmi

Update later with brew upgrade --cask sahkarmi. Remove it with brew uninstall --cask sahkarmi.

The first open, after a DMG download

The Mac app is not notarised by Apple yet. So the first time you open it, macOS shows "Verifying Sahkarmi…" for some seconds and then a box that says Apple could not verify Sahkarmi. Do this one time:

  1. In that box click Done, not Move to Trash.
  2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down to Security, click Open Anyway next to the line about Sahkarmi, and confirm with your Mac password or Touch ID.
  3. Open Sahkarmi again and choose Open.

After that Sahkarmi opens like any other app. Homebrew installs skip all of this.

Updates

The app tells you when a new version is out and opens the download. Open the new DMG file and drag Sahkarmi into Applications again, in place of the old one. Your coworkers, chats and settings are kept. With Homebrew, run brew upgrade --cask sahkarmi.

What works on Mac

  • Control of desktop apps. Coworkers can see the screen, read an app's controls, click, type and use Command shortcuts in your Mac apps. macOS asks you once to allow Sahkarmi under Accessibility and Screen Recording (System Settings > Privacy & Security); Settings > Permissions in the app has a button for each.
  • Voice typing and read-aloud on the computer. The same free voice models as on Windows run on your Mac, Apple Silicon and Intel.
  • Install MCP servers for me. After you approve the card, Sahkarmi installs a server into its own folder and fetches uv or Node.js for your Mac with checked SHA-256 sums.
  • Not on Mac: the Tally connector, and the Microsoft Store, winget and Scoop, which are Windows ways to install. On Mac, use the DMG file or Homebrew.

You stay in control

  • Sending, posting, submitting and paying always wait for an approval card.
  • Your coworkers, chats and files stay on this Mac. Sahkarmi has no cloud account of its own.
  • How well a task goes depends on the site and the AI model, so start with a small task and check the result.
  • To uninstall, drag Sahkarmi from Applications to the Bin, or run brew uninstall --cask sahkarmi. Your data stays in ~/Library/Application Support/Sahkarmi until you delete that folder.

Questions

Is there a free AI agent for Mac?

Yes. Sahkarmi for Mac is free and open source (Apache 2.0) and runs on macOS 12 (Monterey) or later. It includes free AI models, so you can use it without a paid AI subscription.

Does Sahkarmi work on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs?

Yes, both. There is one DMG file for Apple Silicon Macs (M1, M2, M3, M4) and one for Intel Macs. The Homebrew command is the same for both.

How do I install Sahkarmi on a Mac with Homebrew?

Open Terminal and run: brew install --cask ganguydhrubo/sahkarmi/sahkarmi. The app opens straight away. To update later, run: brew upgrade --cask sahkarmi. To remove it, run: brew uninstall --cask sahkarmi.

Why does macOS say it could not verify Sahkarmi?

Because the Mac app is not notarised by Apple yet. On the first open, click Done, not Move to Trash. Then open System Settings, Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security, click Open Anyway and confirm with your Mac password or Touch ID. Open Sahkarmi again and choose Open. This is needed one time only, and Homebrew installs skip it.

Can Sahkarmi control the apps on my Mac?

Yes, from version 0.37.0. Coworkers can see the screen, read an app's buttons and fields, click, type and use shortcuts in Mac apps such as TextEdit, Numbers, Excel or WhatsApp. macOS asks you once to allow Sahkarmi under Accessibility and Screen Recording (System Settings > Privacy & Security); Settings > Permissions in the app has a button for each. Sending, posting, paying and deleting still wait for your approval, and coworkers never type into password fields.

Do my files leave my Mac?

Your files stay in folders on your computer. Sahkarmi has no cloud account of its own; the parts of a file a task needs go only to the AI provider you choose, as part of that task.
