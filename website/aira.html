What is Aira?

Aira is a free, open-source AI agent for Android phones. Say what you need, and it opens your apps, taps, types and browses the web on a hidden background screen until the job is done.

01 · The idea

How does Aira work without taking over my screen?

Aira opens apps, taps, types and scrolls on a separate background screen while you use your phone as usual. Tap the small live view whenever you want to watch.

Background screen · Aira Open the clock app Set alarm 9:40 Set alarm 10:00 Report back
Your screen · you
  1. You say itType or hold to talk. Use plain words, the way you would ask a person.
  2. Aira does itIt looks at the screen and finds buttons the way a person does. No app APIs and no per-app integration.
  3. You stay in chargeKeep talking to add or correct things, or stop it. After a stop it reports where it got to, and "continue" picks up from there.

03 · Abilities

What can Aira do?

Eight things Aira does on your phone. Anything that needs a login, a verification code or a payment is handed back to you.

  • 01

    Operate apps

    Reads long lists and chat histories, taps and types, and shares text into apps.

  • 02

    Browse the web

    A built-in desktop-class browser that keeps you signed in, reads pages, downloads and uploads.

  • 03

    Help with your current screen

    Double-tap the floating capsule to send the screen you are looking at. Ask "how should I reply?" or say "note this down".

  • 04

    Schedules and conditions

    "Remind me at 9 tomorrow." "Do this every Friday." It runs on time, in the original conversation.

  • 05

    Jump in or stop anytime

    Keep talking while a task runs to add or correct things. You can stop it and continue later.

  • 06

    Hold to talk

    Hold the chat input to speak, and edit the words before sending. Offline voice packs can be downloaded inside the app.

  • 07

    Files and computing

    A Linux sandbox for each conversation runs Python, Node and shell on the files you send (arm64 phones).

  • 08

    Use it from your computer

    AI tools on your computer can hand Aira tasks through CLI + Skill or MCP.

04 · Set-up

How do I get started with Aira?

Three steps. The third one is needed only if you want Aira to operate your phone apps.

  1. 1

    Install the APK

    Tap Download Aira.apk on your Android phone and open the file to install it. All versions are on GitHub Releases. For automatic updates, first install Obtainium, then tap "Get auto-updates with Obtainium" above.

  2. 2

    Connect a model

    In Settings › Model, pick a provider and enter an API key, or sign in with an account. Aira works with OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, xAI, Groq, Mistral, OpenRouter, or local Ollama and LM Studio, and more.

  3. 3

    Set up phone control once

    Follow the "Set up phone control" wizard in Settings and pair wireless debugging one time. Skip this if you only want chat, web and files.

07 · Honest limits

What does Aira not do?

An agent on your phone should be clear about where it stops.

  • It does not log in or pay for you. Logins, verification codes and payments are handed back to you.
  • It cannot read screens that block screenshots. On payment and password pages it asks you to take over.
  • It has no AI of its own in the cloud. You connect a model: your own key, an account, or a model on your own computer.
  • It is for Android only. There is no iPhone version.
  • It does not need root. Phone control uses wireless debugging, which Android may turn off when you change Wi-Fi or restart.
  • It does not replace your judgment. Using apps and websites automatically may break their terms of service. Check important results.

08 · FAQ

Questions about Aira

Short answers. For more, read the README on GitHub.

Is Aira free?

Yes. Aira is free and open source under the Apache-2.0 licence. You pay only your own AI model provider, if any.

Does Aira send my data to its own server?

No. There is no Aira server. What you say and what is on your screen go only to the model provider you choose.

Which phones does Aira work on?

Android phones. Chat, web and files need Android 11 or later. Operating phone apps needs Android 12 or later, and Android 14 or later is recommended.

Does Aira need root?

No. Phone control is set up once with wireless debugging, using the wizard in Settings.

Is Aira safe with payments and logins?

Aira hands logins, verification codes and payments back to you. In Ask mode, the default, it also checks with you before spending money.

How do I get updates for Aira?

Use Obtainium for automatic updates, download the newest APK from GitHub Releases, or use About, Check for updates inside the app.
