Free alternatives to cloud computer-use agents

If you are looking for a free alternative to ChatGPT agent, Claude computer use or Manus, it helps to know what kind of tool you need. This page compares well-known computer-use agents by what they are, where they run, licence and cost, and says honestly where Sahkarmi fits and where it does not.

Which one fits

  • You already pay for ChatGPT or Claude

    Their own agents are the simplest start. Sahkarmi can also use those same subscriptions, through the Codex and Claude CLIs, when you want several coworkers, each with its own browser, routines and folders, or work in Windows desktop apps.

  • You want to pay nothing

    Sahkarmi runs on free built-in models with no account. Free models have usage limits and are less capable than the best paid ones, so expect simpler tasks to go better.

  • You are a developer building an agent

    A library such as Browser Use, or a framework such as UFO, gives you building blocks. Sahkarmi is a finished app, not a library.

  • You want a sandboxed computer

    Container-based agents keep the agent on its own desktop. Sahkarmi works on your real PC, which is more useful and less isolated; its permissions and approvals are how you limit it.

  • You are on macOS or Linux

    Sahkarmi is Windows-only for now. Cloud agents and cross-platform open-source projects are the better choice today.

  • You care about the licence

    Sahkarmi is Apache 2.0, so it can be used, changed and shared, including at work. Check each project's own licence file before you depend on it.

What is different about Sahkarmi

  • A team, not one agent. Several coworkers work side by side, each with its own chat, folder, browser profile and task queue, and can hand work to each other.
  • Your logins stay on your PC. Each coworker's browser keeps its own sign-ins locally, separate from the others.
  • Browser and desktop. The same coworker can work on websites and, with Control this PC, in Windows apps. How computer use works.
  • Approvals you can see. Posting, sending, submitting and paying wait for you, in the app or on Telegram.
  • Any of several AIs. Free built-in models, or Claude, ChatGPT (Codex), Gemini (Antigravity) and other providers, chosen per coworker.

And the honest limits: Windows only, a development preview, no benchmark results of its own, and results that depend on the model you connect.

Questions

What is a free alternative to ChatGPT agent?

Sahkarmi is a free, open-source desktop app for Windows whose coworkers browse websites, use desktop apps and work with files, using free built-in models or an AI plan you already have. Open-source projects such as Browser Use, UI-TARS Desktop and Bytebot are other free options, mostly aimed at developers.

Is there a free alternative to Claude computer use on Windows?

Sahkarmi runs on Windows 10 and 11 and can operate desktop apps and websites. It can use your Claude plan through the Claude CLI, or free built-in models if you have no plan.

Is Sahkarmi better than these tools?

It is different, not universally better. It suits people on Windows who want a free, local app with several coworkers, persistent browser logins and approvals. Cloud agents need no install and work on any operating system.

Sahkarmi is free and open source (Apache 2.0). No account, no card. Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.
